About this archive

What was saved, how it is organized, and how to rebuild it.

This is a personal, offline backup of one subject cluster on metallicman.com: the writing about The Domain, the EBP communications with its Commander, extraterrestrial species, MAJestic, the MWI and world-lines, intention and prayer campaigns, reincarnation, heaven and the prison planet. The rest of the blog (news, China, food, politics, fiction) was left out on purpose.

What is here

  • 431 articles, 2,891,054 words, by Metallicman (333), Daegonmagus (33) and PissedLizard (3).
  • 26 collections: the author's own subject index pages, kept in his order, plus one generated collection for related posts no index links to.
  • 9529 images and 21 PDFs saved locally next to the text, so nothing depends on the source site staying online.
  • 33 "(Repost)" / "(copy of)" duplicates folded into their originals; 44 links on the index pages could not be resolved (dead URLs or off-topic pages) and are shown greyed out.
  • Password-protected parts 13, 14 and 15 of the EBP communications are included: the author published that password on The Domain index page. Part 12 is Patreon-only with a different password and is not archived.
  • 30 pages recovered from the Wayback Machine: the Daegonmagus series (parts 1 to 32) and a few others were deleted from the site but still linked from its index pages; they are marked with a notice and a link to the snapshot.
  • ΨORM: the author's 2026 GitHub repository (the Ψ-Ontic Reality Model seed document, 33 sections, bridges, glossary) is archived as a collection; its 202 "Early PDFs" (950 MB) are listed with links and included in the release zip.
  • Patreon: every post on the author's Patreon is indexed (title, date, type, thumbnail, link); public videos link directly, member-only bodies can be merged in with tools/import_patreon.py using your own login.
  • Reality, explained: 40 illustrated summary parts (92,668 words) written by an AI (Claude) from the archived articles, in reading order, each ending with the list of sources it restates. They carry a sparkle icon everywhere so they are never mistaken for the author's writing.

How to use it

  • Everything works from the local files, no server needed. Open index.html.
  • Press ⌘K (or /) to search titles, headings and tags. Tick full text to search inside every article (loads about 17 MB once).
  • Each collection keeps the author's reading order; use Previous / Next inside an article to read a collection straight through.
  • Every article links back to its original URL. Images open in a lightbox.

How it was built

Scripts live in tools/: wp_index.py lists the whole site through the WordPress REST API, crawl.py follows the curated index pages and pulls every article (with a title-keyword pass for stragglers), strips site boilerplate, downloads images and rewrites links to local copies, and build.py renders these pages from data/articles.json and data/indexes.json. Verbatim article bodies are kept in raw/body/. Re-run python3 tools/crawl.py && python3 tools/build.py to refresh.
