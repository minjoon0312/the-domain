The Domain

Metallicman 20 September 2021 3 min read · 711 words Original ↗

On 17SEP21 I posted an article that related the fact that The Domain opened up a dedicated channel to me via the EBP. As always, it was one-sided, and detailed. But during the conversation, I had no real mental ability. I was in a receiving and reporting state. I was really unable to think for myself. I just queried what I was told to ask and recorded the answers.

You can read this article HERE, if you are confused with what is going on. In fact, I urge you all to read it if you haven’t already.

This quickly became a recruitment drive for volunteers to act as “irregulars” for The Domain. And in very short order, those that added anything suggestive of helping The Domain suddenly found themselves experiencing “sleep paralysis”. And what was going on was that The Domain was performing medical operations on their bodies so that they could indeed perform services with The Domain.

This Index is a list of all such related articles and events.
