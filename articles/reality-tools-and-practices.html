Tools and practices: lucid dreaming, Hemi-Sync, remote viewing, fate

Beyond affirmations, the archive describes a small toolkit for working with the model from inside a body: lucid dreaming (the guest writers' specialty), the Monroe Institute's Hemi-Sync, remote viewing, fate forecasting, "channels", and the plain health advice the Commander keeps repeating.

AI summary · part 13 of 40 7 min read · 1,472 words 28 sources ↓

Written by an AI, not by Metallicman. This is a plain-language summary of 28 archived articles (mainly the collections Daegonmagus, Gateway Techniques (Hemi-Sync & FFR), Fate Forecasting, Past Life Regression Index, PissedLizard). It restates what those sources say and does not add claims of its own. Every part links to its sources at the bottom so you can check the original wording.

In one breath

The suit seals the exits, but not perfectly. Sleep is the crack in the wall, so lucid dreaming is the archive's main practical skill: guest writer Daegonmagus teaches it as a discipline and uses it for reconnaissance. Hemi-Sync audio (from the Monroe Institute) is MM's recommended way to loosen the body's grip. Remote viewing works but shows probabilities, not fixed futures. Fate forecasting (Chinese BaZi) reads the template you were born with. And underneath all of it, the Commander's advice is dull on purpose: sleep, whole food, exercise, calm, good company.

The toolkit at a glance

Lucid dreaming, the Daegonmagus way

Daegonmagus (DM) came to the blog in 2021 as a reader with decades of practice: sleep paralysis from the age of eight, then a slow discovery that the paralysis was a doorway. His articles became the archive's lucid-dreaming course, "the framework to allow others to tap into the power that lucid dreaming can offer", which he says he was tasked to write. He is dismissive of hobbyist "reality checks" and treats the practice as training for reconnaissance and contact.

  1. Lesson 1: the void space and three meditations. The "lucid void space" is the dark, formless state between waking and dreaming that you learn to enter and hold. It is reached through three trained skills: visualisation (holding an image steady), silence of thought (letting the inner voice stop), and stillness of the body (lying without any movement until the body "falls asleep" while you stay awake).
  2. Lesson 3: techniques for inducing an effective LD. Wake-induced entry, timing, and what to do in the first seconds so the dream does not collapse.
  3. Lesson 4: advanced practices. Deliberate targets, contact with non-physical entities ("replace words such as black mirror and crystal ball with words such as void space and sleep paralysis"), remote viewing from inside the dream state.
  4. The simplified technique (2023). A short wake-induced method DM devised after "trying to devise a simpler method": go back to bed after a few hours' sleep, hold stillness and silence, and let the paralysis come.

What he does with it: about seven years of "LD espionage into amnesia compounds" with his wife SD, written up as a briefing for the Commander (holding facilities, a "portal theatre", a consciousness-doping facility, eight-level "consciousness mazes"); an escape from a simulated world into what he took to be a control hub; a remote-viewing session where a Grey waved at him from an asteroid. The Commander's comment on his LD network: it is coordinated with Domain "sappers", and the tracers it leaves are how The Domain finds Old Empire traps.

How the Commander frames dreams

Dreams are "unsorted imagery": thought paths of the non-physical bodies, physical memories, self-set signposts, messages from friends, and sometimes malevolent noise. Rules he gave: keep a dream journal for three successive nights and read the trends, never the literal content; a recurring theme over about three nights means real non-physical activity; a dog, a pack of animals or a team in the dream means you were working with support; for volunteers, "the stranger your dreams, the more changes" have been made to your non-physical body. Contact with The Domain, when it comes, arrives as feelings, urges and thought streams, "not words"; "listen with the heart". The main obstacle is impatience: "pulling up seedlings to help them grow".

Hemi-Sync and the Gateway technique

MM's second recommendation after affirmations is the Monroe Institute's Hemi-Sync audio: binaural beats that "isolate your consciousness for independent travel through space and time". He hosts several full programs (Journey Home, Going Home, Radiance, Positively Ageless) and reprints the declassified 1983 CIA assessment of the Gateway process, which he summarises as sceptical about fidelity (out-of-body travellers "never got the numbers exactly right") but supportive of the physics. In the model, the audio does one job: it nudges consciousness from particle toward wave while the body stays put, which is the same door lucid dreaming opens by other means. Bruce Moen's Monroe-style explorations are also reprinted: the "Reentry Station" account in the Entry director article is where the diving-suit image of the constriction comes from (part 3).

Remote viewing

  • It works, on a moving target. MM's article on remote viewing in the MWI: a viewer sees the highest-probability template as it stands, and templates change. His example is the Deagel population forecast, which the Commander later called "a remote-viewing snapshot of a changing template", not a fate.
  • What to watch for. Viewers colour results with their assumptions ("if you think the question is about the Iran hostage situation, you will interpret the imagery in those terms"), the same failure MM admits in his own EBP transcriptions.
  • Examples in the archive. The CIA's 1984 viewing of Mars a million years ago; a viewing of the tunnel of light; DM's session targeting the amnesia mechanism; Farsight's 2023 "Greys" series, which the Commander called sincere but aimed at preconceptions.
  • MM's own attempts "were pretty much unsuccessful", and he says so.

Fate forecasting

MM had a Chinese BaZi (Four Pillars) forecast done by a Singapore group in 2003 and found it "uncanny how accurate it can be". In his reading, the birth moment fixes the starting attributes of the body you were issued, "similar to that of a role playing game", and periodic influences ("stars") make some years easy and some hard. He recommends paying a practitioner for a day-to-day forecast rather than learning the system yourself, then using the forecast to time efforts and to know which parts of your template are hills. In the Commander's vocabulary this is fate: the gravitational favour or disfavour on the body, distinct from destiny, the template (part 4).

Past-life regression and "channels"

MM's own regression, around 1999 or 2000, is told as a story: "many issues, secrets and events were uncovered in my past lives", the details kept private. The Commander later said past-life memory is accessible "with Mantid approval and purposeful effort" (add it to your affirmations), but warned one reader that memory recovery was impossible in his case and would be blocked by the people around him. "Channels" is MM's word for tuning in to streams of thought-related quanta: intuition, gut feeling, the neck that prickles when someone talks about you; everyone has the antenna, most have let it atrophy; the implants use channels but are not needed to have them.

The boring advice

Asked repeatedly for guidance, the Commander gives the same list, and MM adds his own. Commander, 2023 to 2024

bodyGet lean, build muscle, sleep

Body fat under about 20 percent for men and 25 for women; strength training; real sleep; whole food, fruit daily, no processed food, little sugar; walk.

mindCalm, and no news

"Calm life." Stop reading news of every flavour. Control fear: it is "noise" and it builds what you fear.

peopleChoose your circle

"Your largest influence in your life are those that you surround yourself with. Family. Then friends." If they are not upbeat, leave them alone.

actionParticipate, add value

"You are not a machine cog." Work, contribute, "generate happiness", volunteer, be known in your community.

practiceMM's four

"Affirmation campaigns. Fate forecast reviews. Hemi-sync activities. Rufus behaviors." A Rufus is someone who quietly helps.

not recommendedPsychedelics, suppression

Psychedelics can "remove a veil" and some DMT encounters are real, but they alter reasoning and are not a path. Suppressing emotion to be "efficient" misses the point of being human.

Remember

  • Sleep is the crack in the wall; lucid dreaming is the trained way through it (void space, three meditations).
  • Hemi-Sync loosens the body's grip; remote viewing reads probabilities; fate forecasting maps the terrain you were issued.
  • Read dreams by trend over three nights, not literally; contact comes as feeling, not words.
  • The foundation is unglamorous: body, calm, company, contribution.
