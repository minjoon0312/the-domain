Daegonmagus: the lucid-dream reconnaissance

The deep dive on part 13. The 32-part series by MM's most prolific contributor, an Australian electronics technician who says he has been leaving his body since childhood: the Elder Guardians and their 20,000 souls, the "slave gods" and their PWM codes, the Unseen 5, seven years of infiltrating "consciousness programming facilities" with his wife SD, the eight-level mazes, the Test Rig, the Portal Theatre, the craft he says he flew, and the lucid-dreaming lessons he wrote so readers could follow.

Written by an AI, not by Metallicman. This is a plain-language summary of 31 archived articles (mainly the collections Daegonmagus). It restates what those sources say and does not add claims of its own. Every part links to its sources at the bottom so you can check the original wording.

In one breath

Daegonmagus (DM) is the reader MM most often quotes back to the Commander, and the Commander's replies about him ("a treasure trove", "something else entirely", "already being under monitoring") run through parts 23 to 25. His own series, posted on MM's site from August 2021 to December 2023, is a different document from anything MM wrote: a first-person log of lucid dreams and astral projections going back to childhood sleep paralysis, in which he says he met a council of "Elder Guardians" on 11 May 2012, was told of a "spiritual amnesia" engineered by "slave gods", was put in charge of 20,000 souls, was later initiated into a military-style astral cell called the Unseen 5, and spent years with his wife SD infiltrating facilities where consciousness is "doped", programmed and reset. In between the reports he wrote four lessons on how to do it. MM posts the series under DM's name and adds only short comments of his own ("Jeeze! Louise!", "Well done. For certain."); what follows is DM's account, in his words.

The life, in his telling

The Elder Guardians and the slave gods

The audience: "abducted" out of a lucid dream into "a more complete and real reality... so real, that I was forced to the conclusion that the physical world was the lesser of two reals". A courtyard "a few acres in size", floating in space with a moon "10 to 20 times the size of our moon" circling it, and a crowd he estimated at 20,000, all sharing "a confusion born from knowing that I knew this place, but also knowing that I had forgotten it". A "Grand Elder" waited for him to remember, then spoke, and "my mind began opening up and processing information at an incredible speed". The message: "there was a sort of spiritual amnesia affecting mankind"; "the human brain had been deliberately engineered to cut them off from this state of awareness"; the amnesia "had been created by a race of beings that did not want humans reconnecting with this power", the same ones "responsible for the Ancient Egyptian Slave trade", whom he called "the slave gods"; and it "would see the end of human life if it was left to continue unimpeded". DM's nickname among the Elders was "trick and trip", for evading the enemy and for travelling. He was told a "hive consciousness" he belonged to had tracked the amnesia to "a black hole anomaly" at the edge of the universe where the device was hidden; taken to its edge; and "put in charge of the 20,000 other souls", with the task of rewriting their "soul code" as an unlocking mechanism and, back on Earth, regathering them "as one fighting force". Projecting to the anomaly against advice "completely disintegrate[d] my consciousness... Within minutes I was reduced to an insane, incoherent mess."

The Unseen 5, and the simulation

In November 2016, on the same night he says he met "the all being / god (supreme consciousness that connects all others in existence)", he was initiated into "the Unseen 5". They are not the Elders. "Whereas the EG are a bunch of old wisened sages that are charged with the up keep of the soul origin point, the Unseen 5 is a military-like intelligence gathering organisation, first and foremost", stationed "inside of the traps in the astral / etheric planes in the orbital pathways of the astral earth, approximately half way between the earth and the moon", concealed by technology, "the SAS or special forces equivalent of the astral plane". Training took place at "time travel towers"; in February 2019 a "time reset" theme returned and he found himself "flashing in and out of two separate timelines", then in "the real world", "a place similar to The Capital in the Hunger Games" with Egyptian-style stone houses and hovering screens, where a guerrilla group the Elders belonged to broadcast messages. He asked the direct question. "I asked very specifically if we {back on Earth} were in a consciousness simulation. The Elder confirmed to me that yes, every single person on Earth was trapped in a false reality that had been constructed by the Alien factions present within the Cabal. It had been designed so that consent of these people was unwillingly being given as to mark their votes for the Cabal remaining in office. All contracts signed within the physical reality apparently equated to a vote." Sleeping bodies "are hijacked... taken to special facilities in the real world... processed and implemented with fresh programming to keep their physical life story consistent." His own caution follows: experiences where he "fell asleep without realizing it" and only later became lucid he treats as "less accurate", because "I could not completely rule out that I was being manipulated."

Seven years of infiltration: the briefing

Part 10 is written as "a briefing document to be relayed to the Domain Commander via Metallic Man outlining about 7 years worth of recon work into various locations by two very accomplished" lucid dreamers, DM and SD. Its theme: "Reality brainwashing is a very heavy theme present within many of these combined LD experiences, and is suggestive that the amnesia prison system has indeed been taken over."

Reports to the Commander

part 14Remote viewing, 17 December 2021

DM "deliberately set about targeting physical locations that had something to do with the amnesia mechanisms" and Domain HQ. MM: "Well done. For certain. There's a lot here... I did ask the Domain Commander about this." The Commander's comments on DM's intel are in part 23: "a treasure trove... has already set things in motion toward new projects"; "Psaigreen", DM's name for the operators, possibly correct; "Portal Room 7... evidence of a front door".

part 23Escape from a simulation, 26 March 2022

"I was put into a dream simulation that seemed designed to completely break my spirit", featuring SD "very troubled by her own hallucinations". His end note: "The simulation layer seemed to act as a buffer between the outer pocket universe and this physical reality", with "an AI... trying to create a scenario that would elicit a specific emotional response", the pattern both the Unseen 5's leader and the Grand Elder had warned of. It came "right after contacting my handler and asking for possible nuclear attack locations".

parts 20, 21SD's premonitions

A dream of an imminent nuclear attack "set for 28th February 2022" ("I am not here to fear monger people"), and two synchronised dreams about an assassination plan around Ukraine. Dated and, as the archive notes, unfulfilled; posted as received.

part 26, 30The craft

On 27 June 2022 he says his consciousness "merged and attached to what most people would consider an extra terrestrial space craft", launched from a "consciousness craft launch facility"; the experience was "comparable to the vividness of waking, physical reality", so he wrote a 9,000-word operational report "so it can hopefully be built upon by other explorers". His question to the Commander in part 28: is the "space port mall" and the craft "Domain owned and operated, or owned by a third party?"

part 27"The Mantids finally showed up"

"in our goddamned fucking house... in our goddamned fucking bedroom", the night after flu shots, "right before an uninstigated conversation about manifestation through intent". The Commander, a month earlier, had told MM DM's wife was an elder "from communities outside The Domain" on a de-tethering mission (part 23).

parts 7, 13, 22The engineer's projects

"Project Alpha", telepathy explained "using basic radio engineering principles" (an aura field near the head, RC oscillators tuned in tens of hertz); "Project Edison", a single frequency for communication with "the world of the dead", petitioned "under lucidity" on 29 November 2017; "Operation Rainbird", a clandestine radio network for a grid-down emergency.

The lessons

The occult chapters, and the theology

DM writes as a practitioner: a chapter of the Hell Fire Club he founded ("Dragon Flame", with a charter that has "nothing inherently sinister within it"), an essay arguing that Aleister Crowley's Thelema had "an order" inside NASA "or was it MI6", a survey of "non physical contact through participants external to Metallicman" (Blavatsky's root races, the Watchers, Tool's lyrics, the Wandjina paintings of north-western Australia and the "Annunaki" line in Alien Interview), thoughts on not being a Freemason, and, at the end, "The Leverian Theology", "a philosophical system of categorizing thought experiments" named after "a word I once heard spoken to me during a meditation". Part 24 records a night at sixteen near an abattoir in Wooroloo, Western Australia: missing time, a cattle mutilation, a friend's broken memories, and SD's separate sighting on another night. Part 17 speculates that Australia's Dreamtime cultures, and something in the Australian realm that "fucks them (our captors) up", may be why he and SD can travel "more successfully than people in other parts of the world".

Read with care

DM's series is testimony, not doctrine, and he says so more carefully than most: he distinguishes "mystical experiences" with continuity of consciousness from ordinary lucid dreams, distrusts anything received after a gap ("I could not completely rule out that I was being manipulated"), and files SD's premonitions with a disclaimer. His vocabulary (slave gods, the Cabal, PWM codes, the Test Rig, AI simulations) is his own and does not map one-to-one onto MM's or the Commander's; the archive keeps it distinct. The Commander's assessments of him are in parts 23 to 25 and are themselves claims. The engineering projects are hypotheses he labels "by no means finalized". Nothing here is a recommendation to attempt the practices described.

Remember

  • A lifetime of sleep paralysis and projection, then an audience on 11 May 2012: amnesia, slave gods, 20,000 souls, "trick and trip".
  • Two bodies of guides: the Elders at the soul origin point; the Unseen 5, astral special forces in Earth orbit.
  • Seven years of recon with SD: the Test Rig, the Portal Theatre, the Island, the Marketplace, eight-level mazes.
  • Lessons: three meditations, a pose, a "tennis match", the void, and tennis-ball portals six degrees above the eye.
  • Reports up the chain: remote viewing, a simulation escape, a craft flown; the Commander called it "a treasure trove".
