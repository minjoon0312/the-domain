Glossary in plain words

Every special word in the archive, defined in one or two sentences, with the part of these summaries where it is explained. MM's own glossary pages are linked at the bottom; this one is shorter and plainer.

AI summary · part 16 of 17 8 min read · 1,700 words 11 sources ↓

Written by an AI, not by Metallicman. This is a plain-language summary of 11 archived articles (mainly the collections Terms and Conventions, The Domain, Intention and Prayer Campaigns). It restates what those sources say and does not add claims of its own. Every part links to its sources at the bottom so you can check the original wording.

How to use this

Terms are grouped by topic, not alphabetically, so that related words sit together. The part number in brackets is where the idea is explained. Words in italics are MM's own coinages or borrowings; words in quotes are the material's exact phrasing.

Reality and time

World-line
One frozen, complete snapshot of a universe, "a frozen 3D photograph". Reality is made of countless world-lines side by side. [2]
MWI
"Many worlds interpretation." In the archive it means the whole set of world-lines, and also the reality bubble itself ("the MWI universe"). [2]
Time
Not a flow. The experience of a consciousness moving from one world-line to the next, about four per second when alert. [2]
Slide
A step to a neighbouring world-line that differs from the one you were on. Small slides come from thought; big ones from technology. A dive is a run of slides ending somewhere else. [2, 12]
Cluster
A group of nearly identical world-lines that "feed off each other" and act like an inertial damper; also, a group of consciousnesses that travel together. [2, 7]
Bleed-over / cross talk
Influence between nearby world-lines: coincidences, Mandela-effect memories, ghosts, figures at the edge of vision. [2]
Quantum shadow
What other people are, seen from your world-line: projections of consciousnesses on adjacent lines. Not fake, not fully "here". [2]
Shadow people
Bodies with little or no consciousness in them at a given moment; "empty containers". [3]
Particle / wave
The two behaviours of a consciousness: located in a body (particle) or spread out between lines and after death (wave). [2]
Main universe / master universe
The big universe outside the bubble, where The Domain lives and where thought manifests freely. [1, 8]
Reality universe / bubble universe / pocket universe
The small spawned universe we live in, with modified rules: amnesia, a containment field, throttled thought. [8]
Frozen moment, attractor basin, consciousness vector
ΨORM's names for a world-line, a cluster, and a life-line. [14]

You

IS-BE
"Immortal Spiritual Being", from Alien Interview: the whole of you, which cannot be destroyed. [1, 3]
Quanta
The basic stuff of souls; MM also says "quantum strings". Unordered quanta plus experience becomes ordered quanta. [3]
Garbon
A cluster of quanta working as a unit, like an organ of the soul. [3]
Swale
A shared path between garbons, "a highway". [3]
Soul
A self-aware cluster of garbons; "the repository of who we are independent of our consciousness and our memories". [3]
Consciousness
A slice of a soul placed in a reality as an interface; a soul can run several at once. [3]
Sentience
The behaviour pattern of a soul type. Human types: STO (service to others), SFA (service for another), STS (service to self), DIS (disjointed or disrupted: thinks STO, acts STS). [3]
Fractional
An IS-BE split into several consciousnesses in different bodies "for the purposes of control and education". [3]
Construct
A "new" soul assembled in a Heaven from components of dismantled incorrigible souls. MM says he is one. [3]
Hybrid
MM's word for a human whose suit has been altered by MAJestic or The Domain. [3]
Channels
Tuning in to streams of thought-related quanta: intuition, gut feeling, the prickle at the neck. Everyone has the antenna. [13]

The body and the prison

Skin suit / inmate suit / meat suit
The engineered human body: a physical body plus a simplified non-physical body, built to hold a small slice of you, block memory and snag on the fence. [3]
Containment field / force screen / the fence
The boundary of the bubble. Free beings cross it; inmates' non-physical bodies are rigged not to. [8]
Amnesia / memory erasure
Loss of access to memories at every death (the memories themselves survive, outside the brain). [3, 6]
Prison complex / prison planet
The whole facility: six solar systems inside the field, Earth near the edge, a control centre in the Moon. [8]
General population (GP)
Life in a body, in the reality bubble: the "punishment" side. [7]
Old Empire
The conquerors who built the prison; "does not exist any more" as an empire; escaped leaders and machinery remain inside. [8]
Mades Escapleon
The Old Empire administrator of our solar system, who hid inside general population; the Commander says MM was built from his components. [8]
Lost Battalion
The 3,000 Domain personnel captured and recycled through the prison; their recovery is The Domain's mission. [9]
The Hole
A special holding micro-universe for very powerful prisoners, the "Goetic spirits" of old magic. [7]

Death and Heaven

Translation
The Commander's word for dying: consciousness translating out of the body into the non-physical. [6]
Exit template
Where you find yourself right after death, near your last world-line, before the welcome begins. [6]
Tunnel of light
"The primary amnesia mechanism": the passage that erases memory access on the way into Heaven. [6]
Heaven
MM's word for everything outside a world-line; more narrowly, the species-specific sub-pocket universe attached to the prison, for rest, review and planning. Prison Heavens "do not actually exist in the Master Universe". [7]
Recycling / reincarnation
The loop: life, death, welcome, Heaven, memory wipe, consent, new life. [6]
Life review, council of elders, life selection
Stages of Heaven as described in Michael Newton's books, which MM annotated as a map of the parole wing. [7]
Parole
The Commander's term for the Heaven side; also the planned conditional releases ("exit visas", "parole suits"). [7, 8]
Call for pickup
What The Domain asks at death: call to The Domain, "yell for help and pick up", do not stop, do not enter the light. Nothing happens unless you ask. [6]

Guides and species

Mantid
The multi-dimensional insect-like species assigned to every human: your guardian angel, template keeper and lesson runner. "Type-3" in MM's species list. [7]
Mantid Prime
The senior layer of Mantids, in Heaven, who write the curriculum and still follow Old Empire directives. [7]
Type-1 Greys
The small, manufactured, hive-souled species that tends the nursery and has treaties with the US. In Alien Interview, Domain officers. [11]
Type-2
A human-like species seen at facilities on Mars and Titan. [11]
Progenitors
The ancient seeders of life in this sector, thought to have had a hand in making humans. [11]
Benefactors
MM's MAJestic-era word for the extraterrestrials working with the program, mainly the Mantids and the Greys. [10, 11]
The Domain
The non-corporeal organisation that administers this sector, conquered the Old Empire and speaks through MM. [9]
Domain Commander (DC)
The officer who answers questions through the EBP. Unnamed, because Domain members do not use names. [9]
Airl
The Domain officer interviewed at Roswell in 1947 according to Alien Interview. The Commander once passed on "Airl says Hi". [9]
Oompa Loompas
MM's nickname for a diminutive allied species that works with Domain "sappers". [11]
Elder Guardians, Unseen 5
Non-physical groups described by Daegonmagus: old sages keeping "the soul origin point", and a military-style intelligence office that monitors astral travellers. [11, 13]
Nursery / sentience nursery
A solar system where a new intelligence is cultivated toward an approved soul type. Earth is one of five nearby. [11]

Steering your life

Pre-birth world-line template
The route through the world-lines agreed before birth, with fixed signposts: your fated life. [4]
Master template
"The source code for consciousness movement in our reality universe"; MM says it shifted in September 2021. [4]
Template map
The route you write for yourself by directed thought; it replaces the pre-birth one while you maintain it. [4]
Signpost / telltale / premonition
A fixed event region on your route; a felt hint of one before it arrives. [4]
Fate / destiny
Fate: the gravitational favour or disfavour on the body (BaZi). Destiny: the template. [4, 13]
Affirmation campaign / intention campaign / prayer campaign
Writing goals down and saying them aloud daily for a few months, then releasing, to steer world-lines. [5]
Dream board / intention canvas
Pictures of the wanted life, with written rules for what they mean, used alongside the affirmations. [5]
Slipstream effect
Tying your campaign to other people's so shared goals manifest faster. [5]
Rufus
MM's name for a person who quietly helps others; "being a Rufus" builds positive entanglements. [7, 13]
Karma / entanglement
Attachments made by every interaction, good or bad, that pull you back into the same situations. Offset by kindness. [7]

MAJestic and the hardware

MAJestic / MAJIC / MJ-12
The US program for extraterrestrial matters; MM's employer 1981 to 2006; now "carve-outs". [10]
W(U)-SAP
"Waived, unacknowledged special access program": the legal wrapper such programs live in. [10]
ELF probes / core kit #1 and #2
Implants controlled by extra-low-frequency radiation: kit #1 controls memory access; kit #2 allowed MM autonomous world-line travel. [10]
EBP
The extraterrestrial implant placed in MM's physical and non-physical bodies in 1981; the channel for the 2021 comm. [9, 10]
Feducial
A triangular indexing icon an agent focuses on "to center the brain" and unlock a portal. [10]
Dimensional anchor
MM's role: an implanted person whose life and thoughts were used to steady clusters of world-lines. [10]
Biological artifice / drone / drone pilot
The ET body MM was entangled with, and the operator who chose his slides. [10, 12]
Egress, portal
Leaving a world-line through a fixed installation; the Pensacola portal of 1981. [12]
Retirement
Mandatory shutdown of the probes, followed by life-long monitoring, "as sex offenders". [10]

The comm

Comm
MM's word for a session with the Commander through the EBP. [9]
Chill / hard chill
A physical confirmation MM feels when an answer is right; a "block" is the opposite. [9]
Volunteer / irregular
A reader who has verbally offered to work with The Domain and had their non-physical body altered ("a kit"). [9]
Small bads / medium bads / big bads
The Commander's scale for crises: one or two million dead, hundreds of millions, a billion or more. [8]
Deagel forecast
A published 2025 population forecast the Commander called "a remote-viewing snapshot of a changing template". [13]
ΨORM
The Ψ-Ontic Reality Model, MM's 2026 formal statement of the model under his own name. [14]
