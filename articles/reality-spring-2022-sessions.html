Parts 7 and 8 of the comm: the reform program, the fences, and the hollow Moon

The deep dive on parts 6, 7, 8 and 11. Two sessions from February and March 2022: why the Heaven curriculum is "a bastardized / hybrid program of little value", who subverted the prison and for what, why an IS-BE cannot be destroyed, how the people around you can be "fences" rather than enemies, the exact pickup procedure at death, teleporters and weapons, leadership "slices", the physics of a functionality-reduced universe, and the Moon as an ark that turned out to be hollow.

AI summary · part 26 of 40 11 min read · 2,404 words 2 sources ↓

Written by an AI, not by Metallicman. This is a plain-language summary of 2 archived articles (mainly the collections The Domain, Heaven). It restates what those sources say and does not add claims of its own. Every part links to its sources at the bottom so you can check the original wording.

In one breath

By early 2022 the questions had become personal and precise: one reader, told he had spent centuries in a Heaven "learning cluster" only to dig himself deeper, asked whether the curriculum itself was broken. The answer is the archive's fullest account of the reform program: built to reshape dangerous IS-BEs over millions of years and then release them far away, then quietly changed by the Old Empire's later leadership so that "there would never (truly and actually) be a period of parole", drifted further by the Mantid Primes, hijacked by entertainment-seekers and emotion-harvesters, and finally desynchronised by The Domain. The same two sessions explain why you cannot simply kill the bad guys (an IS-BE cut up becomes thousands), why the people who box you in may be doing so on purpose and for your good, what to shout when you die, and that the Moon was an ark, hollow inside, and now a Domain-run control room. "Arl says Hi!"

Part 7: the curriculum on trial

The follow-up, 7.6b, asked whether karma can be "infectious" and whether the wipe is used to saddle decent beings with debts they never chose. "Questioner is correct." The prison's systems "have been subverted to other uses and purposes", five of them: entities who "use the Prison Complex for entertainment purposes, and have created never-ending bouts of recurring terror... for their own personal amusement"; entities that "feed off the emotions" of hard times, so that "the human inmates are utilized as a farmed animal or creature to generate quantum alignments that can be extracted through harvesting... similar to reliving a movie scene over and over"; the Mantid Primes' new directives, which "encourage the creation of inadvertent quantum (karma) attachments to solidify group clusters"; the founding belief "that if an IS-BE entity would endure similar event sequences for millions of lifetime reincarnations that it would grow tired of that experience and never want to inflict those pains and terrors on others"; and service-to-self entities who work with their Mantid Primes to "construct lifetime experience... for their own personal amusement, growth, or pleasure".

7.1bTeleporters

"We have numerous teleportation facilities on the Earth Planetary environment." But an inmate suit "is not sustainable outside of the Prison Complex. It is a custom made envelope for this prison complex only"; outside it would suffer "a rapid period of mental, emotional melt down" and the freed IS-BE would be ejected straight back into the non-physical side of the prison. The machines move doll bodies and non-inmate suits "through the wire".

7.2aThe cold dark sea

A questioner's impression of what lies outside the bubble was "correct": a normal region of space, "an area of tides, vortexes, winds, and pressure fluctuations", not to be confused with the space outside the solar system.

7.2bSmuggled weapons

A rumour of nuclear devices moved by teleporter: "This intel is incorrect." MAJestic "cannot utilize teleportation technology without Domain overview and permissions"; The Domain reads intentions "prior to entry access" and has "easily distracted the thought patterns to other venues". A detonation in the non-physical would leave the bubble and every Heaven "destroyed", their inhabitants "in a rubbled state with a long, long time to recover".

7.5aAsia's next hundred years

"Overall positive and free of the attempts to militarize it"; new "understandings" on Korea and south-western Russia; Japan "expected to break away from the relationship with the United States". Short term: "worrisome elements" and "a strong possibility of disruptive elements" with catastrophic potential. Deagel "not without fault, and the template can change rapidly". Logged as claims.

7.7b to 7.7dVolunteers and membership

"The Domain will assist all irregulars... on an individual basis." Requirements: service-to-others sentience, acceptance of changes to the non-physical body ("the Old Empire fear-inducement mechanisms will be activated"), and obedience to Domain rules, with a review "to determine in what way they would best fit within the Domain society and social structure". Life paths are personal, set pre-birth with your cluster.

7.8aThe Old Empire today

"The Old Empire does not exist any more. It was conquered by The Domain and broken up into regional entities. All of the regional entities are under monitoring authority and watched closely."

Question 7.10: the mechanism at death

"Once the entity dies, and gets their bearings on where they are and what is going on, they must direct their thoughts to [1] calling to The Domain and [2] yelling for help and pick up. Do not stop until we arrive. Do not get distracted, and do not be led astray by any events or memories. We will NOT do anything unless explicit permission is asked. That is our firm rule. This is true even though we may have elements and assets surrounding the entity at that particular time." And from 7.9f: "This is a requirement for all entities within the Prison Complex irregardless of who they are or what their background is. This includes MM."

Part 8: why not just destroy them

8.1 Missing time
The craft a questioner's mother's co-workers saw was not Domain but the vehicle of a redacted group "that investigate nuclear systems". Her "missing time" was "similar to that of MM when he was upgraded in 1985": biological investigation and monitoring.
8.4a Implants in joints
Other groups monitor inmates "for their own purposes"; The Domain has "extradited this activity to third-party resources". The redacted group "prefer to place tracking items in the joints of the physical extremities", with little change to the non-physical body.
8.4b Past, present, future
The Domain can see an inmate's past, present and future only through an EBP, "a physical connection and non-physical modifications". Otherwise "we can only observe the state of the present IS-BE", and advise "the hills and valleys of the present templates".
8.5 Leaders
"Yes and No." MM's image: a person in about 200 transparent slices, of which "perhaps 15% to 20%" are coloured by influence, pulsing "like ocean tides", a "quantum participation dial". Putin appeared held "firmly snugly" in a big hand, smiling "cat ate the canary". "Joe Biden is not a controllable asset / entity / clay at this time... The skin-suit is compromised." Others: "occasional walk-ins and walk-throughs", with no confidence in outcomes.
8.6 De-cloaking
A reader wanted an affirmation to reveal hidden Domain members. The Commander cut in: doing so "will trigger catastrophic event sequences which will initiate immediate recycling into another skin-suit... Quantum alarms are set off when thoughts related to a member of The Domain is recognized." The Domain already knows where every battalion member is (repeated in part 5).
8.7a Thought and physics
"Thoughts can manifest physical events" in both universes, but the reality universe is "functionality reduced": "Newtonian laws operate within known physical space and is predictable", plates move, volcanoes erupt, orbits shift. Thought does not change the rules; it changes which template you sit on: "thoughts control brain selection", and "groups of people... can switch to different templates which then define different physical conditions".
8.7b and 8.7c Scars and dates
Earth's geological scars are real "remnants of the great disruptions" of the Old Empire's conquest wars; physics was never suspended. "The earth sphere is expected to enter a period of geologic upheaval in mid 2030's into the 2040's", timing "dependent upon mass thought cluster influences". A claim, dated 2022.
8.8a Dream assault teams
A consciousness "sits" in different regions of the brain; a dreamed assault team "possess a similar seating of the consciousness". 8.8b to a worried reader: "everything is on track and to plan... He is never alone."
8.9 When WWIII began
"Most future historians will attribute the start of World War III (also known as the fracturing of the global order) as 2008 with the collapse of the too big to fail banking cartel"; others will say 2014. MM adds the 2008 Bucharest NATO summit. A claim.
8.11 To a "woke" Domain member
Stay confidential; "migrate actions in careful steps so as not to arouse suspicion or opposition that could alert snares"; at the end of the life contract, call for pickup, then "follow our guidance and processing".

Read with care

The leadership answer (8.5) is MM describing pictures, and he says so: the slices, the hand, the smile are his images of what he received. The Asia forecast, the 2030s to 2040s upheaval and the 2008 dating of "World War III" are claims logged as stated. The personal readings for the "multi-segmented" questioner are heavily redacted in the original and paraphrased here. And note the consistency check the archive can make: the "call for pickup" rule is stated identically in parts 22, 25 and here, and again for Lawrence Spencer's death in part 27.

Remember

  • The reform program was real, then lost its parole, then drifted, then was hijacked; The Domain has unhooked the everyday Mantids from the Primes.
  • The prison feeds entertainers, emotion-harvesters and cluster-builders; the founding idea was that pain teaches.
  • You cannot destroy an IS-BE; hence the wheel. The Domain's resources here are limited.
  • Your fences may be friends; you cannot tell, and neither can they.
  • At death: bearings, call The Domain, yell for pickup, do not stop. "That is our firm rule."
  • The Moon was an ark, is hollow, and is the control room. "Arl says Hi!"
