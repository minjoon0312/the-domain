The extraterrestrials in this material

MM claims first-hand knowledge of a few species and second-hand knowledge of a few more. No reptilians, no star children, no Zeta home world. Here is who is who, what each one is said to want, and how the galaxy is organised around "sentience nurseries".

AI summary · part 11 of 40 7 min read · 1,458 words 27 sources ↓

Written by an AI, not by Metallicman. This is a plain-language summary of 27 archived articles (mainly the collections Extraterrestrial Species, Type 1 Grey Extraterrestrials, MAJestic Related Index). It restates what those sources say and does not add claims of its own. Every part links to its sources at the bottom so you can check the original wording.

In one breath

MM describes a galaxy "teeming with all kinds of intelligent life": well over a million species, most of them not humanoid, in a region that is "a well patrolled and policed manicured world", not an empty frontier. Around Earth the cast is short. The Type-1 Greys tend the nursery. The Mantids guide each human. A human-like Type-2 works at facilities on Mars and Titan. The Progenitors seeded life long ago. The Domain administers the sector and the Old Empire built the prison. Everything else on the internet (reptilians, Pleiadians, "star children") he calls nonsense.

Who is who

Type-1The Greys

Small, ancient, manufactured, many body forms, one hive soul. The species with treaties with the US. Gardeners, police and cullers of the nursery. In Alien Interview, The Domain's officers.

Type-2A human-like species

Seen staffing the Oxia Palus facility (an ancient smelting site on Mars repurposed for study) and a small base on Titan. Little else is said.

Type-3The Mantids

Multi-dimensional, insect-like, "angels". Each human has one. The true cultivators of human sentience. Part 7 has the details.

ancientThe Progenitors

Seeded rudimentary life across this sector perhaps a billion years ago; believed by the benefactors to have "had a hand in the creation of the human species" (from a CIA remote viewing by Joseph McMoneagle).

administratorsThe Domain

Non-corporeal, vast, disciplined. Part 9.

buildersThe Old Empire

Conquerors turned jailers; now regional remnants. Part 8.

nativesNon-physical Earth species

The faeries of the British Isles ("cousins worldwide", not part of the prison, a war was fought over its construction) and an inner-earth, deep-sea species behind "ant people" and dwarf legends. Both avoid inmates' thoughts.

allies and othersHelpers and watchers

A diminutive species MM calls "Oompa Loompas" that works with Domain sappers; Daegonmagus's "Elder Guardians" (old sages keeping the soul origin point) and the "Unseen 5" (a non-physical intelligence office that monitors every astral traveller); the "Goetic spirits" held in the Hole.

mysteryUnknowns

The Lapu-Lapu ridge miners, the movers of a modular structure, the Baltic Sea object, the beings behind the Susquehannock colony, the makers of a fuselage in Victoria Falls. Reported as puzzles, not identified.

The Type-1 Greys in detail

MM's longest species article is about the Greys, because they were the first extraterrestrials he met and the ones MAJestic dealt with. He warns that "there is a lot of disinformation regarding this species" and that his own knowledge is limited to what he was shown. His main points:

  1. They are manufactured. "What we encounter is not wholly naturally evolved." Many physical forms (short, tall, thin), mobile consciousness, DNA edited for roles, and one central hive or matrix soul: "all the same species, the same hive soul construct". Skin like a dolphin's, not green or grey.
  2. They think; you react. Some can talk in odd English; mostly they communicate by instant thought. "They think, I react."
  3. They are the gardeners. "They tend to the nursery that we are part of. They are the gardeners, the policemen, the guardians, and the caretakers of sentient life on earth", for this solar system, Alpha Centauri and "a few others", under a local governing species.
  4. They cull. Genetic lines "not in line with the preferred development path are culled from the planet"; about 3,000 years ago they destroyed an entire human sub-species. They prefer "a degree of strife" in the world.
  5. They are service-to-self. In MM's MAJestic-era reading they are "worker bees" for an older species, and a service-to-self race that would like humans to evolve the same way: STS humans who fail to advance are "absorbed into the Type-1 grey extraterrestrial hive" or become "a farmed creature". Their goals were never disclosed to MAJestic's rank and file.
  6. They are old here. Involved with humans "for at least 30 to 50,000 years", with Earth for 200 to 300,000; historically they behaved as gods to subjects; formal treaties only from the late 1940s; hostilities with the US in May 1975 and the mid-1980s, later "forgiven as misunderstandings".
  7. Nobody knows their home. "Anyone who states that they know where this race evolved from is wrong." Zeta Reticuli is a guess. The internet name "EBAN" he treats as fabricated.
  8. Not demons, not saints. "They are not inherently evil. They are a neutral race inhabiting artificial bodies to work and exist within our solar system." Abductees, he says, always agreed pre-birth; the memory isolation is what makes it feel otherwise.

Two portraits, one species

MM's 2019 article (from MAJestic memory) paints the Greys as service-to-self caretakers with their own harvest in mind. The 2021 material, following Alien Interview, treats the Greys as The Domain's own officers and pilots (Airl was one). MM does not reconcile the two in one place; in the FAQ he notes the Greys "do not get involved with a human soul construct without prior approval" and that "the Grays get to keep the psychic energy they turn and their master gets the soul". Part 17 lists this as an open tension in the archive.

The galaxy, as presented to MAJestic

Nurseries, dwarfs and neighbours

  • Five nurseries. "Within our (tiny) local region of space are certain solar systems that are all acting as nurseries for evolving intelligences": five systems, five species, tended by the same benefactors. Alpha Centauri is one of them, and (per the Commander) also part of the prison complex.
  • Dark suns are prime real estate. Planets around brown dwarfs are, in MM's view, better long-term homes than ours: stable, tidally stirred, shielded from bombardment. A species evolved there would see in infrared and find our Sun "too energetic". The Commander adds that the reality bubble's energetic star is exactly why "only certain approved forms can exist" here; the crowds of visiting species in popular lore are "an illusion".
  • Sirius is not home to anyone enlightened. A young, hot, dangerous system that changed violently 125 million years ago; MM is "skeptical" of every claim that beings from there guide humanity.
  • Hycean worlds are populous. Hot ocean worlds, the Commander says, are widely inhabited; several are prisons too. Life in the main universe is everywhere and mostly non-humanoid.
  • Cephalopods. MM's example of a native Earth intelligence that built its own soul archetype: octopus and squid edit their RNA and "demonstrate some amazing abilities"; an "approved archetype" exists for them today.

What MM says is not real

Reptilians
"I have never met a reptilian, nor have I any contact with MAJestic literature regarding such a creature." The shape-shifting-lizard story comes "from certain very rich oligarchs", not from extraterrestrials.
Star children, crystals, ascended masters
"There is no spiritual being that is going to show you the way through magic crystals (that you can buy on-line for only $99.98)."
Secret space fleets
Space marines, Mars colonies, "Project Serpo", "Alternative 3", chrononauts recruited as children: fabrications, in his reading, that share one trait: heroics without implants.
Named channelled aliens
The Commander's 2023 verdicts on popular figures: one "a businessman and story-teller", one "a profiteer", one not MAJestic; "many charlatans" are used by The Domain to herd people, "this person is not one of them".

Remember

  • Greys tend and cull; Mantids guide; Type-2 staff facilities; Progenitors seeded; The Domain administers; the Old Empire built.
  • The Greys are painted two ways in the archive: STS caretakers (2019) and Domain officers (2021).
  • The galaxy is crowded, policed and organised around approved sentience types and nurseries; Earth is one of five nearby nurseries.
  • No reptilians, no Sirius sages, no space marines.
