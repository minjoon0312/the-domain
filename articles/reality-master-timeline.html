The master timeline

Every dated claim in the archive on one line, from before the physical universe to the 2040s: the galaxy's history as MAJestic told it, the prison's history as Alien Interview and the Commander tell it, MM's own life from 1958 to China, the years of the blog and the channel, and the forecasts that carry dates. With the places where the dates disagree.

AI summary · part 39 of 40 12 min read · 2,689 words 17 sources ↓

Written by an AI, not by Metallicman. This is a plain-language summary of 17 archived articles (mainly the collections The Domain, MAJestic Disclosure). It restates what those sources say and does not add claims of its own. Every part links to its sources at the bottom so you can check the original wording.

In one breath

The archive gives dates for almost everything, from the age of the galaxy to the day the channel opened, and they are scattered across four hundred articles and thirty-eight parts of these summaries. This part puts them on one line. Five stretches: deep time (billions of years, from MAJestic's galaxy briefing), the prison's history (from 208,000 BCE in Alien Interview to the Commander's 2023 answers), MM's own life (born 1958, recruited 1981, retired 2006, writing since 2018), the years of the blog and the channel (2018 to 2026), and the forecasts that carry dates (2022 to the 2040s). Every date is a claim from the source named beside it; where two sources give two dates for the same thing, both are shown. Nothing here is more certain than the part it came from.

How to read it

Time in this model is not a single track (part 2): world-lines are static snapshots and "history" is assembled backwards from event pockets (parts 15 and 29). So a timeline is, strictly, a reader's convenience, and MM would say so. It is still the fastest way to see the shape of the story and to catch its contradictions. Dates come in three grades and are marked as such: source (a date printed in a document MM reprints, such as Alien Interview), MM (a date from his own memory or writing), and Commander (a date given through the 2021 to 2024 channel). Forecasts are marked claim.

Deep time: the galaxy as MAJestic told it

From "Our Galaxy as Presented to MAJestic" (2018) and its companions, with MM's own caveat attached: "I pulled this all out of my head, and I have no clue as to whether it is accurate or not." Part 32 has the detail.

The prison's history

Two sources supply this stretch and they overlap only partly. Alien Interview (a 1947 transcript published in 2008, part 21) gives a dated chronology from 208,000 BCE to 1307 AD. The Commander's answers (2021 to 2023, parts 22, 23 and 29) give round figures. Both are shown; where they disagree, the disagreement is kept.

Before the prison
Commander, October 2023: humanoids "appeared more or less uniformly" across the world, regional variants 400,000 years ago, seeding "from 2 to 3 million years ago", with no evolutionary or migration track. Earth was "a tranquil and benign world". MM's OOPART reading (part 36) adds tool-using humanoids at Hueyatlaco 250,000 years ago, and his own dating that humans "did not exist until around 25,000 years ago", which he uses to argue that the coal objects are non-human.
The Greys' involvement
MM's 2019 species file: with humans "for at least 30 to 50,000 years", with Earth for 200 to 300,000; formal treaties only from the late 1940s; "hostilities in May of 1975, and again in the early to middle 1980s"; autopsy data "gained between 1951 and 1978"; a policy position "dated to 2005".
The last physical fights
Commander, September 2021: Tunguska in 1908 was an Old Empire logistics facility under a force shield, "believed at the time to be the last"; the 1914 entry in Alien Interview is the officer who rode in the Archduke's body, captured at Sarajevo and held 27 years under Mars.
1947 to the 1950s
Sessions with Airl from 9 to 31 July 1947 (source). MM's reconstruction (part 21): a joint program chose "special individuals" by 1955/56; "My program came into being around 1955 to 1957."

MM's life

All dates in this stretch are MM's own, from memory, unless marked. He offers no documents and says so: "ten years ago did you go grocery shopping and buy some milk?" Parts 10 and 30 carry the narrative.

1958
"And was physically born in 1958." (MM, December 2021, dating his own program to the 1950s chain.)
1976
High school, "the Jimmy Carter years", the year he would be trapped in if he age-regressed himself (part 37).
1981
Aviation Officer Candidate School at NAS Pensacola; the pitch, the implantation of seven probes, the first portal to the off-world hospital and the EBP, calibration at China Lake. "Use of a MWI portal has been available at least since 1981 to selected members of MAJestic." Part 30 has the sequence.
1983
Corpus Christi, December: "major world-line template cluster realignment" (Commander, November 2021), one of the rare moments The Domain takes over a body.
1984 to 1985
Nags Head, North Carolina: an operation "conducted by a team of specialists" (MM's memory, read in 2021 as a Domain containment procedure); 1985 "upgraded", with missing time (part 26).
1988 to 2004
The "sentience crisis": implanted agents relocated and exposed to thought-generating situations (part 4). October 1992: the sick world-line (part 18).
Late 1990s to 2006
Commander: phase-out from the late 1990s, American operation shut "in the early 2003 / 2004"; "sex offender retirement" from the mid-1990s. MM: active 1983 to 2006, mass deactivation 2005, retirement 2006, prison, China.
1999 to 2003
Past-life regression around 1999 or 2000; a BaZi forecast from a Singapore group in 2003, "uncanny how accurate it can be" (part 13).

The blog and the channel, 2018 to 2026

This is the only stretch where the dates are publication dates, and therefore firm. It is also the stretch in which the model itself changed (part 17): MAJestic-era writing to 2020, Alien Interview and the channel from 2021, video and Patreon from 2022, ΨORM in 2026.

The forecasts that carry dates

The channel made predictions with years attached. These are the archive's most checkable claims and, as parts 24 to 29 said each time, they are logged as claims. Today's date on the site is 2026, so some have already passed their window.

Where the dates disagree

A timeline exposes the seams. These are the ones the summaries have already flagged, collected in one place so no reader has to find them twice.

When MAJestic ended
Late 1990s phase-out and a 2003 / 2004 shutdown (Commander, January 2022); mass deactivation in 2005 and retirement in 2006, "active 1983 to 2006" (MM). Part 30 keeps all of them.
When The Domain beat the Old Empire
"About 10,000 years ago" (MM's reading of Alien Interview, part 8); the Domain "interrupts prison planet management" in 3,450 to 3,100 BCE and the fleet is gone by 1150 AD (Alien Interview's chronology, part 21); a central complex destroyed "about 1,200 years ago" and a suppression device "around 1150 AD" (Commander, parts 8 and 22).
When the prison was built
The Old Empire took the junk yard "about 208,000 years ago" and built the complex "about 75,000" years ago (Commander); Alien Interview starts its chronology at 208,000 BCE with the import of beings "from another galaxy".
When Earth got its humans
Seeded "from 2 to 3 million years ago", variants 400,000 years ago (Commander, 2023); tool-users 250,000 years ago at Hueyatlaco (MM, 2021); humans "did not exist until around 25,000 years ago" (MM's coal-object argument, 2018).
Apollo
April 2022: feasible, shielded and fast. December 2023: the craft landed but "no men" walked; the footage "is a fiction".
The tunnel
Through 2022: operating, "the primary amnesia mechanism", avoid it. June 2024: "adjusted, culled, deactivated, rendered impotent". The 2021 instruction is never withdrawn.
Anunnaki
2022: Old Empire residents working the region. 2023: originally Domain task teams.
When MM's program began
July 2021 parsing: government contact in the 1970s. December 2021: a 1950s chain, "around 1955 to 1957", birth in 1958.
Titor's divergence
"1% to 2%" (Titor); "2 to 3%" and rising (MM).
World-lines per second
Not a date but the same kind of seam: 144 in one article, 4 in another (part 2).

A timeline is not evidence

Putting claims in order makes them look like a record. They are not. The deep-time dates are MM's recollection of a briefing he cannot document; the prison dates come from a 1947 transcript of unknown provenance and from a channel that speaks through MM's own mind; the life dates are his memory, without papers; the forecasts have, where the calendar has caught up with them, not happened as described. The only firm column is publication dates. Part 17 explains how to hold all this.

Remember

  • Five stretches: deep time, the prison, MM's life, the blog and channel, the forecasts.
  • Only the publication dates (2018 to 2026) are firm; everything else is a claim graded by its source.
  • The story's hinge dates: 1947 (the interviews), 1981 (Pensacola), 16 September 2021 (the channel), 2026 (ΨORM).
  • Four end-dates for MAJestic, three dates for the Old Empire's fall, two answers on Apollo and on the tunnel: kept, not reconciled.
  • Forecasts with years: 2022 to 2025 have passed; the 2030s and 2040s are still open.
