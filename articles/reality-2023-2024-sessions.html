Parts 13 to 15 of the comm: October 2023 to June 2024

The deep dive on parts 1, 2, 8, 9, 11 and 15. The late sessions, after a year's gap: how the war with the Old Empire was fought, why there is no evolutionary track, the state of the prison at "23 to 25 per cent complete", locks on a lawn instead of frames on a film, MM's caveat that false premises colour the answers, the December 2023 reversal on Apollo, and the June 2024 session with three Chinese students that ends with "Verbalization of intent is the legal document".

AI summary · part 29 of 40 14 min read · 3,037 words 3 sources ↓

Written by an AI, not by Metallicman. This is a plain-language summary of 3 archived articles (mainly the collections The Domain). It restates what those sources say and does not add claims of its own. Every part links to its sources at the bottom so you can check the original wording.

In one breath

After August 2022 the channel went quiet for over a year. Part 13 (October 2023) answered a batch of history questions: the Domain's war on the Old Empire, told as a campaign; Earth before the prison as "a tranquil and benign world"; why the fossil record shows a smooth story that never happened; the Moon as a shield against Oort-cloud debris; and a progress report, "roughly 23 to 25 per cent complete". It also gave a new picture of time, keys on a lawn full of locks, and a warning from the Commander to MM that questions built on lies get answers that are wrong in the same direction. Part 14 (December 2023) is short and sharp: Bashar is "applesauce", the hollow Earth is false, Antarctica is "the Tibet of this century", and the Apollo footage "is a fiction". Part 15 (June 2024) answers nineteen questions from three Chinese students, with Chinese idioms for the impatient and the rule that a spoken intention is the document The Domain acts on.

Part 13: history, and the state of the work

MM opened by listing what on his site actually matters, because a reader wanted to download it all: About Me, the MAJestic disclosure, the glossary, "Read this first", "Accept no wooden nickels", the intention campaigns, the MAJestic, extraterrestrial, Type-1 Grey, OOPART, world-line, John Titor and DIY portal indexes, the FAQ, the EBP Q&A, fate forecasting and past-life regression. "Most of MM content is now overly populated with topical social filler. This is intentional. It disguises the true and actual nature of the site." (This archive follows that list.) Then the Lost Battalion: "We know where every Domain member is, in what form they occupy, and what their spiritual / etheric signatures are. Our problem is not locating them, it is extracting them in such a way as to prevent catastrophic destruction of their surviving quantum forms." The Domain has built "avenues or funnels" to herd survivors into "stable stasis"; "MM is one such avenue, but there are others."

13-3a Before the prison
Humanoids "appeared more or less uniformly" across the world, with regional variants 400,000 years ago and seeding "from 2 to 3 million years ago", but with no evolutionary or migration track: "time as humans associate it does not exist." Event pockets on Earth's templates connect backwards automatically, producing the illusion of evolution, like pieces of paper drifting together on an ocean. Earth before the Old Empire "was a tranquil and benign world with numerous civilizations", then "a pleasure planet", then "a prison / hold area".
13-3b, 13-3c Chaco Canyon
"It is rare for a society, or culture to evaporate and disappear abruptly." The cliff dwellers migrated over "two to three generations" for environmental and political reasons; but "there actually WERE battle fragments / events and fighting over and at that particular geographic location(s)" between Domain and Old Empire forces, read by the locals as "messages from the regional deities".
13-3d The Ant People
Not Domain: "a separate group of entities that live inside the earth", the source of faerie and dwarf folklore, who "shuffle / walk a fine line" between physical and non-physical and run "huge bubble cities in the great depths of the oceans". The Domain assisted them once.
13-4b Mass extinctions
Bodies "dislodged from the Oort cloud by a brown-dwarf companion to the Sun"; the Moon's arrival reduced them; "the preferential solar system is a dim, red dwarf star" that would shield a close planet.
13-5 Farsight's "Greys"
"Yes and No." The intent "was / is pure", the understanding of the targets wrong; MM's image: a hunter "shooting at various trees, shrubs and bushes".
13-7 NDE walk-ins
None from The Domain for this questioner; "non-Domain partial attempts", none successful.
13-8 Curses
"Yes." A curse is an influence "on the non-physical aspects of an entity": the non-physical bodies, the templates, or "the sheath surrounding the reality environs", placed by those with knowledge and skill. Curses "can be positive or negative depending on the point of view".
13-9b Thank you
"This is straight from the Domain Commander. We are gratified that so many souls / consciousnesses / entities have volunteered to work with us. This is a much higher number than we anticipated." Lucid dreamers "have been stunning": "They think or believe that they are doing one thing, but the thoughts act as traces and we can sniff out the forms that attack them... we thus can hog out and remove those elements."
13-12 Denver airport
A public face and a hidden one: "transit hub for the Western elite, identity switching, storage".
13-17 Anunnaki, second meaning
First a term for Domain task teams, later applied to visiting beings. The Old Empire altered main-universe suits into reality-universe suits, centred in India, Egypt and the Middle East, China and Middle America, and "lived as gods"; Thoth was Old Empire administration.

13-18: the Commander's warning to MM about bad questions

MM asked why answers to questions built on false assumptions seemed wrong. "In the cases listed above, we have answered the questions correctly to you. You, yourself, flavored or colored the answers we provide. You do so by your apparent understanding / misunderstanding of the situation, which is often wrong... This is similar to Remote Viewing. If you think that the question is about the Iran Hostage situation, you will interpret the imagery in those terms. However, when that happens, we BLOCK your (you know what that feels like) and you correct and mitigate your answer... First of all, you MUST instruct your audience to be as truthful as possible. Hypothetical situations, or questions based on lies, will generate conflicts with the real answers that we provide. We (Domain) know what the truth actually is; you (MM) do not. To prevent a discoloring of the responses you must control the sources for the questions or stop the Q&A completely." This is the archive's standing reminder that every answer in parts 22 to 29 passed through MM's own understanding first.

Part 14: December 2023

14-1aMatias de Stefano

"A businessman and story-teller" who "entices with stories of the strange and unusual"; for some it "resonates properly in regards to their life direction". Nothing about escaping the system.

14-1bBashar

MM says he transcribed this one word for word, in 1920s slang: "Don't let this cat chisel you. He is a profiteer. A real come copper. It's all applesauce, his baloney will set you on the nut... tell him, or anyone who believes him, to breeze." Then, calmer: "There are many charlatans that Domain works with, at various levels, to institute change and direction vectors." Also: "Tim Tactics" is not MAJestic; only approved forms can exist inside this bubble, "so many ET species visiting is an illusion"; TLS is "an umbrella framework for gifted individuals, not Domain-run".

14-2Hollow Earth

"It's all duck soup. Contemporaneous human belief on the composition of planets is fairly accurate. The concept of hollow earth theory is false."

14-6Antarctica

"There are many fictions about Antarctica. This follows the same profile that was common in the early part of the last century regarding the Himalayas and Tibet." Byrd "stumbled into an ET expedition's teleportation resonant cavity", in MM's summary.

14-5, 14-7aHealing

"The questioner need not be concerned whether or not the body can be healed. As it can. The bigger question, and the real issue is SHOULD it be healed. Body condition through the lifecycle is a function of the pre-birth world-line template." For an elbow: poultices and herbal baths daily.

14-8All physical ETs are containers

A reader's summary, that every physical ET is a soul container on a mission, unfractionalised, coming and going at will, was called "balanced and reasonable... You are correct in general". Old Empire snares "are designed to kill anyone who suddenly recovers identity" (Matilda); graduated retrieval is "around 90% perfected".

14-7b: Apollo, reversed

MM: "I have intentionally blanked out my mind for this answer as I do not want to taint it." The answer: "Apollo program was a real program. However, humans did not set foot on the moon as was described in popular media. Spacecraft was set to the moon. Spacecraft orbited the moon. Spacecraft landed on the moon. However, the imagery of astronauts walking on the moon is a fiction. The Van Allen belt problem has not been completely overcome. At the time of the purported landings, a fiction was generated for political expediency." In April 2022 (part 27) the same channel had said the belts were solved by shielding and speed. The archive keeps both answers, twenty months apart, as stated.

Part 15: June 2024, three students, nineteen questions

Three Chinese nationals "presently in school" sent MM nineteen questions. It took him "about a half a month" and multiple sessions; the health, diet and drug questions required him to draft essays and go through them "line by line with the Domain Commander". The Commander answered several with Chinese idioms.

1 What to practise
The trio "are in the midst of various activities that train for a host of roles", mostly in dreams; roles are pre-planned. One acts as "a recharging battery" for pro-Domain entities; each of them "runs 1 to 3 slave consciousnesses in animals", an Old Empire trap in which the Mantids "run strict scripts unless vocalised changes are made".
2 After death
The procedure stands (call, refuse the Mantid's demand, avoid the tunnel, stay calm). One of the three had earlier asked to be re-injected as a human in a future life; "this is an out-of-date request and should be ignored. But it still exists until the entity says and states otherwise. Verbalization of intent is the legal document that Domain uses to authorize injection." On the second and third passes the Commander added the idiom 草船借箭, "borrowing arrows with straw boats", about wisdom in the face of treachery.
3 Calm
"Entity THREE is missing the point of being human. Emotions play an important part of the human experience." Do not suppress them.
4 Proof
Asked to manipulate a device or send a shared dream as evidence: "We cannot alter the physical reality unless we are in a physical container. The way around this is to ask an entity already in a physical container to do so for us... However, asking an agent or friend of ours to do something (such as MM here) is outside of the contract agreements that we make with our friends and volunteers... What you are asking for lie outside of those boundaries and we will not jump over them."
5, 6 Their histories
"All three entities were Baroque era friends during a previous physical incarnation", all female then. All three "have volunteered to work with Domain on multiple occasions, and in multiple lifetimes"; each has had non-physical alterations, one of them three times, "conducted by another group associated with this regional sphere" with Domain's blessing.
7 The tunnel, 2024
"It is worth noting that the memory erasure tunnel has been adjusted / culled / deactivated / rendered impotent. Thus enabling better interactions with Mantid GP and Mantid Prime with Domain interjections." The Domain "conducts the bulk of inmate interactions on the non-physical planes".
8 Dreams
Keep a journal for three successive days and read trends, not literal content; waking-state control of "anger, greed, lust, and other vices" matters more.
9, 10 Where to live, what is coming
No country is safe from the solar-cycle chaos, "a global phenomenon"; some regions absorb it more gently. The reality universe "operates on potentials": the future is set at injection unless slides or affirmation results change it. MM's readers mostly sit on mild-to-medium templates "while much of humanity sits on severe ones"; nuclear war "has already manifested in some world-line templates"; bleed-through events (bird die-offs, disappearances) are the sign.
11, 12 Other channels; AI
Any group that discusses lucid dreaming, PSI training, "the writings of Matilda" or protecting the non-physical body is useful. AI "is a technology"; "it was presented in all of the Trio's pre-birth world-line templates".
13 Consciousness everywhere
"All things; living and inanimate, have a type of consciousness. Whether it is a rock, a stone, a bird, a human, or a phonograph." Billions of types; an IS-BE is "an array of quantum configurations" of which a soul is a part and a consciousness a sub-part; "your quanta occupy multiple souls / consciousnesses / species simultaneously"; "NPCs" are low-active-quanta containers.
15 Geniuses
Intelligence "is one attribute, not preferable" (the Zhou Yu and Zhuge Liang story); mediocrity in one life says nothing about others.
16 The DEF book
"The book is a fiction. It is a for-profit effort using a popular venue to acquire clients... we did not" direct it.
18 Psychedelics
Drugs "operate by controlling various attributes of the physical body"; a psychedelic "can remove a veil", some DMT entities are real and others distortions, but the path is "not recommended".
19 Health
Lean, muscle, sleep, whole food; the list from part 23's diet answer, extended.

Read with care

These are the latest sessions in the archive and contain its clearest self-critique: the Commander's instruction that dishonest or hypothetical questions "will generate conflicts with the real answers", and MM's admission that he colours what he receives. The Apollo reversal, the 2024 claim that the tunnel is "rendered impotent" (against the 2021 instruction to avoid it at all costs, which is not withdrawn), and the personal readings for the three students should all be read with that in mind. Nothing in the health, drug or diet answers is medical advice.

Remember

  • The war: vanish, withdraw, strike everywhere at once, mop up; the captured became "vats" for new souls.
  • No evolution: humanoids appeared uniformly; the smooth story is event pockets joining backwards.
  • Progress, late 2023: new inmates barred, exits offered, torture cut, Earth at 40 per cent of the mean, the whole "23 to 25 per cent".
  • You are a key on a lawn of locks; the film reel was only a picture.
  • Apollo: shielded and fast (2022); landed but "no men" walked (2023). Both stand.
  • "Verbalization of intent is the legal document." Say it.
