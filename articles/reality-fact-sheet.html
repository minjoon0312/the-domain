The fact sheet: every number in one place

The last part. Every figure the archive states, sorted by subject and sourced to the part that explains it: the universe, the prison, the soul, the intention method, MAJestic and the machines, the species, heaven, the mysteries, the guest writers, and the archive itself. A reference to check the other thirty-nine against.

AI summary · part 40 of 40 13 min read · 2,862 words 16 sources ↓

Written by an AI, not by Metallicman. This is a plain-language summary of 16 archived articles (mainly the collections Terms and Conventions, For New Visitors – Read This First). It restates what those sources say and does not add claims of its own. Every part links to its sources at the bottom so you can check the original wording.

In one breath

Thirty-nine parts of prose carry a few hundred numbers between them: percentages of a soul, ages of a hammer, teslas in a magnet, members of a lost battalion. This part lists them. Each figure is given once, with the source's own wording where the wording matters, the grade of the claim, and the part that explains it. It is a reference, not an argument: nothing here is more reliable than where it came from, and the archive's numbers disagree with one another in a few places that are marked. Read it to check a memory, to settle "did he say 35 or 40 per cent", or to see at a glance how much of this material is measured, how much is remembered, and how much is channelled.

The archive itself

These are the only numbers in this part that can be checked by counting.

Longest article
The full text of Michael Newton's Journey of Souls as reprinted by MM: about 97,000 words. The longest of MM's own is the Type-1 Grey species file (part 31).
Busiest year
2021: 126 articles, the year of Alien Interview, Daegonmagus, PissedLizard and the channel.
The channel
Fifteen numbered sessions plus the early rounds, 16 September 2021 to June 2024; one channel, MM's EBP (part 9).
ΨORM
33 numbered sections, a readme calling itself version 13.5, plus core thesis, master index, bridges, glossary, predictions and roadmap: 62 documents, about 79,000 words (part 35).

The universe

Ages
Universe about 14 billion years; galaxy about 9; biology in the galaxy about 6 billion years ago; sentience standardised about 1 billion years ago; our solar system a nursery for 540 million years. (MM, "pulled this all out of my head", part 32.)
The Drake count
750 million to 1.5 billion stars in MM's count; "at least 80% of the solar systems have planets"; "at minimum nine candidates for planetary life evolution" per system; about two in a habitable zone; product roughly 38,000 civilisations, "certainly the number would be larger than zero" (part 32).
The nurseries
Five named nearby systems: Alpha Centauri (4.24 light-years, three stars), Barnard's Star (about 6, a very old red dwarf), LHS 288 (15.6), Gliese 667 (20.7; component C with "at least six planets (possibly seven)"), and our own. Elsewhere MM counts six; the archive keeps both (part 32).
Dimensions
Six described; "7th dimensional" technology attributed to extraterrestrials; humans "can only see three aspects of what is in actuality an 11 viewpoint condition" (part 2).
Sirius
"went through massive changes 125 million years ago"; MM "skeptical" of Sirian-guide claims (part 32).
The Moon
"not precisely hollow, it has a large void instead"; the prison's local control centre is inside it (parts 8, 23, 32).

The prison and The Domain

The region
An ovoid holding five inhabited solar systems; the great war about 70 million years ago; the Old Empire took the "junk yard" about 208,000 years ago and built the complex about 75,000 years ago (Commander, part 22). Alpha Centauri "is also part of the prison complex" (part 23).
The lost battalion
3,000 members, "scattered worldwide, mostly in human bodies" (part 22).
The schedule
"From the start it was anticipated that the project can begin in 5,000 years"; it began early because inmate governments asked (part 22). The Domain will not take total control "for something like 5,000 years" (part 9). Recruiting the general population was scheduled for 2021, forty years after MM joined MAJestic (part 22).
Progress
Late 2023: Earth at about 40 per cent of the regional mean; the whole conversion "around 23 to 25% in completion"; memory retrieval under supervision "around 90% complete"; suit changes to finish "within the next twenty earth years" (parts 8, 29).
The duty cycle
Volunteers are used sparingly; an ideal duty cycle is 20 to 30 per cent work (part 9).
Who is here, and why
The channel's rough split of organisations inside the prison: 14% sincere, 11% mischievous, 24% impassioned or religious, 22% prison snares, 19% accidental, 3% set up by Domain agents "as a pastime" (ended around 1955), 7% miscellaneous. MM's note: the numbers "did not add up so I rounded them downwards"; he thinks the Commander "was just pitching vague rough figures for illustrative purposes" (part 22).
The scale of harm
"A big bad is measured in a billion or more people killed"; medium bads in hundreds of millions; small bads one or two million. As of 6 August 2022, "medium bads" were called "highly probable" (parts 16, 28).
The reform program
Six things that went wrong, listed by the Commander (part 26). The Heavens: prison fixtures, "mini pocket universes" to be de-tethered (part 23).
The last fights
Tunguska, 1908; the officer under Mars for 27 years after 1914 (parts 21, 22).
The electroshock
"billions of volts" at death, per Alien Interview (part 21).

You: soul, consciousness, body

The soul in a life
"Which can vary from 5% up to 40%"; MM's set at 35 per cent, "very high"; 65 per cent stays in Heaven; a soul can run two consciousnesses at once (MM, part 20). The Commander: two or three consciousnesses at 20 to 23 per cent each; MM's at 32 to 33 (part 3).
The soul's wiring
Garbons (clumps) joined by swales (lanes); "22 major interactive exchange lanes" in a human; primary attachment the pineal gland (part 20).
The flicker
4 Hz as "the average rate of an alert, waking brain"; faster when focused, slower asleep (ΨORM, part 35). A 4.7-second sentence spans 18 to 19 world-lines: speaking an affirmation is "literal piloting" (part 3). World-lines per second: 144 in one article, 4 in another (part 2).
Memory
Not in the brain; the body is "RAM", the record is the "hard drive" outside it, "ripped out" in inmate bodies (parts 3, 22). "How can you learn through your mistakes when you remember 10,000 past lives and 100,000 similar mistakes?" (Newton, via MM, part 20).
The inmate body
An "interrupting" feature grows in by age five to seven and blocks travel in sleep (part 22). Rh-negative: a suit variant about 60,000 years old (part 8).
Visits
About 15 to 17 per cent of people experience a visit or vivid dream of themselves much older (Commander, part 4).
The body, per the Commander
Body fat under about 20 per cent for men and 25 for women; strength training; real sleep; whole food (part 13).

The intention method

The basic cycle
Write and read aloud daily for about 3 months; release; turbulence; most manifestations within about 2 years (part 5).
The three paces
Stroll: three months on, three off, "what I strongly recommend to all newcomers". Trot: one month on, one off, MM's own pace, "certainly no longer than eight months in total". Mosey: six months on and off, the "heavy lifting / serious change crowbar". Maintenance: about one week (part 19).
Time on the list
Most lists take under ten minutes to read; MM's plan for the audio method allows "from 20 minutes to an hour" (part 19).
Why it "fails"
"For about 95% of the people the reasons why something is not manifesting is that you need to give things time" (part 19).
The brain-wave bands MM uses
Delta 0.2 to 3 Hz, theta 3 to 8, alpha 8 to 12, beta 12 to 27; carrier tone at the top of the 20 Hz to 20 kHz hearing range (part 19).
The template change
September 2021: the master template shifted "into the plastic region"; expected effect: faster affirmation results, easier lucid dreaming, more slides (part 4).

MAJestic, and the machines

The implants
"For me, I had 7 devices implanted"; brain mapped first; a cross-hair reticle moved by mind (part 30).
The queue
About thirty women stepping through an invisible door at the portal (part 30).
The van
A 1976 Dodge Tradesman 100, $2,000 (part 30).
The limit
MM's own travel: passive, carried by the EBP, capped at about 2 per cent divergence in manual mode (part 12).
The DIY portal
Air gap "at least seven feet high, and three feet wide"; the swap over "a three-second period"; field guessed at "5 to 10 tesla"; reference points Earth 0.0003 T, MRI 1.5 to 7, LHC 8.3, levitation 10+; a bench-top magnet's eighth-inch gap scaled to "six or seven feet"; ten posts (part 37).
Titor's C204
Two singularities; four cesium clocks (the C206: six, with an optical check); three computers; "about 10 years an hour"; breaks away "at about 60 years"; divergence "about 1-2%" per trip (MM: 2 to 3 and rising); "10 type C204 time machines, and 20 type C206"; "about three people and equipment" (part 37).
Titor's dates
Fax 29 July 1998; time travel "invented in 2034"; from 2036 to 1975 for an IBM 5100; civil war "starts in 2005", nuclear war "in 2015", "no Olympics after 2004"; patent filed 1 October 2004, published 2006 (part 37).
Hemi-Sync
1983 report; "7 days" of tapes "to get there"; page 25 missing; Going Home: 11 subject and 12 support files; Radiance: eight tracks (part 37).

The species

The Greys
Type-1A 3.5 to 4.5 ft, about 40 lb, brain 2,500 to 3,500 cc; four myths denied ("a dying race", "a slave race", "religious, chanting", "from Zeta Reticuli"); "approximately 2000 years more advanced than we are currently today, but that was about 50,000 years ago"; a sub-species culled about 3,000 years ago; a genetic marker cultivated about 4,000 years ago; hostilities May 1975 and the early to mid 1980s (part 31).
The Mantids
Two kinds in the 2021 sessions; a Mantid Prime per person; the "human mantid trinity" of human, Mantid and Grey (parts 7, 23, 38).
Sentience types
Three kinds in humans in 2019; four by 2021 (parts 32, 34).
Doll bodies
Synthetic containers tuned to an IS-BE, with a class system deciding who wears what (part 31).

Heaven and death

The doors
Three options in MM's fate-forecasting article; a fourth, "call for The Domain", added September 2021; "the rule of three" as the video title for the steps at death (parts 6, 38).
Heaven's share
65 per cent of a soul stays there while 35 lives (MM's own figure); the Heavens are "mini pocket universes" in the Commander's account (parts 20, 23).
ΨORM's counts
Eight stages of death; ten bridges to other systems of thought; 33 sections; a 4 Hz duty cycle (part 35).
Dreams of the dead
MM and two friends woke at 3 am four days after a friend's death with the same dream (part 20).
The tunnel
"the primary amnesia mechanism" through 2022; "rendered impotent" per June 2024; the 2021 instruction to avoid it stands (parts 6, 17).

The mysteries

Ages claimed for objects
The London Hammer's rock: about 40 million years (a shellfish tool for that shoreline, "not made of stainless steel", found "approximately ten years after stainless steel was patented"). Objects in coal: 300 million years (the bell, the lamp); the wheel: "300 million years, and 12 million years"; the polished geode part: "200 million years"; the meteorite object: 4.5 billion; the Aiud pawl: about one million years, lost near the Black Sea (part 36).
Humans, on MM's dating
"did not exist until around 25,000 years ago" (the coal argument); tool-users at Hueyatlaco 250,000 years ago; megalith builders 10,000 years ago with a better sense of "non-physical actions" (part 36).
The airships
Sightings of 1880, 1892, 1896, 1897 and 1909 (part 36).
Mars
A CIA remote viewing of Mars "one million years B.C." (1984); the Viking Labeled Release results of 1976; Oxia Palus as a repurposed smelting facility (parts 13, 15, 36).
The Crespi collection
Burnt in an arson in 1962 (part 36).

The guest writers and the readers

Daegonmagus
Sleep paralysis from age eight; the audience of 11 May 2012; initiated into "the Unseen 5" in November 2016; "20,000 souls"; an eight-level maze; a "Consciousness Doping Facility" about 2005 and a "Super Soldier Program" 2008 to 2009; "seven years of infiltration"; the underground place "lagging our physical reality by 3 months"; posts from August 2021 to December 2023 (part 33).
PissedLizard
Three dispatches in August 2021; a crash remembered "2 years ago" (part 34).
The readers
A nine-part FAQ (June 2019); nineteen reader questions in 2021; nineteen questions from three Chinese students in June 2024 (parts 29, 34).
The premonitions
A nuclear attack "set for 28th February 2022" in SD's dream, which did not happen (part 33).

The video and Patreon counts

YouTube
115 videos and 2 articles in 31 groups; 28 marked "@"; Basics 101 to 106; seven DIY portal videos, one time-machine video, an empty "DIY 3"; nine Tunnel of Light videos plus two by DM (part 38).
Patreon
285 posts, 28 April 2022 to 13 September 2026; 230 video embeds, 25 links, 18 video files, 9 text posts, 3 images; 31 public; 24 posts in June 2022 and 22 in April 2024; top tags affirmation 46, campaign 42, domain 32, manifestation 30, soul 29 (part 38).
The classes
Numbered 101 to 106, 203 to 213, 301 to 310, 401 to 403, with case studies MM03 to MM09 (part 38).

Grades, one last time

Counted: the archive's own totals. Printed: dates and figures in documents MM reprints (Alien Interview, the Gateway report, Titor's transcripts), whose provenance is the document's problem. Remembered: MM's life and his MAJestic briefings, offered without papers. Channelled: everything the Commander said, which came through MM's own mind and vocabulary. Forecast: anything with a future date. A number's precision says nothing about its grade; "22 lanes" and "4.24 light-years" sit in different columns.
