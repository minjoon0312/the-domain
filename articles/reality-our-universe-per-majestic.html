Our universe, as presented to MAJestic

The deep dive on parts 1, 9 and 11. The 2018 to 2020 "Our Universe" articles: the galaxy's timeline from 14 billion years to a 540-million-year-old nursery, the Drake equation redone with a policed galaxy, the five nursery systems in the neighbourhood and why Sirius is not one of them, brown dwarfs as the preferred real estate, the bases on Mars, Titan and the Moon, six dimensions, the sentience-sorting Rufus test, cephalopods, and the fear of a genetic caste system.

AI summary · part 32 of 40 11 min read · 2,426 words 22 sources ↓

Written by an AI, not by Metallicman. This is a plain-language summary of 22 archived articles (mainly the collections MAJestic Related Index, Extraterrestrial Species). It restates what those sources say and does not add claims of its own. Every part links to its sources at the bottom so you can check the original wording.

In one breath

Before the Commander, before Alien Interview, MM's picture of the cosmos came from MAJestic binders, a handful of remote-viewing reports, and his own reading of astronomy. The "Our Universe" articles of 2018 to 2020 lay it out: a galaxy "simply teeming with all kinds of intelligent life", "well patrolled and policed" rather than empty; a timeline in which sentience standardised itself a billion years ago and our solar system was set aside as a nursery 540 million years ago; a neighbourhood of five nursery systems around dim red stars, all under one jurisdiction; a Mars facility at Oxia Palus, a base on Titan, and a Moon with a void inside it; six dimensions, of which humans can touch the fifth by thought; and a species being sorted by sentience, with the "frightening possibility" that the sorting ends in a genetic caste system. His caveat on all of it: "I pulled this all out of my head, and I have no clue as to whether it is accurate or not."

The galaxy's timeline, in MAJestic's version

A limit he insists on: "for a successful transfer of information and technology from one species to another, there has to be a uniformity of understandings and perceptions. Oh, forget that Hollywood bullshit about mathematics being the language of species communication. It isn't." A species that sees in infrared "sees a different kind of sky at night". That is why, he says, "myself and a handful of others were tasked with our roles", and why what follows is translated into "our established understandings".

The Drake equation, with a policeman in it

The neighbourhood: five nurseries

brown dwarfsThe real estate they prefer

"Rather than a big empty area of nothingness, the space that surrounds us is full of all sorts of objects": brown dwarfs, rogue planets, comet-like bodies. A planet close to a small dim star gets "[1] stability, [2]... tidal forces to jump-start evolution, and [3]... a deflective mass" against bombardment. To such a species "our sun and solar system would be too energetic", which is why he thinks "any purposed intent of this planet would be quite different from what we would expect". A companion article asks whether our own Sun has a brown-dwarf partner; the Commander later said the mass extinctions came from bodies "dislodged from the Oort cloud by a brown-dwarf companion" (part 29).

habitabilityMoons of gas giants

"All the objections to earth-like planets around cold stars are eliminated by the presence of a gas giant": a large moon of a giant in the habitable zone gets tides, a magnetic shield and a stable orbit. "Our solar system is unique", which "suggests one of two things": intelligent life is rare, or we are not looking at the typical case.

the rule"Adaptability, not earth-like"

"We should stop thinking about earth-like habitation, and instead think of adaptability... by a species that evolved upon a stable planet that orbited around a long-life dwarf." Perception shapes science: a species that sees in infrared builds a different astronomy.

Facilities: Mars, Titan, the Moon

The NASA article explains the public silence: "All American led space exploration came to an end in the 1970's. This was the result of direct contact with an extraterrestrial species. From that moment on, all actual space exploration efforts went deep-black. NASA was cut down in size to keep public perception intact." Two programs remain, "a very active black program, and a token white (public) program"; CARET, the Silicon Valley office reverse-engineering non-human hardware, is his example of the first.

Six dimensions

Sentience, sorting, and the caste-system fear

The Rufus test
From "Why the Earth is no longer a Prison Planet" (September 2021): watch a "Rufus" video, one of MM's clips of everyday heroism. If "in your heart, you think that they are suckers, idiots, or fools; then you are probably a Service to Self sentience." Joy and a wish to help: Service to Others. Boredom: "Service to Another". Curiosity without connection: "a disjointed sentience". "The ability to leave this environment depends on your true nature."
Corralled animals
"We are corralled animals being farmed for our sentience" (2020): the human herd is kept, tended and sorted; the earlier Grey article's "farming of humans" (part 31) in its bluntest form.
The Progenitors
Joseph McMoneagle's remote viewing of humanity's makers, reprinted "as just another puzzle piece": "Progenitors: created the foundation for the human species. Benefactors: presently involved in cultivating the human species." "I'm not saying this is exactly how humans are created."
Cephalopods
"One of numerous intelligent species who has evolved on earth"; they "have edited their RNA", show abilities "beyond human technology", and "have an individualized soul construction that has adopted and evolved into an approved archetype", so that today "we can see what an approved archetype looks like for this species", and, in extinct ancestors, what unapproved ones looked like.
Stratification
The "Frightening Possibility": once our dominant sentience is determined, "another species... will assist in large scale genetic reprogramming of our species during the conception process. There is a chance that the genetic reprogramming could result in a stratified human caste system." His own job description sits in the same article: agents with technical backgrounds "lived normal lives while the probes ran 24-7", monitored by others who "conducted world-line slides or MWI readjustments... to assist in the development of unified human sentience". "Pretty boring stuff. I will admit."
The secrets
"Here are the Secrets of the Universe" (2018) is mostly a list of what is not: "There are no Reptilians, or star beings... There isn't any shape-changing Reptilians that are secretly directing the United States government towards a one-world government. Nope. That idea is from certain very rich oligarchs. Extraterrestrials couldn't care less." And the bracket around all of it: "everything that you know, what you think you know, and what you are taught in most schools is wrong."
Structure
"Our universe is often quite confused and mislabeled... what we are observing is a string of static world-line universes while we experience time." All of them lie in "the physical universe", which "connects to the place where our soul resides... with a tunnel, also known as a tunnel of light." Drugs and altered brains give "glimpses" of the rest; "NOT being able to see the world as it actually is... is really a good thing... for now."

Read with care

These are the pre-2021 articles. Their vocabulary ("galactic federation jurisdiction", "benefactors", "policing group") is later replaced by The Domain, the Old Empire and the prison bubble, and some content is superseded outright: five nurseries become six systems in a containment field; "the galaxy is protected" becomes "you are in a prison". The astronomy is a mix of published science, MM's summaries of it, and claims he attributes to MAJestic material ("recounted by memory"); the dated galactic timeline, the Oxia Palus facility, the Titan base and the L-shaped landers are unverifiable. The Drake arithmetic is his and his terms are guesses, as he says. The archive presents the 2018 to 2020 cosmology as the foundation the later sessions were laid on, not as a second, competing model.

Remember

  • A policed, crowded galaxy; sentience standardised a billion years ago; our system a nursery for 540 million.
  • Drake with a guardian term: tens of thousands who could talk, few who do, and a reason for the quiet.
  • Five nurseries around dim stars, not Sirius; brown dwarfs are where the neighbours live.
  • Mars (Oxia Palus), Titan, a hollow-ish Moon; NASA is the white program, the black one went dark in the 1970s.
  • Four dimensions to live in, a fifth to steer by thought, a sixth called Heaven.
  • Sorting by sentience is the point of the place; the risk is a caste system written into the genes.
