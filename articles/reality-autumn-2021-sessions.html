October and November 2021: tasks, backdoors, and the war fear

The deep dive on parts 7, 8, 9 and 10. The second and third months of the channel: the call for lucid-dreaming scouts, Daegonmagus's island prison, MM's own questions about who he is, the Moon as the prison's control room, two kinds of Mantids, the "one-way mirror" pockets, the six-system bubble, and the November session in which MM asked about nuclear war and got answers he told readers to treat as claims.

AI summary · part 23 of 40 18 min read · 3,866 words 8 sources ↓

Written by an AI, not by Metallicman. This is a plain-language summary of 8 archived articles (mainly the collections The Domain, MAJestic Related Index). It restates what those sources say and does not add claims of its own. Every part links to its sources at the bottom so you can check the original wording.

In one breath

After the first month (part 22) the channel changed character. Instead of answering readers' questions, the Commander started giving them jobs. In October MM relayed an official request for lucid dreamers to scout the prison's "backdoors", published what they saw (a volcanic-island prison, a celebrity "workshop"), and then asked the questions he most wanted answered about himself: why he flies into "structured rage", who "Mades Escapalion" is, what happens to him when he dies. The answers redrew the map: the prison's control room is inside the Moon; there are two kinds of Mantids; the complex is a bubble of six solar systems, Alpha Centauri included; and the whole thing is "in a complex state of disarray with many factions". In November, frightened by the news, MM asked about war and was told to answer his own question. He did, and then wrote that all of it should be read as claims.

8 October: the task call-out

MM reports "quieter" EBP signals arriving in three packets, three projects: an article on backdoors with a request for volunteers; a "wish machine" built from mind-control patents to accelerate affirmation campaigns (the device of part 19); and building and selling it. The article opens with a hypothesis he says is his, not the Commander's: that other parties profit from the prison the way people profit from prisons in America. The trigger was a near-death experiencer's email, forwarded via a site called "Tricked by the Light", describing a "Demiurge", souls treating human lives as "life movies" watched "like Netflix", and the line "we are called Love Puppets".

15 to 18 October: what the scouts brought back

The response was "overwhelmingly positive", and then nearly every volunteer had the same experience within hours or days of speaking the affirmation: sleep paralysis with worst-fear imagery, which readers began calling the "Join the Domain dream". MM's explanation, consistent with September: in the non-physical, thoughts are tangible, fears are Old Empire "trigger blocks" that make inmates resist changes to their bodies, and The Domain has to operate on the non-physical body ("remove the chains", "change you out of the inmate uniform", give an "access pass to go through the fence", scrub trackers, erase pre-life behaviour settings). He also introduced a validity scale for everything that follows: KNOW, BELIEVE, SUSPECT, with confirmation "pings" felt as an endorphin rush.

17 October: MM's own questions

Between task reports MM asked about himself, "very personal and selfish. But that is my personal choice." He prefaced it with what he does not believe: no shape-shifting reptilians, no star-children, the Greys are not "a dying race" and not "humans from the future"; his implants "can be seen in an MRI".

The Domain's war
Expansion in the Milky Way continues; "much of the Old Empire conquered; pockets of resistance"; they met a very large, "rather benign and peaceful" empire about 10,000 light-years across that grew by conquest.
What the Old Empire looked like
Not skyscrapers: "gargantuan public constructions", a society of diminutive people (the source of dwarf, elf, faerie and goblin tales), technology "akin to magic spells, hexes, alchemy / occult".
Why conquer at all
"You got me there." The Domain made the bulk of the Master Universe; other universes merged in and brought negative systems that "tear the fabric", like a snag in silk or pantyhose. The Domain smooths the wrinkles before "the NEXT-BIG-THING".
Not dying, not from the future
A social structure of IS-BEs "who spend most time in the timeless state"; none die; a merit system "for trillions of years"; they can enter the MWI at any time, so "time travellers" only in that sense. Evolved future humans? "Absurd."
Cats
Not inmates: no amnesia, no template; they latch onto a human and share the ups and downs, "feline lucid dreaming" while resting, and find a body near you again and again. Cats consider humans slow, lovable companions who "can't hunt for shit".
Mantids
"A hybrid-construct based on a local dominant earth-centrist body form", altered by the Old Empire to herd humans (and pigs, horses, dolphins, elephants); bred to help humans "endure strife and then be rewarded"; they ignore non-amnesiac creatures like cats.
Why me
A pre-birth agreement: "We made you this way." To carry out the mission they changed his physical and non-physical bodies: removed diseases ("hidden time-bombs") and trackers, "filled holes", removed the "DO NOT CHANGE" areas of his template.
After death
"You will not be discarded." His IS-BE body is being rehabilitated to work outside the prison; a role continues.
Who is MM
Not a battalion member. He "was Mades Escaplion": a functionary, minister or administrator in the Old Empire, "a cog in the machine", neither cruel nor evil, tracked by "personality signature" after the fleet was destroyed and steered, with the Mantids, into this life. Useful now "for unravelling the traps".
Who is speaking
"The unit Commander for all Domain operations in this region." MM is "my I/O... hands, ears, senses"; thousands are implanted, "only a handful vetted".

26 October: the Moon, and two kinds of Mantids

The session opens with the Commander's version of "the highest truth": the fish tank. Fish see the telephone on the table as "a rectangular shaped object" and the lady who feeds them as "a swanky goddess"; "the fishbowl is a construct. The lady, as beautiful and kind as she is, is their keeper." Then the plain version: "you are in a prison", and "outside the prison walls is a universe that does not at all resemble what you think." No Star Trek rocket ships. Battalion members are among MM's readers, and it is better they do not know yet: "gruff and cantankerous by nature".

Other answers of the day: Mades Escapleon "was not a human archetype" originally; the Old Empire was home to several species, faerie-, goblin- and dwarf-like; MM's image of "a smallish, ugly businessman with an abrupt manner and a sneering demeanour" was close. Cats, four weeks late: the feline archetype "refused / thwarted every attempt to make a feline prison suit", so engineers shelved the project, which is why cats are free. The road to release, in order: [1] the Old Empire purged of its darkest elements; [2] trapped IS-BEs return to former lives; [3] before release, sentience sorting, "scrubbing" of skin suits (STO get natural archetype suits; STS, SFA and DIS get "parole suits"), memory re-injection ("we will attempt and succeed"), phased release with the battalion first and STS last, and the most dangerous "banished to the Heaven pocket-within-the-pocket" or "reduced to basic components through a system resembling the tunnel of light until rendered inert". A near-term forecast, asked in October 2021: about seven years of adjustment to "a new normal", with the US, UK and parts of Europe hit hardest. A Clifford-algebra question was first rejected as an "insincere request made by a malevolent entity" (MM banned the questioner and his city), then answered: yes, real-coefficient Clifford algebra can represent the inmates' reality, though mathematics is "awkward and feeble" from inside and relies on time existing. MM: "I don't know if he is pulling my leg." And a book called Domain Expeditionary Force Rescue Mission: "Read the book, and then ask the question again."

5 November: rage, Jesus, dinner, and the war

The book verdict came back: a fiction, "discard it", with a concept attached, "quanta antibodies" that attack every liberating message (the teachings in India, the gospel, the New Age). "Alien Interview is 100% accurate." Then the personal question MM had been avoiding: why he goes into "structured rage". The answer is one of the most striking in the archive.

"You are NOT a normal human being."

"When our agency / MAJestic installed the ELF probes, they / we / us also transformed your personality. We compartmentalized you." Beyond the personalities he knows (the scientist, the Naval officer) are others he does not: "Dr. Jeckle and Mr. Hyde", "The Wolfman (actually, the image is of a half-man, half-sabertooth tiger)", "Mr. Dracula / The Mummy", "The Incredible Hulk". "None of these alter personalities are muted. They are all active." They can be triggered by memories, events, codes and radio frequencies (a heliport emitter near his home puts him in "stand by for orders"). "You are a loaded gun with no safety switch", which is "why you needed to be sent to prison" and why he is advised never to return to the United States. "No one is harmed. At worst, your wife looks at you strange." Later the same session: MM is "a hybrid" (human archetype, inmate suit, ELF, EBP, operations), as are all members of his sub-project, and so, in a lesser way, are all Domain volunteers: "the stranger your dreams, the more changes."

sleepWhy we sleep

Designed into animals so the IS-BE can leave the body; prison suits keep the need but "seal the egress", so inmates "unwind and dream randomly". Volunteers whose blockages were removed may now dream of battles: mission taskings. A dog, a pack of animals, or others in the dream means you are working in a supervised team; "we never allow our assets to work without support."

Jesus"Christianity is a vehicle"

"An engineered social control", taken over repeatedly by good and evil factions; The Domain did not create it, "at least not like others have written", and was busy with space battles at the time. Second answer: Jesus is a figurehead often used as a trap, and also "instrumental in sorting service-to-others sentiences": following the New Testament directives builds STO behaviour and speeds processing out; lip service entrenches you.

pingingHow to ask for a friend

Say aloud before sleep: "I am pinging the Domain Commander in assistance of a question to Metallicman." One reader's pastry-chef teacher turned out to be not a battalion member but "a very important, long-missing IS-BE" tied to an organisation "higher, larger, older than The Domain"; superiors were alerted.

dietDinner from the Commander

The Domain "participated in the development of the human archetype", so: fresh produce, soaked in dilute bleach then rinsed (the environment is toxic), fish, a staple with each meal, unprocessed proteins, little sugar or salt, no reused oil, one coffee or tea a day, red wine in moderation, no beer, liquor or smoking, few medicines, lots of water, slow meals of one to two hours.

signalThe suit is a frequency

The inmate skin suit is "a very complex frequency"; controlling it lets you change aspects for egress, and it is how The Domain tracks inmates; changing it is dangerous. Reiki questions: "null".

scamsIf you were cheated

Isolate from bad people; add affirmations for justice and the return of the money plus "10 to 25% pain and suffering"; take no other action; stop thinking about it.

Then the war. MM, watching the news in November 2021, asked about WWIII. The reply: "You have the tools." The "M-band noise" had reached "a directed frenzy" aimed at a long war "under specified operational parameters that will not occur"; the slides that could stop it were impossible ("systems / personnel off-line"); the MWI "will evolve according to the pre-programmed event sequence". The Domain risks taking over a body only when the result is strongly probable, as at "Corpus Christi December 1983 (major world-line template cluster realignment. We avoided world war III with the Russians at that time.)" "We do not want a radioactive result... However, the most probable event sequence will be one that includes nuclear detonations", with most of China and the South Pacific likely spared and "damage and dangers to the United States, and their allies". The mRNA injections "are not a vaccine... a preventative measure for the next major attack... it will not work as intended." Then the Commander turned the question round: "Use your skills and answer the following yourself." MM's best case (no war, a replaced US government) was scored "Zero" likelihood by his own reading; his raw, unedited feed described an American provocation and a Russian-Chinese first strike triggered at "85%" readiness, and was called "correct", with Australia "the key unknown". "Will you be able to take over a body and stop a button being pushed?" "We can, but we will not. Not in this instance, and not at this time."

Logged as claims

These are November 2021 predictions made through MM's own senses under prompting, in his words "the raw feed... do not scrub away what does not make sense". Some elements (the "boomerang" bio-weapon claim, the 85% trigger) are unverifiable; the war they describe did not occur on the schedule implied. The archive keeps them because MM published them; the summary reports them without endorsing them.

13 November: the influencers' questions

A citizen's life
All Domain citizens are non-corporeal, "trans-dimensional spirit form", timeless; they "inject consciousness into the Master Universe to experience time"; families and clusters "very similar to your Heavens"; roles by ability (engineers, administrators, historians, social engineers, soldiers, pilots); very long duty cycles, "like commuting"; "Work is play for many members." Vacations are taken in skin suits.
Your own past
Everything is recorded; an IS-BE can freeze and observe any moment. Inmate access is metered by the Mantids but can be bypassed: guided past-life regression, affirmations asking who you were, or asking your assigned Mantid directly. "Never question your intuition."
Fear
To a frightened reader: "We will NEVER abandon our people." Costa Rica: a stable "life raft" region. Help for a family member only on that person's own formal request.
Family as guards?
"Yes. No. Maybe. Probably." Shadow people can be constructs placed in your template by Mantid Primes "to funnel your thoughts and actions into predetermined outcomes"; ask your Mantid handler.
Who wears the suits
A sizable share of inmates in human suits are not human-preferential; the Old Empire had few humans. Other inmate forms: donkeys, dolphins, elephants; "the second most populous skin suit form is monkey."
Is the Old Empire gone?
"At first glance, a group of inmates have taken over the control mechanisms and are trying to develop escape bodies." The Heaven pockets run autonomously as if the Old Empire still ruled; The Domain cannot cross the tunnel. The prison is "in a complex state of disarray with many factions"; the Moon complex is staffed by former Old Empire staff now working for The Domain to keep core systems from collapsing.
DM's intel
"A treasure trove." "Psaigreen", DM's name for the group running the amnesia programming, may be the operators; The Domain wants location, systems, Mantid involvement. "Portal Room 7" is a hub, evidence of "a front door" between the Earth side and the old Old Empire; critical. DM's medieval village: a typical community on an Old Empire planet, its inhabitants "demon-like to us".
The universe
Stars and galaxies exist, "but the distances are an illusion. Time is an illusion." In the Master Universe transport is teleportation that would look like magic (pentagrams, runes, chanting) because thought is not suppressed there; MM's reference is Heinlein's Glory Road.
War, again
Not inevitable: thoughts anchor templates and are in flux; mass belief in war makes war, "the danger of mass media". Current reading: "a strong probability of an abortive military war of great destruction during a very short period, thwarted by an early advanced horror."

26 November: six systems, kits, and a 72-hour day

  • Heavens, restated. Species-dependent (the same species on different planets share one); all use a tunnel of light; they "do not actually exist in the Master Universe as inmates would understand"; they are prison-specific rest-and-learn sub-pockets. Other systems have their own bred caretakers in the Mantid role.
  • Kits. Every volunteer is altered, differently; some were altered in childhood; a "kit" removes suit restrictions and enables lucid dreaming and out-of-body travel with practice.
  • DM's wife. An IS-BE "of extraordinary ability", an elder from communities outside The Domain and the Master Universe, who volunteered for Earth; her mission involves "unmasking / de-tethering the mini-pocket universes (Heavens) from the baseline Reality Universe".
  • Mantids and mice. Mantids manipulate electrical devices, a mouse pointer moving, deleted screenshots reappearing, to "shout pay attention" when a human misses a confirmation.
  • Dreams and days. Dreams are unsorted imagery: thought paths of the non-physical body, memories, self-set signposts, friends' messages; a theme repeating over about three nights marks significant activity. "The human archetype as intended... was designed to operate with a day equal to 72 / 73 hour length"; the inmate suit's "hyper-accelerated mind" forces the 24-hour cycle and shortens the lifespan.

Remember

  • October: the scouts. Look, do not engage; expect the "Join the Domain dream"; DM's island prison is the amnesia complex, running badly.
  • MM's file: compartmentalised by MAJestic, four hardwired alters, a former Old Empire administrator, "my I/O".
  • The control room is inside the Moon. Mantids in the body cooperate; Mantids Prime in Heaven do not.
  • Six systems in a tilted bubble, Alpha Centauri included; hidden pockets everywhere; "many factions".
  • November's war answers are on the record as claims, with MM's own raw feed beside them.
