Early Writings — The Archaeological Record of ΨORM

Metallicman 4 min read · 783 words Original ↗
From the author's GitHub repository MyMetallicMan/SORM--Ontic-Reality-Model- (branch develop, fetched 2026-09-14).

Author: Robert Vannrox
Date Range: 2017–2020
Purpose: To document the raw, unfiltered exploration that preceded the formalization of ΨORM.

A Note to the Reader

These writings are not ΨORM. They are the archaeological record of how ΨORM was excavated.

Between 2017 and 2020, I was exploring ideas that would later coalesce into the Ψ-Ontic Reality Model. At the time, I was not writing a formal framework. I was thinking out loud — rambling, experimenting, and trying to make sense of a reality that did not fit the conventional narratives I had been taught.

These posts reflect that process. They are:

  • Raw — Unedited, unfiltered, and often unfinished.
  • Influenced — Shaped by my experiences, the American media, politics, and the cultural environment of the time.
  • Contradictory — I hit many snags and dead ends. I went back and forth. That is how discovery works.
  • Personal — These are not academic papers. They are the thoughts of a person trying to understand something that defied easy explanation.

What You Will Find

Element Description
Explorations Early attempts to articulate ideas that would later become core ΨORM concepts.
Dead Ends Ideas I pursued and later abandoned. They are part of the process.
Cultural Influence Reflections of American politics, media, and the social environment of the time.
Personal Voice My honest, unfiltered thoughts — including views and beliefs that have since evolved.
Pseudo-Science Some posts venture into areas I would later reject or refine. They are included for historical completeness.

A Warning: This Is Not the Framework

If you are looking for the formal ΨORM framework, please refer to the MASTER-INDEX.md and the sections/ folder. These early writings are:

  • Not vetted — They have not been reviewed or refined.
  • Not consistent — They do not always align with the final framework.
  • Not authoritative — They represent a work in progress.

They are included here for one reason: to show the process. To show that ideas do not emerge fully formed. To show that discovery is messy.

How to Read These Writings

Do Don't
Read them as historical artifacts. Treat them as the final framework.
Look for the seeds of later concepts. Assume every idea survived.
Notice the evolution of thought. Hold me to views I no longer hold.
See the influence of the time and culture. Use them as evidence against the current framework.

A Personal Note

When I wrote these posts, I was a different person. I was shaped by different experiences, different influences, and a different understanding of the world. My beliefs, feelings, and attachments have substantially changed since then.

These posts are ghostly relics of a time long gone. They are not who I am now. They are who I was then — and that is precisely why they are valuable.

Some Context: The Era

Era Influence
2017–2020 The posts were written during a time of political, cultural, and technological upheaval.
American Media The media landscape heavily influenced my thinking at the time.
Personal Experience My MAJestic service, my experiences, and my personal life all shaped the content.
Exploration, Not Conclusion I was exploring — not concluding.

What These Writings Are Not

  • They are not a manifesto.
  • They are not a final statement.
  • They are not a guide to my current beliefs.
  • They are not a reflection of the formal ΨORM framework.

They are, simply, the raw material from which ΨORM was eventually refined.

Final Thought

"The road to discovery is not a straight line. It is a path of wandering, backtracking, and stumbling. These early writings are the footprints left behind during that journey. They are not the destination — they are the map of how I got there."
