The Alien Interview, as MM reads it

The deep dive on parts 8 and 9. The 1947 Roswell transcript that MM says matched his MAJestic training: what the document is, what the officer called Airl said session by session, MM's line-by-line scorecard of what he accepts, what he corrects and what he thinks was tailored for a room full of soldiers, the history dates, and how the crash led to MAJestic and, in 2021, to the direct channel.

AI summary · part 21 of 40 13 min read · 2,932 words 19 sources ↓

Written by an AI, not by Metallicman. This is a plain-language summary of 19 archived articles (mainly the collections The Domain, Type 1 Grey Extraterrestrials). It restates what those sources say and does not add claims of its own. Every part links to its sources at the bottom so you can check the original wording.

In one breath

On 4 July 2021 MM read a PDF called Alien Interview: a 2008 book by Lawrence Spencer built from a package of notes and typed transcripts he says he received from a dying Army nurse, Matilda O'Donnell MacElroy, who in July 1947 had "spoken" telepathically with the one survivor of the Roswell crash, an officer who called herself Airl. MM says the book matched what he had been taught inside MAJestic "and what I was exposed to through entanglement", so he spent the rest of July parsing it sentence by sentence in eight posts, then four more sorted by time-scale. His verdict: genuine, and true, with three big qualifications. Every date is unreliable, because the Greys do not count time the way we do. Some claims were shaped for the audience, a base full of men who had just fought two empires. And a Type-1 Grey "tells you what you want to believe, not necessarily the truth". Two months later, he says, the reading "opened a clear comm bridge" and the Commander began answering questions directly (parts 22 to 29).

What the document is

The package
Spencer's introduction says a letter from Mrs MacElroy arrived on 14 September 2007 with three kinds of paper: her handwritten notes on lined school paper, notes typed on a manual typewriter, and "many pages of typewritten transcriptions of her interview with the alien". She was old, "ready to die", and wanted the experience preserved.
The interviews
Dated sessions from 9 July to 31 July 1947 at the base of the 509th Bomber Squadron. The first days are one-line answers; the long passages come after days of silence and reading. Only the nurse could "hear" Airl; the officers' questions went through her.
The being
"I AM NOT A BIOLOGICAL BEING." The body was a "doll", a synthetic construct with a nervous system tuned to the frequency of the IS-BE wearing it, "much the same way that an actor wears a mask and costume"; the craft was tuned the same way to its crew. She called herself feminine because "I AM A CREATOR. MOTHER. SOURCE."
MM's relation to it
It "pre-dates MAJestic". He had first-hand experience of Type-1 Greys but his own entanglement was with Mantids, "and they view things quite differently". He reprints the PDF, parses the transcript, and leaves out the nurse's marginal notes.

The interview, session by session

24 JulyWho she is

Officer, pilot and engineer of the Domain Expeditionary Force, in this sector "for several thousand years"; last in contact with Earth people around 5,965 BCE, when she learned Sanskrit while investigating a lost Domain base in the Himalayas. Then the first mention of "Old Empire" ships still hidden in the solar system.

25 JulyTime, IS-BEs, the Domain

"Time is simply an arbitrary measurement of the motion of objects through space." Every sentient being is an IS-BE, an immortal spiritual being. The Domain: a "space opera civilization" that claims "one-fourth of the entire physical universe" and treats Earth as property.

26 JulyThe prison

Since 1150 AD, with the Old Empire fleet gone, science has revived. But the "remote control hypnosis operation" remains: Earth is a prison, its population an "unusual combination of inmates", kept behind "electronic force screens" by "ignorance, superstition, and war".

26 to 28 JulyHistory and biology

A dated chronology from 208,000 BCE to 1307 AD, then her own "many billions of years ago" career at the Arcadia Regeneration Company, engineering life forms for planets of different star types.

29 JulyScience and the spirit

"Science is the religion of matter." No science that omits "the spiritual spark" can save Earth; the problems to solve are "social and humanitarian". The Domain never developed amnesia technology and wants Earth kept habitable, not its people.

31 JulyLimits and departure

"Security and protocol prevent me from revealing any but the broadest, general statements." The force screen covers "this end of the galaxy, at least"; memory "cannot be permanently erased"; the Moon and asteroid belt are Domain bases; "the IS-BEs of Earth are here forever, until they can break the amnesia cycle".

MM's scorecard

Most lines get the same stamp, "No problem with this." The interesting ones are where he adds, corrects, or explains why Airl said it that way.

Genuine
"The more that I parse this document, the clearer it is (to me) that it is genuine. It is exactly what it says it is." By part 5: "Yes, I can honestly say. Everything herein is accurate and is exactly what has occurred." He believes both the back-story and the transcripts, and says "there is no reason for the Type-1 grey extraterrestrial to lie. None."
Time and dates
His one blanket rejection. "Humans think of time as shared and linear. The type-1 greys think of time as circular and repeating", a "corkscrew" through world-lines, "WAY beyond the concepts of anyone in Roswell at that time". So "all dates and time... are in error, and can be ignored", or treated as unresolved. "Trillions" he reads as "an unfathomably long period of time". The claim that nearby suns have burned for 200 trillion years he flags as simply "Not true".
The crash
Airl blamed "an electrical discharge from the atmosphere". MM: "I was told that radar technology developed by the Germans during World War II were being tested in New Mexico, and they are what brought the vehicle down." Within two years, he says, the Domain worked this out and changed its control systems.
Size of the Domain
"One-fourth of the entire physical universe" gets a shrug: "It could be. Or it could not." His own understanding: about a quarter of our galaxy, with the solar system "somewhere off to the side".
Home planet
The officers' request to point to a home star was "an impossible question": a non-physical species that builds bodies for use in physical space has no single home planet.
Universes
Airl said each IS-BE's home universe ceases to exist when it leaves. MM: "This understanding differs from mine. But only slightly." He had assumed bubbles were permanent; a soul, he thinks, anchors them.
Small additions
The "hypnotic commands given between lifetimes" are, in his vocabulary, the pre-birth template (part 4). Bases "on the moon and in the asteroid belt" match his Hollow Moon article. The force screen covers more than Earth: "there are at least five sentience nurseries or recovering prison planets in this neighborhood." Destroying the amnesia machines will stop new erasures but "will not be able to recover previously lost memories".
Big reactions
On Airl's account of engineering life for planets billions of years ago: "Could the Domain be the same thing as the progenitors? Mind BLOWN!" On humans: "Humans DID NOT evolve on Earth. Nor did they evolve from primates." On the mind-control passages: they "ended up spawning American covert effort in the Monarch Program".

What it settled for him

The parsing changed MM's account of his own work. "It is crystal clear to me now, that my role was, and still is, in the rehabilitation aspects of moving the Earth from a Hellish Prison Planet to that of a sentience nursery." The key points he repeats at the end of every part: Earth "is no longer a prison planet", it "is now a sentience nursery"; an abduction "is actually a good thing"; the Domain's Greys are "service-for-others"; and "MAJestic is changing everything, hand in hand with the Type-1 greys". He also reads his own writing back into it: "Look at the dates on my articles, and look at what I covered." The document, he says, "helped to clarify elements and aspects of my role that were blurred and obscured from me. To that I am eternally grateful."

The history, in four time-scales

Because Airl "jumps all over", MM re-sorted her statements into four buckets and gave each its own article.

The parts that became the model

  • The prison mechanism (part 8's spine): a force screen that detects departing IS-BEs at death, an electroshock of "billions of volts" that erases memory and identity, hypnotic commands to "return to the light" and "report back to Earth as if on a secret mission", and the list of who was dumped here: "the vicious, perverts, the unproductive, political prisoners, free thinkers, artists, geniuses, inventors, skilled managers". MM: "I have no problem with this."
  • The trap that feeds on struggle: traps use an "electronic wave of beauty" to attract an IS-BE and are powered by its own thought energy when it fights back. "There are no saints in this universe."
  • Biology as engineering: life forms designed for "Star Type 3, Class C" or "Sun Type 12, Class 7" planets; a "trigger" of "aesthetic pain" that draws an IS-BE into a flesh body; reproduction as a programmed impulse. MM's gloss: "Evolution does not occur naturally. It requires a consciousness to make it happen."
  • The counter-offensive of ideas: the Vedic hymns brought 8,200 years ago by an unauthorised crew "as an innocent diversion"; Buddha, Laozi and Zoroaster descended from them; later attempts to teach "everyone is an IS-BE" captured by Old Empire priests. MM: "The philosophy of the Vedic verses are what is important. Not the content."
  • Boredom and games: "One could say that the entire physical universe is made of unadmired illusions", and games solve boredom. MM: "Reincarnation as a mortal being solves the problem of boredom", and he admits "dim, curious memories of a something else" when Airl describes universes merging.
  • The plan, as MM counts it from her last words ("a four step plan", though he lists three): break the amnesia cycle, conquer the electronic traps, free yourselves from this region. On her line that humanity "must cooperate", he adds: "Humanity must cooperate with the Domain. Which is probably why MAJestic was established."

From the crash to MAJestic, and to 2021

Then the loop closed. On 16 September 2021, two months after the parsing, MM reports that an officer of the Domain "opened a direct EBP channel" and the question-and-answer format that fills parts 22 to 29 began. His 2022 account of how that channel works puts the reading in sequence: MAJestic gave him the vocabulary, the writing came next, then the reality narratives, "then Alien Interview, then the direct channel". The first thing he asked readers to do with it was practical: add "lines of code" to their affirmation campaigns in support of the 3,000-strong Lost Battalion the interview describes, such as "I volunteer and support The Domain in their mission to rehabilitate the IS-BEs on Earth."

Read with care

The book is a third party's; its provenance rests on Spencer's account of a package from a woman who was dying. MM's endorsement is personal and total ("it reaffirms, from a secondary source, the validity of my experiences"), but he also says the Grey warning applies to it: "They tell you what you want to believe, not necessarily the truth." Several of Airl's claims were later revised by the Commander in MM's own sessions: the prison is a spawned pocket universe the Domain entered unaware, the Old Empire "does not exist any more" as such, and the whole time-and-history framing is "not the way the true universe works" (parts 22 to 29). Where this part quotes numbers, they are Airl's; MM's own instruction is to ignore them.

Remember

  • A 1947 transcript, published 2008, read by MM on 4 July 2021 and parsed in twelve posts that month.
  • Verdict: genuine and true; dates unreliable; some framing chosen for a military audience.
  • Corrections: radar not lightning; a quarter of the galaxy, not the universe; no single home planet.
  • What it gave the model: IS-BEs, doll bodies, the force screen, the amnesia cycle, the untouchables, the Lost Battalion.
  • What it gave MM: a name for his job, and two months later, the channel.
