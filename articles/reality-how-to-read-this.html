How to read all this: claims, evidence and open questions

The honest notes. What kind of claim each part of the archive is, what could be checked and how, where the sources disagree with each other, and what MM himself says about proof. Plus a reading map for the whole archive.

AI summary · part 17 of 17 7 min read · 1,561 words 19 sources ↓

Written by an AI, not by Metallicman. This is a plain-language summary of 19 archived articles (mainly the collections For New Visitors – Read This First, The Grey Web, Questions and Answers). It restates what those sources say and does not add claims of its own. Every part links to its sources at the bottom so you can check the original wording.

In one breath

Nothing in this archive comes with documents. MM says so, and says the reader "is free to believe whatever they want". What the archive does offer is a large, internally fairly consistent model, told from three kinds of source, with a handful of dated predictions and one formal document (ΨORM) that names tests. This part sorts the material by what kind of claim it is, lists where the archive disagrees with itself, and gives you a map for reading the originals.

Five kinds of statement

What MM says about proof

  • He offers none. "Why don't you offer PROOF? ... ten years ago did you go grocery shopping and buy some milk or eggs? Good. Now, you prove it to me."
  • He asks for discernment. His "army of fake disclosures" article says his own story "seems just as far fetched" as the ones he mocks, and that the difference must be found by the reader: "Discernment. An underappreciated fundamental."
  • He admits the channel is noisy. Answers are "flavored" by his own understanding; questions built on false premises get answers wrong in the same direction; garbles and redactions are marked; some things he "cannot elaborate upon"; his remote viewing "was pretty much unsuccessful".
  • He disowns his own model where it suits. The consciousness-migration picture is "a teaching crutch"; the film-reel is "an MM construction to help the reader"; the six-dimension scheme is "my best guess... I have no clue as to whether it is accurate". He says he might be "played the fool" by his probes, but thinks the effort involved makes that unlikely.
  • He wants the format to look like noise. The blog is deliberately padded with "topical social filler" to disguise "the true and actual nature of the site" and to keep bots and trolls away.

Where the archive disagrees with itself

These are not gotchas. Most are the difference between MM's MAJestic-era writing (2018 to 2020) and the Domain material (2021 on), or between two answers years apart. They matter because a reader will meet both versions.

What the Greys are
2019: a service-to-self caretaker species with a harvest of its own in mind, "worker bees" for an older species. 2021 on: The Domain's own officers (Airl), allies in the rescue. The 2019 article also says they never touch a soul without pre-birth approval. [11]
Prison or nursery
MAJestic-era: Earth is a "sentience nursery" tended by benefactors. Domain-era: Earth is a prison built by the Old Empire. MM's bridge article says it was a prison and became a nursery when The Domain took over; the Commander's later answer is that the nursery function is being built and is "around 23 to 25%" done. [8, 11]
How many prison systems
MM from MAJestic: Earth "plus perhaps four other solar systems". The Commander: six, "five plus one", including Alpha Centauri. [8]
Apollo
April 2022: the radiation problem was solved by engineering, so the flights were feasible. December 2023: the craft went, orbited and landed, but "no men" walked; the footage was fiction. [8]
The word Anunnaki
2022: Old Empire residents working the region. 2023: originally Domain task teams, later applied to other visitors. [8]
The tunnel of light
Through 2022: fully operating, "the primary amnesia mechanism". June 2024: "adjusted, culled, deactivated, rendered impotent". [6]
Does consciousness move, or does the bubble change
MM's own belief, stated in 2018: the bubble itself changes and "migration" is a teaching crutch. His later how-to articles use migration throughout. The Commander in 2023: world-lines are static states you "resonate" with, "like a key in a lock". [2]
Who runs the lessons
MAJestic-era: the benefactors guide human evolution toward a purer state. Domain-era: the Mantid Primes' curriculum is corrupt and The Domain has cut the general-population Mantids loose from it. Both are in the archive without a retraction. [7]
MM's own origin
He is "not human" but a hybrid; he is a construct built from the components of the prison administrator Mades Escapleon; he is "Domain" but not lost battalion. These are given as compatible, in different sessions. [3, 9]

Dated forecasts

The archive contains predictions with dates. The summaries do not rule on any of them; they are listed so a reader can look them up. check against events after the date

Dec 2021 · injections
The Commander: mRNA injections are "not a vaccine" but a population-control platform; deaths from side effects rising through 2022 and 2023, "obvious by late 2023", peaking in 2024, unless counter-programs intervene. [part 1 of the comm]
Mar 2022 · World War III
"Most future historians will attribute the start of World War III... as 2008" (the banking collapse and the Bucharest NATO summit); others 2014. [part 8]
Apr 2022 · China
China "will not initiate any overt kinetic military action preemptively" unless facing a direct threat; the threat vector "is planned for implementation before the end of 2025"; China "will seize Taiwan in a manner that is intended to prevent third-parties from involvement". [part 9]
Aug 2022 · "medium bads"
Hundreds of millions of deaths from war and "hidden bio-wars" had become "highly probable"; China's only way to survive would be a joint first strike with Russia; "massive die-offs" in the United States mostly from starvation, illness and lack of care. [part 11]
Mar 2022 · geology
"The earth sphere is expected to enter a period of geologic upheaval in mid 2030s into the 2040s", timing dependent on mass thought. [part 8]
Oct 2023 · the prison
Non-physical suit changes to be completed "within the next twenty earth years"; the overall conversion "23 to 25% in completion". [part 13]
Sep 2021 · the master template
MM: the shift he sensed will show as easier lucid dreaming, faster affirmation results, more slides and PSI, longer lifespans. [MM]
2019 to 2021 · doom that will not happen
MM: California will not slide into the sea; the ice caps will not melt or a new ice age start; no fake alien invasion is planned. [FAQ, part 10 of the comm]

What could be examined without a channel

  1. ΨORM's predictions. The five EEG-based tests are written in ordinary neuroscience language and could be attempted by any lab with EEG equipment. Nothing in the archive says they have been. [14]
  2. Public records. MM's naval aviation candidacy, his prison record in Arkansas and his engineering career are the kind of facts that exist in records, but he publishes none of them and uses a pseudonym.
  3. The objects. The OOPARTs are physical and some are in museums or private hands; their dating is disputed in the ordinary literature. [15]
  4. Published texts. Alien Interview, Newton's books, the Titor transcripts and the CIA's Gateway assessment are all public; what the archive adds is interpretation.
  5. The forecasts. See the table above.
