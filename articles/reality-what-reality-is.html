What reality is, according to this material

The big picture in one page: you are an immortal consciousness inside an engineered body, inside a pocket universe built as a prison, moving through frozen world-lines that you experience as time.

AI summary · part 1 of 40 6 min read · 1,356 words 12 sources ↓

Written by an AI, not by Metallicman. This is a plain-language summary of 12 archived articles (mainly the collections The Domain, MAJestic Disclosure, Terms and Conventions, For New Visitors – Read This First, World-Line Travel Index). It restates what those sources say and does not add claims of its own. Every part links to its sources at the bottom so you can check the original wording.

In one breath

You are not your body. You are an immortal consciousness (the material calls it an IS-BE) living inside an engineered body, inside a small pocket universe that was built as a prison. Reality is not one shared world: it is a stack of frozen "world-lines" that your consciousness moves through, several times a second, and that movement is what you feel as time. Your thoughts choose which lines you pass through. When the body dies you lose nothing, but the local system steers you into a "tunnel of light" that erases your memory and sends you back for another life. A military organisation called The Domain says it now runs the place and is slowly taking the prison apart.

This archive holds about 430 articles by Metallicman (MM), a retired member of a secret US program called MAJestic, plus messages he says he relays from an extraterrestrial commander, plus guest writers. The articles are long, scattered and written in MM's own slang. This part gives you the whole model on one page. The parts that follow take each piece apart slowly, with drawings.

The model on one map

Everything in the archive fits on this map. Read it from the outside in.

Ten ideas that carry everything else

  1. You are an IS-BE: an immortal spiritual being. The term comes from the book Alien Interview (a 1947 interview transcript that MM treats as a mostly reliable secondary source). An IS-BE cannot be destroyed; it can only be confined, split, or made to forget. claim
  2. Reality is personal. Each consciousness lives in its own bubble of reality. The other people you see are "quantum shadows": other consciousnesses on nearby world-lines, which is why everyone remembers things slightly differently.
  3. Time is movement, not a river. World-lines are frozen snapshots. Your consciousness flickers across them, about four per second when you are alert, and the sequence feels like time. Nothing "flows"; you move.
  4. Thoughts steer. Thoughts are the only thing a consciousness can create, and they act like a magnet that pulls you toward the world-lines that match them. Spoken, written intention (an "affirmation campaign") is the practical tool for steering on purpose.
  5. You were born on a template. Before birth, your life path was mapped out (a "pre-birth world-line template") with fixed signposts. Most people never leave it. Leaving it is possible, but it takes work and has side effects.
  6. The body is engineered. The human body here is an "inmate suit": only about a fifth to a third of your consciousness fits inside it, memory access is blocked, and the ability to manifest by thought is suppressed. The rest of you stays outside.
  7. Death changes nothing about you. Memories are not stored in the brain, so you keep them all when you leave the body. What you then meet is a guided process (loved ones, a light, a tunnel) that ends in a memory wipe and a new life. The material says you can refuse it.
  8. This region is a prison built by the "Old Empire". A fallen galactic power conquered this area, built a containment field around several solar systems, and used amnesia and endless reincarnation to hold dangerous or unwanted beings. The original idea was rehabilitation; over time it became a treadmill with no exit.
  9. The Domain now administers it. A large military-style organisation from outside says it conquered the Old Empire long ago, lost a battalion of its own people inside the field, and since 2021 has been talking through MM's implant (the EBP) to recover them and to reform the prison. Its one firm rule: it will not help anyone at death unless that person calls for it.
  10. Earth is also a nursery. MM's older MAJestic-era writing describes Earth as a "sentience nursery": humans are being cultivated toward a stable soul type, and what you become depends on whether your nature is service-to-others or service-to-self. The 2021 writing says the nursery and the prison are the same place seen from two sides.

Who is who

youIS-BE / consciousness

The immortal being. Made of "quanta" organised into clusters (garbons) joined by paths (swales). A "soul" is the larger structure; a "consciousness" is the part of it plugged into a body.

the bodyInmate suit

The engineered human body. Also called a skin-suit. Other modified container species exist (horses, elephants, dolphins, llamas).

your guideMantid (guardian angel)

A multi-dimensional insect-like species; each human has one. They plan life templates, run the "lessons", and meet you at death. "Mantid Primes" are their senior layer in Heaven.

the caretakersType-1 Greys

The small, hive-minded species that has treaties with the US. They tend the nursery, cull what does not fit, and in the 1947 interview describe themselves as The Domain's officers.

the administratorsThe Domain

A vast, disciplined organisation from the main universe. Conquered the Old Empire, holds this sector, speaks through the "Domain Commander".

the buildersThe Old Empire

The former rulers of this region who built the prison. "Does not exist any more" as an empire; its machinery and some of its escaped leaders still operate inside the bubble.

the human programMAJestic

The US special-access program that handled extraterrestrial contact. MM was recruited in 1981, implanted, used as a "dimensional anchor", and retired in 2006.

the authorMetallicman (MM)

Aerospace engineer, ex naval aviation candidate, MAJestic member, later exiled to China. Writes the blog; since 2021 relays Q&A sessions with the Commander.

Where the material comes from

Three streams feed the archive, and it helps to know which one you are reading at any moment.

How to treat all this

None of it is proven in the ordinary sense, and MM says so himself: "the reader is free to believe whatever they want". The summaries do not argue for or against it. They explain what the material says, keep the three streams labelled, mark dated predictions as predictions, and point you to the original wording. Part 17 lists what could be checked and where the sources disagree with each other.

Reading order

The parts build on each other. Parts 2 to 7 explain the mechanics of reality and of your own life and death. Parts 8 to 11 explain the history and the players. Parts 12 to 15 cover technology, practices, the formal model and the mysteries. Parts 16 and 17 are the glossary and the honest notes on evidence.
