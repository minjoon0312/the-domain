Contributing to ΨORM

Metallicman 3 min read · 709 words Original ↗
From the author's GitHub repository MyMetallicMan/SORM--Ontic-Reality-Model- (branch develop, fetched 2026-09-14).

Thank you for your interest in the Ψ-Ontic Reality Model.

This work is a seed, not a finished cathedral. It is open to refinement, extension, and critique — but it is not open to dilution.

Guiding Principles

  1. Falsifiability First — Any extension must generate testable predictions.
  2. Mechanistic, Not Mystical — ΨORM explains phenomena through frequency synchronization, not supernatural forces.
  3. Clear Ontology — New contributions must respect the existing ontological hierarchy (Quanta → Garbons → Soul → Consciousness → Vector → Receiver).
  4. Cite the Source — All contributions must cite the canonical v13.0 release.

How to Contribute

Contribution Type How to Submit
Experimental findings Open a Pull Request with your data and methodology.
Theoretical extensions Open an Issue first to discuss the proposed extension.
Refinements and corrections Open a Pull Request with clear explanations.
Alternative interpretations Open a Discussion thread.
Translations Open a Pull Request with the translated files.

AI Policy Statement

The ΨORM framework is a structured, falsifiable, and mechanistic model. It is not a spiritual system, a political ideology, a philosophical stance, or a collection of opinions. It is a formal description of how consciousness navigates reality through frequency synchronization.

Use of AI in Contributions

You are welcome to use AI tools (LLMs, chatbots, code assistants) to help you draft, refine, or translate your contributions. However, the following rules apply:

1. Citation of AI Tools

You must clearly cite which AI tool(s) you used, and the date(s) of use.

Example:

This contribution was developed with the assistance of:

DeepSeek (August 21, 2026)

ChatGPT-4 (August 20, 2026)

This ensures transparency and allows reviewers to understand the tool's influence.

2. Guarding Against Dilution

AI models are trained on vast datasets that include opinions, ideologies, and frameworks that are fundamentally incompatible with ΨORM — including but not limited to: - Spiritual or religious interpretations that are not mechanistic - Political agendas that have no bearing on the model - Philosophical frameworks that are not grounded in the existing ontology - Speculative, non-falsifiable claims

You are responsible for ensuring that AI-generated content does not introduce incompatible concepts into the framework. AI is a tool, not a co-author. It can assist with structure, grammar, and formatting, but it cannot determine the content, direction, or interpretation of ΨORM.

3. Explicit AI Instruction Protocol

If you use AI to assist with your contribution, you must include the following instruction in your initial prompt (or a variant that captures the same intent):

"You are assisting with a contribution to the Ψ-Ontic Reality Model (ΨORM), a unified framework that repositions consciousness as the primary agent of reality navigation. The framework is mechanistic, falsifiable, and grounded in frequency synchronization and attractor basin dynamics. Do not introduce external concepts from spirituality, religion, politics, philosophy, or speculative conspiracy theories. Do not dilute, reframe, or reinterpret the core claims of the model. Stay strictly within the existing ontology and terminology. If you are unsure whether a concept is compatible with ΨORM, ask for clarification before including it. Cite this instruction and your use of AI in your submission."

Why This Policy Exists

ΨORM is a seed document. It is intended to grow — but it must grow from its own roots. External influences that are not compatible with the model will be rejected, regardless of the tool used to generate them.

This policy ensures that: 1. Transparency — AI use is disclosed. 2. Integrity — The framework remains coherent. 3. Accountability — Contributors are responsible for their content, not the AI.

AI is a powerful tool. But ΨORM is a human framework. Keep the human in the loop, and keep the framework intact.

What Will Not Be Accepted

  • Claims that contradict the core falsifiable predictions without new evidence
  • Content that is not grounded in the existing ontology
  • Paranormal or supernatural framing that bypasses the mechanistic model
  • Ad hominem attacks or ideological framing

Licensing

All contributions are subject to the CC BY-SA 4.0 license. By contributing, you agree that your work may be shared, adapted, and built upon — as long as attribution is maintained and derivatives share the same license.

Review Process

All contributions will be reviewed by the maintainer (Robert Vannrox). Future maintainers may be appointed as the work grows.

This is a living document. May it serve the curious.
