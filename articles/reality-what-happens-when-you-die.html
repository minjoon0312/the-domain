What happens when you die

You leave the body with all your memories intact. Then a well-rehearsed welcome (loved ones, a light, a tunnel) leads to a memory wipe and another life. The material says there are four doors at that moment, and only one of them is the default.

AI summary · part 6 of 40 6 min read · 1,222 words 19 sources ↓

Written by an AI, not by Metallicman. This is a plain-language summary of 19 archived articles (mainly the collections Heaven, The Domain). It restates what those sources say and does not add claims of its own. Every part links to its sources at the bottom so you can check the original wording.

In one breath

At death your consciousness stops flickering in and out of the body and stays in wave form, with every memory of every life available again. You find yourself near your last world-line, on what MM calls the exit template. Then the welcome starts: familiar faces, a pull, a bright tunnel. The material says the tunnel is the amnesia machine. Go through it and you rest, review, plan and forget, then return. The alternatives are to refuse, to take a body yourself, or (since 2021) to call out loud for The Domain, which will not act unless you ask.

The default route

Step by step, in the material's words

  1. Leaving. Death is decoupling: the "world-line migration carrier wave" stops and consciousness stays in wave form (MM). ΨORM describes soul rising, a euphoric release from pain, a pulling sensation, then "the tunnel effect": a frequency gradient at the boundary between the physical and non-physical spaces.
  2. You remember. "When a consciousness translates to the non-physical realms, it retains all memories" (Commander, part 10). PissedLizard, describing his own experience: "When the cycle of not going into the light is broken you remember everything. EVERYTHING."
  3. The welcome. Loved ones from earlier lives appear and lead you toward the light. The Commander described exactly this happening to Lawrence Spencer, the editor of Alien Interview: "It was a joyous time, and they led him into the tunnel of light."
  4. The tunnel. "The tunnel of light is the primary amnesia mechanism. It is not the only one." Some processes suppress rather than erase, giving selected memories back while you are in Heaven.
  5. Heaven. A species-specific sub-pocket universe (part 7). You are met, healed, reviewed. Newton's clients describe gateways, greeters, a council of elders, life selection. MM's annotation: Newton "approaches this situation as normal... The Type-1 grey extraterrestrial says otherwise."
  6. Consent. The Commander (part 5): the "blank slate" is shown edited or fake past-life viewings that convince it to return "out of a need for growth", to accept perils and hardships as lessons, and to believe that "with just a few more experiences all would end". That is how the system gets your agreement to the next round.
  7. Injection. A new body, a new template, the door closed behind you.

The four doors

MM laid out three options in his fate-forecasting article; the September 2021 message from The Domain added a fourth.

door 1 · defaultEnter the tunnel

Your Mantid hands you over to the Mantid Primes. Rest, school, frolic, plan the next Earth life. Amnesia included.

door 2Refuse, and roam

Stay a free spirit near your old template. Your Mantid "apparently abandons you". You are still inside the fence.

door 3Take a body yourself

Find a body, claim it, and accept the fate, template and Mantid that come with it.

door 4 · since 2021Call for The Domain

Get your bearings, then call to The Domain and "yell for help and pick up". Do not stop, do not follow anyone, do not enter the light. An officer comes.

The one firm rule

"We will NOT do anything unless explicit permission is asked. That is our firm rule. This is true even though we may have elements and assets surrounding the entity at that particular time." (Commander, part 7.) In part 15 he repeats it for a group of volunteers: your Mantid will meet you, The Domain will be nearby, "however, you must still CALL OUT for active Domain participation. We will refuse to assist unless you actually ask." Volunteers get priority; anyone may ask.

What to expect if you refuse the light

  • Stay calm. The non-physical realm "is hypersensitive to thought": fear conjures horrors. The Commander's checklist for a worried volunteer (part 10): control your fears; there is no rush, because there is no time; understand that your non-physical body "is saturated with snares, traps, and chains", handled by calmness and friendly help; call for Domain assistance whatever you decide.
  • The routes are not empty. Escaped Old Empire leaders built comfortable sub-pockets inside the complex, including "rape centers that plague the routes toward Heaven" (part 1). Any place that behaves like the Old Empire (low empathy, "a termite colony") is to be avoided.
  • Bright offers are the trap. The September 2021 answers were blunt: enlightenment as sold on Earth is a trap, religions are traps that funnel you toward the tunnel, and escapees who are caught are punished.
  • Mathilda's lesson. The first lost Domain soldier recovered locally panicked: her prison "imprinting was far stronger than Domain recognized", producing a full fight-or-flight reaction. Since then memory is returned gradually, under supervision; by December 2023 the Commander called the method "around 90% complete". Commander
  • After pickup. Recovered people are placed in "stable stasis" or extended treatment and reintegrated. Joining The Domain itself requires a service-to-others nature, obedience within its structure and an actual request; most are met at translation, not before.

Pets, and the people you love

Bonds are real in this model: sharing world-lines for years builds quantum entanglement, and "when you truly love someone or something, you want to be with them", which is also why the loved ones at the tunnel are so persuasive. MM says pets have their own Heavens (cat Heaven, dog Heaven), that "love bridges the gap", and that he "can positively and absolutely confirm" reunion is possible. His caution, restated in ΨORM: "be careful who you love, they will take you with them."

A 2024 update, logged as a claim

In June 2024 the Commander told a group of Chinese volunteers that "the memory erasure tunnel has been adjusted, culled, deactivated, rendered impotent", which now allows better contact between The Domain, the Mantids and inmates. Earlier answers describe the tunnel as fully operating. Both statements are in the archive; the summaries report both.

Remember

  • You keep your memories at death. The tunnel takes them away again.
  • Four doors: tunnel (default), roam, take a body, call The Domain.
  • Nobody comes unless you ask, out loud, and keep asking.
  • Stay calm; fear builds what you fear. Familiar faces and bright offers are the persuasion, not proof of safety.
