XV.1 Overview

The ΨORM framework offers a unified account of phenomena that are traditionally treated as separate mysteries. This section provides a comparative analysis, showing how ΨORM addresses the "Great Mysteries" more coherently than existing frameworks.

It also includes a comprehensive table comparing the explanatory power of ΨORM against other major models of reality.

XV.2 The Great Mysteries

Mystery Traditional Frameworks ΨORM Account
The Hard Problem of Consciousness Materialism cannot explain subjective experience. Consciousness is primary; matter is the interface.
The Quantum Measurement Problem Collapse is unexplained. Consciousness navigates branches through frequency synchronization.
The Incompatibility of GR and QM Unresolved. Spacetime and time are emergent; not fundamental.
Quantum Non-Locality Anomalous. Reality is a non-local configuration space.
Death and Continuity Materialism says death is the end. The soul persists; death is decoupling from a biological receiver.
NDEs and Past-Life Memories Treated as anomalies. The non-physical state is a real region of the configuration space.
The Mandela Effect Treated as memory errors. Group vector convergence; bleed-over and bleed-through.
ESP and Anomalous Phenomena Treated as paranormal. Natural consequences of altered duty cycles.

XV.3 Why ΨORM Offers a More Coherent Account

Factor Why ΨORM Succeeds
Unified Ontology All phenomena are explained by the same set of principles.
Falsifiability ΨORM generates specific, testable predictions.
Mechanistic Explanation ΨORM provides mechanisms (frequency synchronization, attractor basins) rather than metaphors.
Consistency with Physics ΨORM is grounded in existing quantum mechanical structures.
