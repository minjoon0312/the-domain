The first month of the channel: September 2021

The deep dive on parts 6, 8 and 9. How the direct line to the Commander opened on 16 September 2021, what the first message said, the call for questions, the rescue-mission "lines of code", the two big reader Q&A sessions of 18 and 24 September, the engineering of the inmate body, and the geode picture of the prison, session by session.

AI summary · part 22 of 40 16 min read · 3,463 words 8 sources ↓

Written by an AI, not by Metallicman. This is a plain-language summary of 8 archived articles (mainly the collections The Domain). It restates what those sources say and does not add claims of its own. Every part links to its sources at the bottom so you can check the original wording.

In one breath

On the morning of 16 September 2021 MM sat down to write what he knew about the Lost Battalion, went blank, paced, got goosebumps, and then, he says, "an officer of The Domain opened up a channel and talked to me directly". Between 9:45 and 10:39 he asked the questions he was prompted to ask and wrote down the answers. The next day he asked readers for questions. Within ten days he had published two long answer sets, a recruiting call with affirmation "code" for volunteers, and a description of the inmate body; by 6 October he had drawn the whole prison as a geode. This part walks through that month in order, quoting what was said, and marks where MM himself says the transmission was garbled or the numbers rough.

16 September: how the channel works

The first exchange is short, and it set the agenda for everything after. On shutting down the amnesia machinery: "The Domain has established a task team... working with MAJestic, as well as a number of other earth organizations." Earth is "a battlefield of all sorts of traps and snares. Many of which are independent, individual, self-autonomous in nature." The battalion is "located and tracked" but "most of the battalion are still imprisoned". "Most of the equipment and systems are in the non-physical realms. Any efforts towards directed thought will be most helpful." On timing: "From the start it was anticipated that the project can begin in 5,000 years. This date has been advanced, and is in process now. We do not have a realistic time-table for completion... It will take many human life-times." On who goes first: "We sort by sentience." Domain members are "our most important asset... They hold the keys to unlock the fences." Other species held here: "dolphins, and horses." To MM: "You are just doing great. Follow your intuition." To readers: "do not believe in absolute solutions, as it is your thoughts that modify your reality." Sign-off: "Message sent. Do not worry too much."

17 and 18 September: the call-out and the rescue mission

MM posted a request for questions with a warning: "People! this is your first, maybe ONLY chance, to ask questions to an extraterrestrial." Questions had to fit The Domain's declared interests on Earth: free the 3,000 of the Lost Battalion; control or destroy the amnesia machinery; destroy traps and snares; keep Earth from nuclear, biological or climatic ruin; police against more "consciousness dumping"; rehabilitate and sort inmates so the good ones can be freed. Releasing everyone at once, he was told, would be catastrophic, because "very bad" consciousnesses are here too. The same day he published the rescue mission: a set of "lines of code" for readers' affirmation campaigns, offered as "suppressive fire" to anchor world-line clusters in the soldiers' favour, such as "I volunteer and support The Domain in their mission to rehabilitate the IS-BEs on Earth", "Any and all members of the Lost Battalion are no longer imprisoned", and "I, as an IS-BE, have recovered all of my memories." No rewards were promised. The article also carries the long Alien Interview excerpt on the battalion: the Himalayan base of about 3,000 wiped out by a hidden Old Empire base on Mars, the Archduke officer, the amnesia mechanism (part 21).

18 September questions: the first answer set

MM collected questions over the third week of September and worked through them "in fits and starts", sometimes strong, sometimes weak. The Commander, he says, "is not at all comfortable with the system I set up"; it prefers "they speak, we respond". High amplitude left MM shaking and dehydrated, and he asked for it to be turned down. The answers, grouped:

Enlightenment
Does not free anyone from the machinery at death. It is "part of the Old Empire system of conditioned control... another path that leads one into the tunnel of light."
Lucid dreamers
"Never" attack a trap alone. Mark it, notify The Domain. A lucid dreamer is "a mission-critical asset" to be briefed on a target and "kept dormant until used" in coordination with Domain forces. Locations are need-to-know; a species MM does not know provides technical help.
Frequencies and biology
"Groupings or dances of frequencies" act as keys; The Domain uses them with "electromagnetic cannon in the non-physical realms". Human and animal DNA and mRNA were modified to assist imprisonment; correcting the biology "to the universal norm" makes many amnesia systems fail. Worth studying: biological magnetism, high-voltage electromagnetic frequencies, DNA and mRNA.
The 3,000
Scattered worldwide, mostly in human bodies, in "general population", not holding cells (those are for officers); isolated, poor, frightened; thrown straight back into bodies "without even resting in Heaven"; "military caliber", their strengths used against them like a Chinese finger trap. You cannot recognise one unless told; take no action unless directed.
The tunnel
The fullest statement in the archive; see the box below.
Intention alone?
"The universe is malleable to a point." Manipulating it is a skill, and "the major problem with the amnesia fence" is that you keep forgetting the skills you acquired.
A higher power?
"The Domain is the highest power. The Domain does not report to or serve under any other power." Later in the same set, more modestly: "the highest authority in this region".
Religions
Yes, Old Empire traps, built to erase the idea that "IS-BEs are themselves God" and to instil fear of non-compliance. Does any offer escape? "Null."
The corrupt elite
Today's systems "derive from the Old Empire" and are staffed by beings who reincarnate into power on purpose; escaped Old Empire wardens "rigged the game" to keep memories through "special keys". When threatened they die and flee through the tunnel. "Technology is not evil."
Machinery on Earth
Some autonomous physical systems remain, "expertly concealed". Last physical destruction: Tunguska, 1908, an Old Empire logistics facility under a force shield, believed at the time to be the last. The Domain then tried to stabilise Europe through the Archduke operation, not knowing a full base still existed.
Why 5,000 years became now
Unexpectedly, inmate governments wanted to work with The Domain (for power and technology); leadership approved resources; "a major colony set up on the Moon"; battalion members were collected and EBP-implanted ("unruly to the extreme"). Other secret organisations exist in Russia, India, China, Bangladesh, South America and Africa. Recruiting the general population was scheduled for 2021, forty years after MM joined MAJestic.
Bad leaders
"You are in a Prison Environment." Domain agents now occupy the bodies of key individuals who "will not push the red button"; no repeat of WWI, though a smaller localised event may occur.
Authorised contacts
MM and his cell are the only MAJestic-authorised direct contacts; no individual channel exists "without a physical EBP (you will know)"; channels are for assignments and intel, "nothing else"; "I am aware of every communication effort with the inmates in this region."
Snark
What powers the traps? "The standard local power sources that all the traps use. Duh!" (MM: "he actually went Duh! with an overlay of Homer Simpson.") And: "Humans are IS-BEs. IS-BEs never die."

The tunnel of light, in the Commander's words

"If you enter the tunnel of light you will go straight into the reprogramming machinery. There you will find yourself in Heaven, which is a special universe constructed for this Prison Planet realm." Prior prison-life memories are "carefully measured out" by "a guide (a warden) to give the illusion of control"; then "schools", a new "mission", re-injection, "and subsequently lose all memories and start out all over again". Not entering leaves you "a non-physical consciousness that is still trapped within the electromagnetic containment field, but... permitted to move about at will"; many are "easily tricked by other malevolent non-physical entities" or "return to what they know; the loving warmth of the tunnel of light, and the calls of their loved ones". The analogy: "going into the light" is "entering the main prison building, and Psych ward"; staying out is "the prison yard. In both cases, you need to make contact with someone who has the keys to the front gate." The instruction: stay near your body; Domain workers "will be acquired in short order"; everyone else should not shout for help in general (it "will attract all sorts of entities") but "call out for a Domain Officer to come and pick you up. You do this through thought visualization. Much the same way that people call out for Ganesha, or Jesus."

Three more items from the set became standing advice. The amnesia trap's geography: an ovoid region holding five inhabited solar systems; a great war about 70 million years ago; the Old Empire took over the "junk yard" about 208,000 years ago and built the complex about 75,000 years ago; beyond the edge an IS-BE is free but keeps only its last lifetime's memories. How to volunteer: modify your campaign to accept it, then expect a contact "in the non-physical body. It is very rare to be contacted in the physical body", felt as a vibration, a witnessed procedure, a lucid strange dream, or paralysis; "if you believe, or feel, that this event occurred then it actually did occur". A 19 September addendum: "Everything is proceeding to plan... You will always be supported... Trust in yourself, and follow MM." Sleep paralysis, which several readers were reporting: "an operation (of sorts) [like a medical procedure] that alters their non-physical body", severing "chains / tethers / controls / traps"; the consciousness is "placed in a holding chamber... while the non-physical body is being operated on"; panic "generates nightmares in the thought-sensitive operating chamber". "You are in the midst of friends."

24 September questions: the second answer set

Twenty-nine numbered questions, answered over several nights and published on 27 September. The standouts:

[1]Madesescapalion

A name that had surfaced in MM's mind. Answer: "the name / a name of / one of the names of the Chief Administrator" of the Earth part of the complex, "Warden Escapalion", an Old Empire administrator in human form who hid inside the prison population when the fleet was destroyed and has cycled through lives ever since, dying and fleeing into Heaven whenever The Domain gets close. (Two days later, asked "Who is MM?", the answer was "Mades Escapalion". An October session made him a minor functionary; part 23.)

[2] [3]Mantids and the helpers

The Mantids have "their own civilisation", accepted Domain jurisdiction, and keep their roles in Heaven. The technical helpers decoding the prison are a rare species from a distant galaxy, "not humanoid or insectoid"; MM's image was a sofa-sized caterpillar. "Call them Oompa Loompas / Minions", he wrote.

[4]Catastrophes ahead

"Yes. There will be difficult times ahead": a slow build to a tipping point, treasuries emptied, secret military engagements, "millions dead" unreported; The Domain mitigates the worst clusters and "will not permit substantive environmental destruction", but "some event will happen (as WWI did)".

[5]Why the Mantids keep helping

"The Mantids are not Wardens. They are corrections officers / archivists / medical practitioners and they are bred for their role." They treat humans "as adopted children from their nest" and are "absolutely convinced" that suffering then "a rest-period in Heaven" is compassion, a belief "genetically forced into them by the Old Empire". Both humans and Mantids were "modified to... never leave it". MM's one-word reply: "Jesus!"

[6] [7]Enlightenment, again

Meditation, focus, mantras and breathing are useful for the one skill that matters, thought control. But nirvana as a goal is "a dead end": "Do not try to become enlightened. Never try to become enlightened." It "has never freed anyone from the Prison Complex".

[8]Why not destroy it all

Trillions of IS-BEs would be loose with only their current-life memories, "armies of deranged evil IS-BEs" among them. MM's analogy: the containment grid in Ghostbusters.

[12] [17] [18]When your time comes

You cannot leave while the body lives; at death you are still tied to the non-physical body. "Request assistance from The Domain... like calling the fire department... Call for help and wait for us to arrive." Average people need not make contact now: "we have everything under control." And: "control your thoughts, avoid danger, help others; when the time comes for your egress we will be there."

[15] [16]Devices and escapes

The "Wet Room" that spins a consciousness through worlds is one of many corrections-officer devices; "some are truly awful". Escapes happened early; each one triggered new measures; now "highly unlikely without Domain assistance". Recaptured escapees get harder templates, shorter lives and "a special Hell area in Heaven"; the space around the complex is "a multi-layered minefield that reacts only to inmates".

[20]"How dangerous are the traps?"

"Check your cell phone."

[21]Anchoring and the cake

Mandela effects and world-line crossovers are "unique to the artificial prison MWI, not the way the true universe works". Anchoring is the administration's tool for herding inmates toward wars ("the complex is designed to have war after war"); The Domain uses the same technique to avoid them. Templates are "stacked like a layered cake"; trauma can slide you to a neighbouring layer until your "components resettle".

[22] [23]The interrupter

Inmate bodies grow an "interrupting" feature by age five to seven that blocks travel while asleep; strong characters and spirit-friendly cultures escape it; it "can be destroyed by directed thought and practice". Waking astral projection is hard because a human body cannot run two consciousnesses at once.

[27] [28]The Domain's classes

Everyone is an IS-BE and can rise by merit; classes carry allowances such as doll bodies; Domain members are "inherently neutral in sentience" but merit-based societies drift to service-to-others. Volunteering can make life easier, with trade-offs.

25 September: the inmate body

The same article states flatly: "There is no such thing as evolution." Every creature was manufactured, humans being "one common template for Sun Type 12 Class 7 planets"; Airl's Arcadia Regeneration Company story is the source (part 21). MM adds the Domain procedure that undoes the inmate modifications for volunteers: a "medical procedure in a Domain facility where the consciousness is placed in a containment box", which is how he reads his own 1984 to 85 memory of a North Carolina bath-house, paralysed, hours lost. It must be done "BEFORE being tricked into the tunnel of light". His suspicion, later half-confirmed (part 23): the Old Empire's prison administrators escaped The Domain "by hiding inside the prison population, in normal (non-inmate) bodies", keeping the keys.

6 October: the geode

What MM said about the sessions themselves

  • "Some of the responses were surprising. There was a latent humanity that I was not expecting. Some were aggressive and it was intended for me to transfer that aggressiveness in my report."
  • The numbers in the organisations answer "did not add up so I rounded them downwards"; he thinks the Commander "was just pitching vague rough figures".
  • Some passages are marked "(Garbled / unclear.)" in the published text; he left the gaps in.
  • "Don't shoot the messenger."

Remember

  • 16 September 2021, 9:45 to 10:39: the channel opens; Q&A by prompt, answer by picture.
  • Day-one plan: containment, thought suppression, snares, then biology, then release. "We sort by sentience."
  • At death: stay near the body, do not shout, call for a Domain officer "the way people call out for Ganesha, or Jesus".
  • Mantids are bred corrections officers who believe suffering is kindness. "Never try to become enlightened."
  • Inmate bodies: RAM only, a fence alarm in the non-physical half, technology held down. The whole thing sits in a geode.
