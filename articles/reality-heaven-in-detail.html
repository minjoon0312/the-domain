Heaven in detail: the anatomy of a soul and the map of the way station

The deep dive on parts 3, 6 and 7. What a soul is made of in MM's account (garbons, swales, 22 exchange lanes, the pineal attachment), the three kinds of soul, how a soul splits itself, MM's notes on Michael Newton's map of the spirit world, the Mantids' job of drawing your next life, and the cat stories that started it all.

AI summary · part 20 of 40 14 min read · 2,999 words 25 sources ↓

Written by an AI, not by Metallicman. This is a plain-language summary of 25 archived articles (mainly the collections Heaven). It restates what those sources say and does not add claims of its own. Every part links to its sources at the bottom so you can check the original wording.

In one breath

Parts 6 and 7 told you what happens at death and who runs the way station. This part opens the hood. In MM's account a soul is a lattice of quantum clumps he calls garbons, wired together by swales, with 22 main exchange lanes in a human and a control connection into the body at the pineal gland. Humans have an individual soul, cats and dogs a hive soul, Type-1 Greys a matrix. A soul never puts all of itself into one life: a few per cent to forty, MM says his own is set at 35. Then comes the map. MM reprints Michael Newton's two books on life between lives and annotates them line by line, agreeing with the geography (tunnel, homecoming, healing, guides, library, council, life selection) and correcting the assumption that everyone shares one world. The Mantids draw the next life; a cat in a shallow bowl of sleeping kittens is where MM says he first saw any of this.

One word, several places

MM uses "Heaven" loosely on purpose: "for now, I will just keep it simple and use that term to refer to EVERYTHING outside of a given world-line." Then he narrows it. Each species has "its own region within a universe", treated as a universe of its own, so there is an Earth Human Heaven, a cat Heaven, a dog Heaven. The human one is special in a bad way: it "services the Sentience Nurseries (prison planets)" and is the place the tunnel of light leads to (part 6). The pipe between a soul in Heaven and a body on Earth cannot be a plain pipe, because the two places run on different stuff: "think of Heaven as a place full of water, and the earth as a place full of electricity". Consciousness is the converter in the middle, "a tunnel that bores from Heaven to our physical reality".

What a soul is made of

This is the most technical thing MM ever wrote about the non-physical, and it comes from his 2018 cat article, not from the Commander. Everything is quanta. Experience makes quanta clump; a stable clump is a garbon, "a stable discrete cluster of organized quanta". Garbons connect to each other through swales, and the whole wiring is a lattice or "matrix", a term he says MAJestic used in the mid-1980s before the film borrowed it.

Why humans forget their past lives and cats do not follows from this. The human soul is an "intermediary form" that must "grow in a nursery" toward "a stable archetype", so "our memories of previous life incarnations are intentionally withheld from us so that we can develop and grow". Cats and dogs have a stable configuration and "are fully aware of their previous incarnations". Later, in the Newton notes, he puts it more bluntly: "How can you learn through your mistakes when you remember 10,000 past lives and 100,000 similar mistakes?"

Three kinds of soul

A soul never comes all at once

Newton's clients say a soul is "not totally discarnated" when it takes a body. MM's note: "A soul partitions itself into various consciousnesses. It assigns a percentage of itself into that consciousness. Which can vary from 5% up to 40%. In this life, for me, I actually happen to know that my earth consciousness as Metallicman is set at 35%, which is considered to be very high." The other 65 per cent stays in Heaven. A soul can also run two consciousnesses at once, in two bodies, possibly on different world-lines: "rapid growth in a smaller instance of time", but "ill advised". Every past life, he adds, can be treated as one unified consciousness "if it is your desire"; most souls keep them separate to build a base personality.

The map, and MM's notes in the margin

Dr Michael Newton was a hypnotherapist who regressed clients past their last death and recorded what they said about the time between lives. MM posts both books in full, then re-posts Journey of Souls as a five-part "study guide" with his own comments in boxes. His reason: the book "was written by an imperfect person" who assumed "two consciousnesses would occupy the same world-line simultaneously", which is "wrong, or at best, partially true". His image for the pairing: the book describes the feelings of people inside a car; his notes describe "the operation of the car wash". ΨORM (part 14) later folds the same geography into its own model as sections 28 and 29.

Death and departure
Everyday life already flickers in and out of the body between world-lines (here he gives the figure as "roughly about 144 times a second", elsewhere four; the point is the same). Death is when the consciousness "has stayed in wave form". A slower or faster flicker near death is what the dying feel as calm.
The tunnel
Not ordinary travel between lines: the consciousness is "leaving a plane of existence... and moving upwards to a different universe". The space just outside a line is noisy with "the thoughts of all the quantum shadows nearby". Light and colour are how a human-built soul interprets quanta; other species "would interpret these variations quite differently".
Homecoming
Souls project an image, usually the upper torso of the self they identify with; what you see of a friend is a "handshake", a compromise between your memory and their default image. Vanity "persists in the non-physical worlds". The "warm and sunny" crowd of well-wishers is real: "You are never alone."
Guides and colours
MM had many guides in training who "led me to the school, and then left me with the instructor". He finds Newton's colour grading of souls "trivial": "Color and appearance are all meaningless." Skill levels in a life "have no bearing on soul development"; an advanced soul "might live a life of squalor".
The library
The "book" of a life is an "archival map" of the world-line path taken and the probable paths not taken, not a diary. Buildings "are used to segregate groups". Temples versus office blocks are "all perception".
The council
Newton's Council of Elders reviews the life without punishing; ΨORM's summary line is "The Council does not judge, it guides." MM: "There is no Hell. The only punishment a person can have is to undergo a reincarnation where their roles are reversed." (The Commander, in 2021, added a harsher note: recaptured escapees get "a special Hell area in Heaven", part 8.) Newton's clients also speak of a "Law of Silence" about their reviews; MM keeps the same reticence about his training.
Life selection
Newton's "Ring of Destiny", where candidate lives are screened, matches what MM describes as "a place where simulations of the earth and a sequence of world-lines are created... like a holo-deck": the soul watches candidate lives as teenagers along "most likely world-line tracks", none of them fixed. Souls "do not organically grow... They are assembled. Other souls build them", "almost like a factory making robots".
The nursery, confirmed
His verdict on the whole curriculum: "Is this not what is going on in the studies by Dr Newton? Are the souls not going through an educational program?" And a personal aside: before this life "the entity was involved in many incarnations on earth in a selection of different species... over a 250,000 year period. Is this impressive? I do not know."

What MM says he remembers himself

  • No tunnel, a fog. "There is absolutely a static filled, cloudy, foggy field that surround the earth. I well remember this field and how my memories gradually disappeared as I entered it." The light was "a parting of the fog rather than a clear and direct" tunnel.
  • Elders and guardians. His knowledge "suggest a life in and about Heaven. To include meeting elders, guardian angels, and other important beings", though the details are gone.
  • A past-life regression, around 2000. Under hypnosis, in a recliner in the hypnotist's den, he recalled a last life as a man named Klingsmith who "died at 34 years of age" and "wasn't smart". This was before his MAJestic memories were unlocked, and he offers the session's statements as an independent check on them.
  • The Rhino dream. Four days after a university friend died on the football field, MM and two friends "all suddenly woke up" at 3 am having had the same dream of him saying he was fine.
  • The remote-viewed tunnel. In 2022 he shared a video of remote viewers describing the tunnel as a harvesting mechanism; Daegonmagus (part 33) wrote back "This is very, very similar to my experiences." MM's own comment was more careful: "I really don't know what to think."

How the next life gets drawn

The film reviews are part of the lesson. Defending Your Life gets the sequence right, in MM's view: "Birth in a body. Obtain experiences. Die. Life review. Map out what is next... Repeat." What Dreams May Come gets the scenery right and misses the review, and prompts his most useful correction: a newly dead consciousness "is really not in Heaven". It is on its exit template, "the MWI topographical map that your consciousness was on and following at the time of your death", still moving through lines, only in wave form. Animals, he adds, "do not enter a tunnel of light and retain their memories and self". Humans enter and lose them.

Cat Heaven, where the series began

Everything above was written in 2020 and 2021. The 18,000-word cat article came first, in 2018, and it is stories, not theory. A mother cat, dead, sending MM's wife into a panic on a stormy night until they found and fed a lost kitten. A cat killed in the woods, whose death MM felt as a sudden image of a large black dog. A kitten called Snowball who lived on MM's shoulder, followed him across the street "like a laser beam" and was killed by a speeding SUV; from the "startle" of that moment until late in the night MM felt him "spinning around and around my neck". Days later came what he calls a "special" dream.

Two later articles turn the stories into advice. Pets can follow you across world-lines while you are alive, because love "has quantum components that plug into our very bodies" and two entangled consciousnesses navigate together, "with the dominant personality taking on the majority". After death there are three ways to find them: will yourself to the pet on the exit template (dogs will sense you but stay physical; cats "can enter and leave the physical body at will" and join you); will yourself toward their Heaven and, if your "quantum alignments" stop you at the door, ask for help (he suggests a Type-1 Grey, and warns to be careful who offers); or accept the Mantid's "parole" for a visit once you are in the human Heaven. And prepare now, with a line or two in your campaign: "Upon my death, I will be able to meet with my beloved XXXXXXX."

Where consciousness came from: two answers

Alien InterviewBefore the universe, and already perfect

IS-BEs "have been around since before the beginning"; the physical universe was made later. No growth is needed; the whole idea of Heaven and an afterlife is "part of the hypnotic suggestion". Escape is the goal.

MAJesticBuilt in Heaven, grown by living

"Consciousness is constructed in human Heaven" and needs reincarnation to accumulate quanta and reach a stable archetype. The nursery is real and the curriculum has a purpose.

MM's reconciliationBoth, and a complaint

The earliest consciousnesses "could be considered part of The Domain"; others formed piecemeal in other universes. But "as elaborate as the idea of Heaven is, I simply cannot reconcile the need for amnesia as part of" it. That doubt is the hinge between parts 7 and 8.

evidence he keepsChildren who remember

He reprints Jim Tucker's and others' cases (James Leininger the pilot, the Pollock twins, Shanti Devi) and fits them to the model: a consciousness "stuck in a wave form" must find an infant that is "empty" and has a usable future.

Read with care

The soul anatomy (garbons, swales, 22 lanes, the pineal plug) is MM's own 2018 account, offered with the line "I just didn't pull all this information out of my ass. It came from somewhere", and it is not corroborated by the Commander sessions except in passing (the reform program "reconfigures garbons and swales", part 7). The Newton books are a third party's hypnosis research; MM endorses the geography and rejects the shared-world assumption. The 144-per-second figure conflicts with the four-per-second figure he uses everywhere else; both are his.

Remember

  • A soul is a lattice: garbons joined by swales, plugged into the body at the pineal gland.
  • Individual, hive, matrix. Humans forget on purpose; cats remember and can switch bodies.
  • You are 5 to 40 per cent of your soul; the rest stays in Heaven.
  • Newton's map holds (tunnel, homecoming, council, life selection); the single shared world does not.
  • The Mantids draw the next route; loved ones and pets write themselves into it.
