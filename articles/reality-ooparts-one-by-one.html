OOPARTs, one by one

The deep dive on part 15. Every object in MM's mysteries index with what it is, where it was found, the age claimed for it, and his verdict: the shellfish hammer, the aluminium pawl, the bell and the lamp in coal, the 300-million-year wheel, the geode part, the Victoria Falls fuselage, the 250,000-year Hueyatlaco tools, the walled-up chamber two miles down, the Crespi collection, the Baltic object, the Lapulapu tracks, the Dellschau airships, the Andaste giants, the Madoc and Newfoundland ships, Mars in 1984, and a ring by the Sun.

Written by an AI, not by Metallicman. This is a plain-language summary of 29 archived articles (mainly the collections OOPART Related Index). It restates what those sources say and does not add claims of its own. Every part links to its sources at the bottom so you can check the original wording.

In one breath

Part 15 explained why MM collects "out of place artifacts": if the official time track is a story assembled backwards from event pockets, then a hammer in 40-million-year-old rock is not a hoax to be explained away but a note from another track. This part is the catalogue. For each object it gives the find, the claimed age, and MM's own conclusion in his words, which range from careful ("All of this is speculative") to blunt ("If it looks like a duck... it's a duck"). Most of the articles are his summaries of other people's reports with his commentary appended; the metallurgy of the London Hammer, the aluminium pawl of Aiud and the Lapulapu sea-floor tracks are where he does his own work. Reader's rule, from the hammer article: "It would be interesting to see if someone skilled in Psychometry or Retrocognition would have to say when holding this object."

Objects in coal and rock

coal, 300 million yearsThe bell

A brass hand-bell in a block of coal. "The only way that any item can be encased within coal is if it was placed there while the coal was still soft and wet organic material." Humans, on his dating, "did not exist until around 25,000 years ago"; therefore "an intelligently produced object that predates mankind".

coal, 300 million yearsThe oil lamp

"A cast iron object that is apparently an oil lamp was found in a lump of coal... It was manufactured. It was designed and intended for use. Since there is no evidence that humans existed 300 million years ago, the object had to have been made by non-humans."

sandstone, deep mineThe wheel

A wheel "firmly embedded deep down inside a mine shaft", with ruts "that take many centuries to harden into stone". "Dates are being bantered about of 300 million years, and 12 million years. In any event these dates pre-date apes, monkeys, primates and proto-humans." His Carboniferous sketch: one supercontinent, giant insects that "develop specialized intelligences", "NO apes, primates or humans".

two miles down, 1928The walled-up chamber

Atlas Almon Mathis's account of a smooth concrete wall struck in coal mine No. 5 near Heavener, Oklahoma, "two miles deep". MM maps the mine to the Arkoma Basin coal country, dates the seam to the Carboniferous ("the age of amphibians", "the air was completely different"), and files it with the coal objects.

geode, "200 million years"The polished part

"Since there is little that we can learn from this object except that it appears to be the fossilized remains of some kind of chamber, the use of Psychometry might be useful." His closest match: "something that resembles the oxygen tanks about the Apollo spacecraft", a vessel "tending to be spherical, with probes and a heater assembly".

meteorite, 4.5 billion yearsThe metal object

A reported machined piece inside a meteorite. "Most of the stuff that I, MM have dealt with is millions of years old. Not billions... I really only know about the stuff prior to the formation of the solar system through the chatter of my pilot." "Amazing stuff", and a request that someone in the UK go and look.

Machines and parts

The aluminium pawl of Aiud
A metallurgical study of a machined aluminium block found in Romania in the 1970s beside mastodon bones. "If it looks like a duck, quacks like a duck, waddles like a duck and tastes like a duck... it's a duck. The only thing is that the particular species of a duck is new and unknown." His reconstruction: "A machine, probably an aircraft, lost a part of its retractable landing gear around one million years ago near the Black Sea. The local proto-humanoids at that time, probably a species similar to Homo Habilis found the part and decided that it made a great hand tool. They used it to smash open the bones of the mastodons." "Who flew the aircraft... is unknown", but they "knew their metallurgy... and had the ability to fly in aircraft that encountered high temperature extremes."
The Victoria Falls fuselage
Debris in sandstone read as aircraft skin. "A vehicle crashed or broke up in the oceans of the Cambrian. Fuselage debris washed up on the sandy beaches of what is now Scotland. Over millions of years, the debris became encased in sandstone." The date, he adds, "is associated with the Cambrian explosion" and with "the general time period where the moon entered earth's orbit" (part 32).
The Baltic object
"An object was discovered under the water in the Baltic sea. The object had the shape of a conventionally understood extraterrestrial source UFO." Detailed sonar images were posted and then "taken down"; "someone removed the object from where it was found"; afterwards "photos of the empty debris field" appeared, and today it "is considered a trivial misunderstanding of a discovery of some normal rocks on the sea floor".
The Dellschau airships
Charles Dellschau, a Prussian immigrant in Houston, left coded scrapbooks of airship designs from a secret society, "NYMZA"; found in a junk shop, studied by an art student and by Pete Navarro. MM sets them against the airship sightings of 1880, 1892, 1896, 1897 and 1909, and draws a moral about secrets: "The idea of lighter than air vehicles were a reality at a time when the world's renowned scientists couldn't even contemplate human flight." "The world is filled with secrets. The idea that everyone should know everything and that there are no secrets is a big lie."
The mysterious book
A locked wooden box found with a handmade book of meticulous drawings, one a "patent" never filed, one dated 1939, by someone who "had an experience that was definitely not an everyday one". MM's point is about context: "Without context, the object holds no meaning. Though, however, that doesn't stop our minds from trying to piece together narratives."

Places

Hueyatlaco, Mexico
Stone tools dated to about 250,000 years by several methods in the 1960s and 70s. "Assuming that the array of scientific evidence is correct, then the Hueyatlaco site is truly ancient and indicates tool-using, and tool-manufacturing humanoids 250,000 years ago." Either "early man constructed ocean capable vessels" or "evolved independently in the Americas"; MM prefers "various evolutionary clusters and separate lineages that did not originate from a common Lucy humanoid". "While not popular at this time... this understanding will be embraced hundreds of years in the future." The Commander's 2023 line (part 29), that humanoids "appeared more or less uniformly" with no migration track, is the same claim from the other side.
The Crespi collection, Cuenca
Father Carlo Crespi's museum of metal plates and figures brought by local people, which he held proved "a direct connection between the Old World (Babylon) and the New World"; burnt in an arson in 1962, the remainder "in a terrible muddle" of brass, copper, gold and silver. MM reprints the story (largely via von Däniken) as a mystery that "turned the archaeological world on its side".
The Andaste of Tioga Point
"Did extraterrestrials set up a colony in Pennsylvania?" A community described in local histories as unique: "These people were giants, had red skin, long substantive horns and possessed firearms. The women were also large. They disappeared at the same time that European settlers arrived." What remains is "a museum in Bradford County, Pennsylvania". His rule: "If an extraterrestrial species were to set up a colony on earth they would appear unique."
Megalithic sites
"Ancient man at 10,000 years ago had understanding and abilities regarding the ability to sense non-physical actions, and energy better than what we modern humans have"; copper mining implies metal tools; the sites mark places with "non-physical components" that their builders could feel.
Madoc, and Newfoundland
The Welsh prince Madoc's legendary twelfth-century voyage and a forgotten follow-up expedition, told for the pleasure of it ("visit your local historical society... in Erie, or in Kittanning"); and Ronald Lloyd Ryan's argument for scuttled Chinese ships in Newfoundland: "There is strong reason to believe that Newfoundland was indeed settled by the Chinese. That they interacted, and perhaps forged alliances with local Indians and Vikings." "No one knows what happened to them."
The dinosaur that ate a proto-human
A fossil report of human remains in a dinosaur, with Sumerian carvings of "scaled doglike creatures on leashes" for company. "The evidence is clear that large dinosaur creatures coexisted with humans for centuries. That, perhaps, their demise was due to hunting by humans rather than by extinction via meteor." And the moral he attaches to most of these: "when money is involved, it trumps all."

Sky and space

1984Mars, one million years B.C.

A declassified Stargate session (CIA-RDP96-00788R001900760001-9): a sealed envelope with "The planet Mars. Time of interest approximately 1 million years B.C.", double- and triple-blind. MM's disclaimer: "my involvement was related to other subjects and other agendas. This information is provided as reported." He reprints the transcript, pyramids and tall figures included, and comments at the end.

1976Life on Mars

Gilbert Levin's Viking Labeled Release results: "positive results from a widely-used microbiological test... and the failure over 43 years of any experiment or theory to provide a definitive nonbiological explanation". MM: "Life is common throughout the universe... If you have a difficult time coming to grips with this realization then I suggest that YOU SHOULD STOP PUTTING LIMITS ON GOD."

STEREOA ring by the Sun

A solar-observatory frame with a ring-shaped object. "It looks like a ringed space-station. It is unlikely that it was made by humans, staffed by humans, or near the sun for human interests of any type. That's all that I can say."

the Moon, MarsAlready covered

The hollow Moon and the Oxia Palus facility are in part 32; the Commander's Moon history is in part 26.

a bookForbidden Archeology

Cremo and Thompson's compendium, posted in full: evidence "systematically suppressed, ignored, or forgotten... not through a conspiracy organized to deceive the public, but through an ongoing social process of knowledge filtration". MM's shelf reference for everything above.

a joke"Ah. The handiwork of God."

A 400-word aside on patterns that fit together "no matter how complex, and convoluted life is": "the idea that there is a God, or a grand force that we belong to is too strong to discount casually."

What the list is for

The index also holds the Mandela effect ("a system of soul management"), the chartreuse colour question and, oddly, essays on Genghis Khan's women, Lincoln and the Tenth Amendment, and what high school taught MM about democracy: the category is "mysteries and curiosities", not strictly artifacts. The frame that ties the objects together is in part 15 and in the Commander's history answer of October 2023 (part 29): there is no single evolutionary time track; event pockets on Earth's templates "auto-connect backwards", so a coherent past is assembled from pieces, "like pieces of paper on an ocean" drifting together. An object in the wrong stratum is a piece from a different sheet. MM does not claim to know which sheet: "Who flew the aircraft, or what it was doing when it lost its part is unknown."

Read with care

Nearly every item here is contested. Mainstream accounts date the London Hammer's concretion to recent centuries, treat the Aiud object as probably modern excavator equipment, and read the coal finds as unverifiable nineteenth-century newspaper stories; the Hueyatlaco dating is disputed; the Baltic feature is generally held to be a glacial deposit; Dellschau's airships are art. MM's articles usually mention the sceptical view and then argue past it. The archive records his conclusions as his and does not adjudicate. The Stargate document is real and declassified; its interpretation is not evidence of anything about Mars.

Remember

  • A shellfish hammer from a 40-million-year shore, argued from sulphur, chlorine and a fossilised handle.
  • A landing-gear pawl a million years old, used to crack mastodon bones; "it's a duck".
  • A bell, a lamp, a wheel and a wall in 300-million-year coal; a chamber part in a geode.
  • Tracks through sea mountains off Saipan; airships before flight; giants with horns in Pennsylvania.
  • Tools 250,000 years old in Mexico, and a Commander who says humanoids appeared everywhere at once.
  • All of it "speculative", and all of it, in the model, pieces of other sheets.
