Parts 1 to 3 of the comm: December 2021 to January 2022

The deep dive on parts 7, 8, 10 and 11. The first three numbered "EBP comm" sessions: contact protocols, the escaped Old Empire leadership and their "termite colony", where human inmates have really been taken (the Moon, Mars, Ganymede), Rh-negative blood, the faeries, the disabled missiles, the onion that lucid dreamers cannot cut, the Nags Head operation, and the order to obey your inner Mantid.

AI summary · part 24 of 40 9 min read · 2,019 words 3 sources ↓

Written by an AI, not by Metallicman. This is a plain-language summary of 3 archived articles (mainly the collections The Domain, MAJestic Related Index). It restates what those sources say and does not add claims of its own. Every part links to its sources at the bottom so you can check the original wording.

In one breath

From December 2021 MM numbered the sessions. Parts 1 to 3 were answered over Christmas and New Year "with house construction all around, a two year old, and the demands of life", and he says he asked the Commander to "increase the gain" so his own opinions would not leak into the answers. The content is a grab-bag, because the questions came from readers: how to make contact yourself (you cannot, and should not try), what the mRNA injections are (a claim he logs and we log as a claim), who the escaped Old Empire leaders became, where MAJestic really sent people, why some blood is Rh-negative, whether the faeries are real, what The Domain did to the missiles, why lucid dreaming cannot break the amnesia machine, and, in a "bonus" he calls his cherished beliefs, why the Mantids outrank The Domain in your own life.

Part 1: December 2021

CE-5, meditation contact
Possible but unnecessary. An untargeted beacon is "just M-band noise" ("how often do you enjoy watching commercials?"); a message needs a specific recipient, and group messages stand out more. Hazard: you may invite dangerous entities. Meditation, fasting, diet and "consciousness centring" are good practices anyway.
The injections
Asked about the Western vaccines and the Deagel figures, the answer (December 2021) was that they are "not a vaccine" but a population-control platform: boosters as revenue, additives at will, "harmonics" pulsed through social media to steer emotions; spike-protein damage leading to early organ failure, with deaths rising through 2022 and 2023 and peaking in 2024 unless a different anchoring program or "rectification injections" (a possible black market) intervene. Only the mRNA injections are described as so compromised. Advice: exercise, fresh air, no processed food, and affirmation campaigns "can prevent the bad effects". Logged here as a dated claim; it is not medical advice and the archive does not endorse it.
Personal readings
Readers asked who they were. Daegonmagus is "something else entirely", on a team doing "multi-dimensional fracturing and disassembly of pocket universes", results due in 60 to 75 years. One reader was a satyr-like Old Empire being ("far shorter than what is depicted", three sexes, kept rabbits), an incorrigible criminal (murder, body swapping, soul extraction, fraud, tax evasion) banished in the prison's early years before human suits existed, and now "worthy of release": call for pickup at death. Another was "railroaded into prison so that others might steal from you, and profit from you", by family and business partners.
The MM community
Connections here are pre-birth-planned: "frequency resonance is intentional, artificially induced to create forged alliances"; many were invited pre-birth by MM's cluster. Add affirmation questions to explore your own.

Part 2: where the inmates have been taken

bloodRh-negative

A skin-suit variant created about 60,000 years ago near the Pyrenees to resist a cat-borne (faeces) parasite once humans began keeping cats. It attracts IS-BEs who want science- and STEM-oriented lives; the personality traits people attach to it are coincidental.

faeriesThe sidhe are real, and not inmates

"Native non-physical inhabitants of the British Island grouping", with cousins worldwide; their civilisation centres stayed put through ice ages and sea-level changes, so many are now under water; they avoid the harsh thoughts of inmates; mutual respect with The Domain, no formal dealings.

before the prisonEarth resisted

When the Old Empire built the complex, Earth's existing sentiences fought: humanoid colonies, dolphins, "primitive tool-makers", and extensive non-physical species, of which the faerie realm was the largest. "A war took place."

missilesDisabled in their cradles

"We have intentionally disabled ICBM weapons in their launch cradles, and disconnected the command and control centers... This was sufficient, back in the 1960's and 1970's to persuade both the Russian and the American military that launching these weapons would have dire consequences that we would control. Not them." This region was devastated by nuclear wars long ago, "perhaps one of the many reasons" it became a prison. "Possession of powerful weapons... makes one dangerous."

Deagel"Not misinformation"

The forecast of a Western die-off "was based on two things": historical trends, and "horrific remote viewing results" from "numerous independent remote viewers" who reported the same profile. Logged as a claim.

Mantid PrimesAmerica's cycle

The "catastrophic change" in America is "a cyclic event programmed into the general population system" that everyone agreed to in their templates; reality and Heaven interact ("changes in reality instigate changes in Heaven"). Mantid society is alien, and The Domain avoids discussing it.

The bonus: "my cherished beliefs"

A channel can open "in the elevator... or at the checkout in a store". In one such moment MM wondered why he is not allowed to say much about the Mantids. The answer: "Mantids are the most important species (race) beings in the human inmate suit right now. They are the behind the scenes movers / shakers / lever makers. To question their role in your life is to question your own self. If a choice must be made between The Domain or the mantids you must always obey your inner mantid directives and directions. We (The Domain) lie outside of that bonded interconnection... We (The Domain) are mission directed. The mantids are consciousness focused. Obviously that is more important to your individual growth and eventual release from the prison planet environment than anything else."

Part 3: the cornfield, the operation, and the onion

On banning readers
MM's "cornfield" (his term for banishment from the site, from the Twilight Zone episode) is neither right nor wrong: "no right or wrong, only cause and effect". Fear is "noise". Taming it, "no matter how loudly they clamor at you", is the whole task.
Basic training
Happens in the non-physical, with body modifications; most active MM participants agreed pre-birth to meet here, in clusters of pre-birth recruitment.
Nags Head, North Carolina
MM's memory of an operation in 1984 to 85 (part 22): "conducted by a team of specialists", a mix of "Domain Officers that temporarily occupied the bodies of human inmate skin suits" and "actual / real / non-modified human skin-suits" brought "from another region", who did the physical work. "You did not observe the procedure as your consciousness was container placed, while the physical body was replaced." The non-physical alterations were led by "the Chief Nurse", who "worked on you in other events".
Spaceflight
Manned spaceflight is held back by greed, politics and corruption, and by "unexploded munitions" and traps littering the containment field; The Domain does not interfere with vehicles inside general population.
Radiation and autism
Many synergistic factors (non-physical bodies, diet, fate profile, surrounding thoughts, viral agents); UHF is at best a contributor.
"Dying race", again
Denied again: all IS-BE consciousness is eternal.

Read with care

Parts 1 to 3 contain the archive's most concrete dated predictions (injection deaths peaking in 2024, the Deagel die-off). They are reproduced because MM published them, and only as claims; nothing here is health advice. The personal readings are answers to individual readers and are paraphrased, not verified. And the whole set was received, by MM's own account, in noisy conditions with the "gain" turned up, which is why several passages are marked garbled or redacted in the originals.

Remember

  • You cannot dial The Domain yourself; an untargeted beacon is noise and may attract the wrong listeners.
  • The escaped Old Empire leadership lives inside the complex on "special passes", running pockets, including the "rape centers".
  • Inmate suits have been to the Moon, Mars and Ganymede, by portal, as observers; never outside the walls.
  • Faeries are native, non-physical and free; Rh-negative is a 60,000-year-old anti-parasite suit; the missiles were disabled in the 1960s and 70s.
  • Lucid dreaming cannot cut the onion. And when Domain and Mantid disagree, "obey your inner mantid".
