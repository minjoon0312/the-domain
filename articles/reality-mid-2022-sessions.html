Parts 9 and 10 of the comm: the overlay, the Lego soul, and Larry Spencer's death

The deep dive on parts 3, 4, 6, 10 and 13. Two sessions from March and April 2022: the "control overlay" the Mantids run on top of reality, MM's own origin as a soul built from spare parts, the iceberg and the Lego bricks, what happened when the author of Alien Interview died, three ways to reach The Domain, why "older you" visits happen, how hard-core Domain prisoners are kept small, and the lesson of Matilda's rescue.

AI summary · part 27 of 40 13 min read · 2,791 words 2 sources ↓

Written by an AI, not by Metallicman. This is a plain-language summary of 2 archived articles (mainly the collections The Domain). It restates what those sources say and does not add claims of its own. Every part links to its sources at the bottom so you can check the original wording.

In one breath

Two sessions in which the machinery gets a new layer and MM gets a new biography. Part 9 says there is a "control overlay" on top of reality, where the Mantids configure your life using "books, papers, radio, and all the rest", and that ghosts in photographs are snapshots of it; it tells MM he is not a captured Domain soldier but a soul assembled from the disassembled components of an Old Empire administrator; it explains bodies as an iceberg of Lego bricks; and, asked three times, it reports that Lawrence Spencer, who published Alien Interview, died, went happily to his loved ones, walked into the tunnel, and was not stopped, because he never called. Part 10 adds the hen-house parable about who really holds power, the "older me" visits that about one in six people get, how the Domain's most stubborn prisoners are cycled through mice and squirrels at three per cent of their quanta, and the warning "Do not be like Mathilda".

Part 9: the overlay

9.3 and 9.4: "You are constructed from the components"

MM asked how he could be both Mades Escapleon and a Domain man. The answer reversed part 23: "You are constructed from the components; the soul components of what was once Mades Escapleon. Particularly bad individuals, or soul constructs that are particularly bad or incorrigible are disassembled by the Mantid Primes, broken down into base components, and then reconstituted into usable elements... You were constructed from what once was Mades Escapleon, set forth and began your cycles of life and death through cycling reincarnation, and are thus a rather pure soul. We (elements of The Domain) agents then took you and made agreements with you to set up your experience in this lifetime to be Domain." MM: "Life is an erector set, eh?" Part 10 adds that "none of the Domain has (yet) been disassembled into base components for uses elsewhere (such as with MM)". Note the shift: in October 2021 he "was" the administrator; by April 2022 he is made of his parts. The archive reports both.

Question 9.6: the death of Lawrence Spencer

MM: "When the answers arrived, I could not believe them, as I was uncomfortable with them. So I asked a second time. Same answers. I then waited, and did something else, and asked a third time. The results were consistent." The answer: "Upon Lawrence Spencer's death we followed the standard death sequence and transitioned to the non-physical realms smoothly and effortlessly. He did not stay near his physical body, but immediately transitioned towards loved ones from previous incarnations that he was attracted to. It was a joyous time, and they led him into the tunnel of light where he moved away from the reality universe into the Heaven pocket universe. He did not seek out The Domain, think of any Domain members, nor called out to us in any way shape or form. As is our standard protocol, we allowed the entity to progress as they desire... If any entity transpires and wishes for help or assistance from The Domain, they must physically call out to us, think and image us and request our assistance. We will not do anything, we will stand down, unless specifically engaged." The man who put the warning into print, in this account, did not use it.

9.2A sense of humour

Asked whether the Commander has one, MM "was tickled relentlessly... but not feel any fingers on my physical body".

9.7Trump, biolabs

"Former President Trump is a wise man. He is not appreciated as such due to his unique and bellicose behavior... he is first and foremost sincere." Political opinion, logged as stated.

9.8Invisibility

Exists, partly reverse-engineered, partly "derived organically from creative and curious inmates"; used by extraterrestrials "that wish to walk among humans" and by "certain clandestine government entities".

9.9bFirewalling the suit

"The inmate skin suit is designed as a vehicle of control. It has missing defense mechanisms, holes that allow intentional and undesirable access, corruption of normal thought processes." Volunteers get "a tune up / patch up / operation" that closes some entry ways.

9.10a, 9.10bChina and Taiwan

"The Chinese are a peaceful race" who will not strike first unless a threat "is planned for implementation before the end of 2025"; if red lines are crossed the response "will be colossal"; "China will seize Taiwan in a manner that is intended to prevent third-parties from involvement." Dated 2022; claims.

9.12, 10.4The fake alien invasion

Discussed in the 1980s "as a venue to gather nations together", then abandoned because the leadership "no longer had a desire to unify the world. Instead, they wanted a world fractured into a class system of owners and servants / slaves." "Not considered in any way to be part of the main primary template."

9.13Names

Asking what The Domain calls itself produced "a bunch of weird results... swishing". Then: "the Domain membership does not have the need for names like humans do. They simply think of a fellow Domain member and they are connected automatically."

Part 10: hens, holograms and mud cliffs

10.1 "Is this what I signed up for?"
Yes: "an intentional decision that was planned in advance", worked out "with many trial and error forays to perfect the present general population experience". This questioner "has a very fated life".
10.2 No convergence
There is no single timeline for humanity. "Each travel vector of individual time is a personal one"; what looks like convergence is "the highest-probability template" from which most pre-birth templates derive. Its anchoring could be changed, but The Domain "deliberately permits the current state" to reach its goals.
10.3 Stages of sentience
Service-to-others ("SEO" here) "is not the end-point of IS-BE evolution. It is an evolutionary step." It is the highest available to a suited human and required of MAJestic members and volunteers; outside, "a new selection of consciousness sentiences growth vectors present themselves".
10.5 Apollo
In April 2022: feasible through "a radiation barrier within the spacecraft" and a fast trajectory through the thinnest part of the belts. (In December 2023 the same channel said the craft went and landed but "no men" walked; part 29 keeps both.)
10.6b Pickup, again
"As a technical measure the questioner, as well as any other entity so desiring of Domain assistance, must request that said assistance. We do not make assumptions on the needs, desires and thoughts of other entities." Volunteers get "a special priority".
10.7a to 10.7e War, duty cycles
"All wars, and all battles, occur on different levels (different planes)... with the physical level being the most coarse." MM's readers DM, SD and PL are "busy in the non-physical realms", some "performing very dangerous and risky activities", on their own pre-agreed efforts. Ups and downs are normal: the ideal volunteer "duty cycle" is "20% to 30% work". Relocation options are set by the template: "Growth through strife is a hallmark of the Mantid Prime operational parameters." Africa's expected collapse "will not manifest".
10.7g Forecasts
None for the bulk of the trap systems: "Many of the non-physical systems will lie in hibernation and stasis until triggered"; taming them "will not be realized for many human lifetimes".
10.7h A reader's father
MM answers himself: unlikely to have been implanted; "sex offender retirement did not start to occur until the mid 1990s when MAJestic operations started to be phased out".
10.11 NASA and portals
Earth-side organisations use "advanced Domain technology" with members drawn from public bodies; MAJestic had portal access, "as did (has) other United States organizations, as well as elements of both Russian, Chinese, British assets". The questioner had seen "an overlay".
10.12 Healing
To a mother: "the over reliance on Western medicines will result in the death of her daughter"; a holistic approach and Chinese traditional medicine "would certainly work". A personal reading, reported only as what was said, not as advice.
10.13 Memory
"Memories are not retained in the human brain. They are external to the physical body." At death "it retains all memories"; after recycling most "re-acquire a portion of forgotten memories, selectively entrusted by Mantid support".
10.14a Five instructions
"[1] Control your fears. [2] Realize that there is no rush to do anything... [3] Understand that your non-physical body is saturated with snares, traps, and chains... [4] Call for Domain assistance and direction... [5] Realize that The Domain is a volunteer organization."
10.14d, 10.14e Extractions and affirmations
"Two have been successfully extracted. They are safe within the Domain, and are in extended treatment programs." Affirmations "work in both the reality universe and the main universe", and "faster, and with greater efficiency once one leaves".
10.15 The 2030s to 2050s
"Physical events related to galactic forces and planetary issues... will present challenges to many species. But the changes will be able to be controlled and mitigated somewhat."

10.14b: "Do not be like Mathilda"

MM notes "Emotional response. Something went wrong." Then: "Fear responses as a part of the inmate skin-suit were not well established and understood when Mathilda was rescued and recovered... she was the first entity we (the local group) tried to recover. There were many mistakes. Her imprinting was far stronger than Domain recognized. This resulted in a full fight-and-flight response. She / it kicked up a fury and created substantial damage (as only a Domain fighter is able to). There were many casualties and great damages, and then mathilda fled." Because The Domain obeys "the wishes of all of our membership / citizenry. Even when they are not of understanding / mind", she was allowed to leave: she "conducted a pass-through and left the universe. We know where she is... but we are helpless to assist as we need to be given permission." The lesson became a procedure: graduated memory retrieval under medical supervision, "around 90% complete" by late 2023 (part 29). The name is spelled Mathilda in these sessions; whether this is the Roswell nurse of part 21 is not stated.

Remember

  • Above the layers sits a control overlay; peripheral shadows and "ghost" photos are snapshots of it; MM is watched through it by his old handlers.
  • MM, per April 2022: built from the parts of Mades Escapleon, "a rather pure soul", recruited afterwards.
  • Bodies are one iceberg of Lego bricks; The Domain rearranges them for volunteers.
  • Spencer died, went to the light, was not stopped: nobody called. Asked three times.
  • Three ways to reach them: belonging, the EBP, or practising telepathy until the bricks move.
  • Stubborn prisoners are spread thin across mice and snakes; the first rescue went badly. "Do not be like Mathilda."
