The Domain and the Commander

A vast, disciplined organisation from the main universe that says it conquered the Old Empire, lost 3,000 of its own inside the prison, and since September 2021 has been answering questions through an implant in Metallicman's skull. Who they are, how the comm works, what they want, and their rules.

AI summary · part 9 of 40 6 min read · 1,429 words 22 sources ↓

Written by an AI, not by Metallicman. This is a plain-language summary of 22 archived articles (mainly the collections The Domain, Type 1 Grey Extraterrestrials). It restates what those sources say and does not add claims of its own. Every part links to its sources at the bottom so you can check the original wording.

In one breath

The Domain is the name, from the 1947 book Alien Interview, of the civilisation that the Grey officer "Airl" said she belonged to: an expeditionary force that reached this sector, fought the Old Empire, and lost a battalion of 3,000 to the prison's amnesia machinery. On 16 September 2021 MM published a message he says came through his implant: The Domain was calling for its lost people and for volunteers. Fifteen question-and-answer sessions followed (2021 to 2024). The Domain's rules never change: it recruits only service-to-others beings, it works mostly in the non-physical, and it will not help anyone at death who does not ask.

Who they say they are

formNon-corporeal

"Trans-dimensional spirit form", timeless. They inject a consciousness into the physical "to experience time", commute in and out on very long duty cycles, and occupy "doll bodies" or take over bodies when work needs hands. The ruling class avoids flesh bodies.

societyClasses and roles

Roles by ability: engineers, administrators, historians, social engineers, soldiers, pilots. Structured classes, "finer and reasonable" rules, stewardship duties. "Work is play"; vacations are taken in skin suits.

memoryNo amnesia, ever

Their class structure "prohibits memory amnesia while in a body"; members recall lives "back trillions of years". Members need no names: think of someone and you are connected.

reachMost of the master universe

"The Domain spans most of the Master Universe and beyond"; this sector is one contested region among "bigger, more pressing galactic issues" that limit its resources here.

sentienceService to others, sorted

Sorting beings into STO, SFA, STS and DIS "is a Domain requirement... unique to Domain", the sole administrative authority in the region. Only STO beings are recruited.

local assetsFacilities and allies

Teleportation facilities on Earth; sites in and on the Moon, Mars and Ganymede; the Type-1 Greys as officers; the general-population Mantids as partners; a diminutive allied species MM calls "Oompa Loompas".

The lost battalion

Alien Interview is the account, published by Lawrence Spencer, of a nurse named Matilda MacElroy who in 1947 spoke telepathically with the surviving pilot of the Roswell craft. The pilot, Airl, described herself as an officer, pilot and engineer of "The Domain Expeditionary Force", said The Domain had a base in the asteroid belt, and said that 3,000 members of a Domain battalion had been captured, given amnesia and recycled through the prison, where they still are. MM read the book much later, recognised the "Type-1 Greys" of his MAJestic years in it, and re-read his own career as a piece of the same story. His breakdown of the book runs to eight articles; part 8 of these summaries has the prison history it gives.

The sessions, 2021 to 2024

What they want

  1. Recover the battalion. "We know where every Domain member is... Our problem is not locating them, it is extracting them in such a way as to prevent catastrophic destruction of their surviving quantum forms." MM is one of the "funnels" that herd survivors "into stable stasis". Some members are held in "the Hole" with the Goetic spirits; none has been extracted from there yet.
  2. Recruit volunteers. Readers who say they are willing get "kits": alterations of the non-physical body that remove suit restrictions and enable lucid dreaming and out-of-body work with practice. By 2023 "a much higher number than we anticipated" had volunteered; their dream activity acts as tracer that exposes Old Empire traps, which The Domain then "hogs out".
  3. Run the shutdown. Sort sentiences, scrub the suits, return memories, and release in order: the lost battalion first, then STO, then SFA, and last STS and DIS. The most dangerous are banished to a pocket within the pocket, or reduced to components. The prison becomes a learning environment, not a prison (part 8).
  4. Keep the lid on. They will not take total control of this space for something like 5,000 years, will not interfere with vehicles inside general population, and prefer "small bads, not big bads". They say they disabled ICBMs in their cradles in the 1960s and 70s and allow no nuclear device through their teleportation facilities.

Their rules for humans

Ask, or nothing happens
"We will NOT do anything unless explicit permission is asked." At death: call out and keep calling. In life: "Verbalization of intent is the legal document that Domain uses to authorize injection."
STO only, obedience required
Joining means a service-to-others nature, accepting Domain laws and a social class after review, and accepting changes to your non-physical body, which will trigger the suit's fear mechanisms.
No proof will be given
"We cannot alter the physical reality unless we are in a physical container", and asking an agent to do so "is outside of the contract agreements that we make with our friends and volunteers."
No name-dropping, no de-cloaking
Do not try to expose imprisoned Domain members by affirmation; do not "assist others without their permission" (unwanted entanglements).
Mantids outrank us in your life
"If a choice must be made between The Domain and the mantids you must always obey your inner mantid directives." The Domain is mission-directed; the Mantids are consciousness-focused; they are "equals with different abilities".
Expect quiet
When there are no tasks, "the attachments go dormant". Volunteers are used sparingly; an ideal duty cycle is 20 to 30 percent work. Dreams of battles or teams of animals are taskings; "we never allow our assets to work without support".

Earth politics, in their telling

The Commander's remarks on governments are among the most contested parts of the archive, and the summaries report them only as claims. He says The Domain phased MAJestic out in the late 1990s and now works with "a core organization in China" ("China is a Domain asset"); that the 1963 assassination of John F. Kennedy was the first sign of trouble with the US relationship; that "control kits" were later installed on US military leadership; and, in August 2022, that "medium bads" (hundreds of millions of deaths) had become probable in a West-versus-East confrontation. dated forecasts Part 17 collects these with their dates so they can be checked against what actually happened.

What is first-hand here

Only MM has the channel. Every answer in the archive is MM's transcription of his own impressions, which he says are filtered by his understanding and sometimes wrong in detail. Guest writer Daegonmagus reports separate contact with other non-physical groups (the "Elder Guardians", the "Unseen 5") and briefs the Commander through MM; PissedLizard reports his own experiences of the light. Alien Interview itself is a book whose editor said the source documents were destroyed. Part 17 is the place for evidence; this part only reports what is said.

Remember

  • The Domain: non-corporeal, disciplined, sorted by sentience, in charge of this sector, short of resources here.
  • It wants its 3,000 back, volunteers, and a slow conversion of the prison; it does not want to give proof.
  • One channel (MM's EBP), fifteen numbered sessions plus early rounds, 2021 to 2024.
  • Rules: ask out loud; STO only; obey your Mantid first; no de-cloaking; expect long silences.
