ΨORM, section by section

The deep dive on part 14. A guided walk through all 33 sections of the Ψ-Ontic Reality Model, MM's 2026 attempt to rebuild his whole account of reality as a formal, falsifiable framework: the four claims and the physics they answer to, a qubit toy model, five predictions, attractor basins, the 4 Hz duty cycle, the anomalies, the filter, the practice sections that re-derive the intention course, the Newton chapters, the eight stages of death, and the ten "bridges" written for readers of other faiths and of none.

AI summary · part 35 of 40 12 min read · 2,630 words 42 sources ↓

Written by an AI, not by Metallicman. This is a plain-language summary of 42 archived articles (mainly the collections ΨORM: the Ψ-Ontic Reality Model). It restates what those sources say and does not add claims of its own. Every part links to its sources at the bottom so you can check the original wording.

In one breath

Part 14 gave the shape of ΨORM. This part walks it in order. The document, version 13.5 of August 2026, is MM writing under his own name, Robert Vannrox, for "future generations", with a DOI, a licence, a changelog and a roadmap. It opens by inverting the usual question ("not how does matter produce consciousness, but how does consciousness navigate matter"), states four claims, grounds them in four accepted problems in physics, sketches a two-qubit model, and lists five things an EEG could prove wrong. Then it rebuilds the rest of the site in the new vocabulary: attractor basins for shared reality, a sine-wave duty cycle for the flicker of consciousness, "quantum shadows" for other people, garbons and swales for the soul, and, in the longest section, the whole intention course as "intentional frequency shift". The last six sections fold in Michael Newton's map of the afterlife, ending with death in eight stages. "It is a seed, not a cathedral."

I to VI: foundations

I Introduction
Newtonian and relativistic physics are "incomplete": the Hard Problem stands. "Consciousness is not an observer. It is an active navigator... Reality is the terrain that consciousness navigates through frequency synchronization." The Many-Worlds Interpretation is "a door left ajar": it never says "what determines the path". And the thesis that reality "is not shared".
II Ancestry
Positions ΨORM against Hoffman's conscious realism (agrees that spacetime is "a user interface", adds a mechanism), the James and Huxley filter theory (agrees the brain is "a reducing valve", defines the filter "as phase-locking" and says where memory lives: "the soul"), panpsychism, and MWI itself.
III The four claims
Time is not fundamental ("The film reel is fixed. The projector is the body. The light is you"); consciousness is the navigator, "a quantum waveform" driven by "thought-garbons" and "memory-garbons"; reality is frequency synchronisation; reality is not shared. Also the "Ψ" in the name: psi as the wavefunction and as the psychic.
IV Physics
Four "mainstream, defensible" problems the model claims to address: the Hard Problem, the measurement problem ("the observer is not a physical apparatus, it is the consciousness vector selecting"), the incompatibility of relativity and quantum mechanics, and non-locality.
V Mathematics
"A scaffold", "preliminary but not superficial": see the figure.
VI Falsifiability
Operational definitions, two boundary conditions ("the orthogonality limit": beyond a frequency gap no bleed-over is possible; "the conservation of coherence": high-fidelity transfer "is rare and resource-intensive"), and the five predictions of part 14, each with a measurement and a stated way to fail.

VII to XIII: the mechanics

VIII Ontology
A hierarchy from quanta to garbons ("small, organized clusters of quanta") to swales to souls to consciousness vectors; "sentience as the purpose of experience"; "the purpose of existence: soul growth". Souls "possess the capacity to divide their energy. This is not separation, it is extension." Animal souls are "different", not lesser. Sub-sections argue "why garbons are necessary" and "why swales are necessary" (part 20).
IX Death
"The brain is a router, it accesses the soul but does not store it. The body is a vehicle... The soul is the driver." Death "as decoupling", with Newton's clients quoted for the floating and the "pulling sensation"; "frequency resonance" draws the vector to the non-physical state.
X The duty cycle
Consciousness "breathes, in and out of the physical, like a wave. And the breath never stops." Roughly half of experience "is physical, and half is in-between". The 4 Hz benchmark is "the average rate of an alert, waking brain"; the rate varies with state, and the EEG bands are its correlates. Sleep is a long in-between phase.
XI Heaven, nirvana
Afterlife accounts "are reports of the non-physical state", "a real region of the configuration space", social, layered, and species-specific: "Cats do not go to a human heaven. They go to a cat heaven. But love bridges the gap." Includes "pair consciousness and shared world-lines" (part 20's pets that follow you).
XII Practice, first pass
"Frequency synchronization is not a passive state, it is a practice." Daily habits, environment ("Feng Shui is not superstition, it is frequency engineering"), intention ("The universe responds to the frequency you broadcast, not the words you speak"), and MM's old warning about asking for "opportunities": "Vague intentions produce vague results."
XIII Future work
"The ΨORM framework is a seed, not a finished cathedral."

XIV to XXI: genesis, anomalies, the filter, groups

XIV Genesis
"How does it all begin?" A four-step answer: the void as "pure potentiality"; quanta forming patterns "through entropic resonance" into the first garbons, "the first crystals of consciousness"; a threshold of complexity, coherence and self-reference at which "the system becomes aware of itself. The soul is born"; and a first act that "is not a choice, it is a reflex": attraction to a matching frozen moment. Signposts are "unchangeable waypoints".
XV Comparison
A table of "Great Mysteries" against six other models, with the claim that "ΨORM is not just another model, it is the only model that explains all the phenomena listed". The archive notes the claim without endorsing it.
XVI, XVII Travelers and anomalies
Mandela effects, objects that vanish, shared impossible events, premonitions, communication with the dead, and the "died then restarted" case: "The consciousness vector decouples from the biological receiver during the catastrophic event... re-anchors to a nearby frozen moment... The memory is not false, it is from a different version of reality." PissedLizard's crash (part 34) in formal dress.
XVIII, XIX The filter
"The filters are not walls, they are lenses." Loosened by sleep transitions, meditation, trauma, NDEs and "pharmacologically": psychedelics "reliably reduce filtering across all five domains", with the neuroscience of ego-dissolution cited, and a caution: "Removing it does not produce a more accurate rendering, it produces a different rendering... Insight and delusion can coexist." XIX is the "applied phenomenology": music in noise, moving patterns, "You are not losing your mind. You are losing your filters."
XX, XXI Groups and overlap
"A group is not just a collection of individuals, it is a frequency field." When a group vector shifts, "the rendered reality of the entire cluster reconfigures"; the hundredth-monkey story is used as a case study of "critical coherence". XXI: physical reality "emerges from the overlap of countless attractor basins"; "Other beings are not in your reality."

XXII to XXVII: the practice, re-derived

Section XXII is the longest in the document, 6,500 words, and it is the intention course of part 19 rewritten as mechanism: the pre-birth "preparation class" and its "recognition signs" (from Newton); present tense because "the consciousness vector navigates from the present moment"; "aligning with deeper needs" ("A desire for a beach house may mask a need for peace"); "the mandatory pause" ("you wind the toy, put it down and let go"); "ghost campaigns" as "frequency residue", with the chewing-gum and tyre metaphors; the brute-force tools (crowbar, grinder, detangler, hasp and lock, zipper, spellchecker); synchronicities "as feedback loops"; the grand strategy and sign posts; conflicting affirmations and "the tablecloth metaphor"; the inertia of past affirmations and a "template refresh" technique; a "manifestation timing table"; "but affirmations" as protective clauses that block; and "the master template and affirmation campaigns", the 2020s claim that the baseline template itself has changed. Each sub-section ends with a falsifiability note.

XXIIIJoining a group vector

"When you join a group, you are not just signing a contract, you are entering a frequency basin." Alignment "is a cascade": personal, then immediate contacts, then structure, then identity. The slipstream: "a shared pool of frequency targets that amplifies individual alignment."

XXIVThe techniques, catalogued

Self-hypnosis and affirmations "retune the biological receiver to a new default frequency"; audio (Hemi-Sync) is "frequency entrainment"; "A talisman is not magical, it is mechanical"; "Ritual is not religion, it is frequency engineering"; "A wish machine is not a gimmick, it is a frequency generator"; the power boost, the smile exercise, ESP "as a learned skill".

XXVRemote viewing

"Not magic, it is frequency navigation. The cross-talk is not a flaw, it is a feature of the configuration space." The BlueNarwhal exchange of part 19, formalised: viewers can be trained "to narrow or widen their RV aperture".

XXVITopography

The 3D world-line maps of parts 2 and 4: "The path you follow without intention is the path of least resistance, the fated life." Stable coordinates: "A line can contain an infinite number of points. But an intersection contains only one point, and that point is fixed", hence cars appearing at crossroads (part 18). Plus the pre-birth template, slides, "novel attractor basins", and a "right hand convention" for reading the map.

XXVIIA periodic table of consciousness

The discrete heavens and levels of Hindu, Buddhist and Taoist maps "are not mystical constructs. They are regions of the configuration space", organisable "by frequency signature and coherence level". "The maps of the mystics are not metaphors, they are topographic maps."

XXVIII to XXXIII: the Newton chapters

The last six sections integrate Journey of Souls and Destiny of Souls (part 20), quoting clients throughout and adding a "ΨORM mechanism" line to each stage: the gateway and the pulling ("the natural resonance of the soul returning to its home frequency"), welcoming committees, the "shower of healing", orientation, soul groups, the library of life books, the Council of Elders ("The Council does not judge, it guides. It does not punish, it plans"), the medallions and "the Presence", the "Law of Silence", the Ring of Destiny and its "Timemasters", why souls choose difficult lives ("How can I teach others unless I have gone through fire myself?"), soul specialisations (ethicists, harmonizers, guides, "creating particles of energy for rock shapes on a full planetary scale"), and the colours of souls from white through yellow to blue and beyond, six levels.

The bridges, the glossary, the predictions

Read with care

ΨORM is a self-published framework by one author, and its scientific apparatus (DOI, falsifiability statements, a Hamiltonian) is the author's own and not peer-reviewed; the archive summarises it as a document, not as science. Its "client-verified" chapters rest on Newton's hypnosis cases, a third party's data. It reuses all of MM's earlier vocabulary and most of his 2019 to 2022 material, but it drops the prison-planet layer entirely; readers comparing part 35 with parts 21 to 29 should treat the two as different presentations of the same author's thinking, three years apart, rather than as one system. Quotations are from the repository's own text; section numbers follow its master index.

Remember

  • Thirty-three sections, five groups: foundations, mechanics, extensions, practice, Newton.
  • Four claims; four accepted problems in physics; a two-qubit toy; five EEG predictions; two boundary conditions.
  • Shared reality is a shared valley; the flicker is a 4 Hz sine wave; the filter is a lens.
  • Section XXII is the intention course in formal dress, tools and all.
  • Death in eight stages, from decoupling to re-projection; no prison in this version.
  • Ten bridges, fifty terms, one legacy: "a seed, not a cathedral".
