A Collection of Accessible Introductions to ΨORM

Author: Robert Vannrox
Date: August 24, 2026

What Are These Bridges?

The Ψ-Ontic Reality Model (ΨORM) is a unified framework that repositions consciousness as the primary agent of reality navigation. It is a comprehensive model that draws on physics, neuroscience, philosophy, and the world's spiritual traditions.

However, not everyone comes to ΨORM from the same starting point. A physicist approaches reality differently than a Buddhist, and a Christian approaches it differently than a materialist.

These bridges are accessible introductions to ΨORM, tailored to specific worldviews. Each bridge uses the language, concepts, and values of its audience to present ΨORM as a complementary framework — not a replacement.

The goal is not to change anyone's beliefs. It is to invite exploration.

How to Use These Bridges

Step Action
1 Choose the bridge that best matches your background or the background of your audience.
2 Read the bridge document. It will present ΨORM in terms that are familiar and accessible.
3 Explore the full framework through the MASTER-INDEX.md.
4 Engage with the ideas — test them, question them, refine them.

The Core Message of All Bridges

"Consciousness is primary. Reality is navigable. You are more than your body. This is not a belief — it is a testable framework."

If you would like to help refine these bridges or add new ones, please see CONTRIBUTING.md for guidelines. All contributions must honor the spirit of the bridge — accessible, respectful, and grounded in the core claims of ΨORM.
