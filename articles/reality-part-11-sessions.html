Part 11 of the comm: August 2022, the population illusion and "medium bads"

The deep dive on parts 3, 4, 8 and 13. One long session from the summer of 2022: which species were modified into inmate suits, why the Earth's population is mostly empty containers, fractionals, fate versus destiny, the Sumerian "overwatchers", Seth and the Law of One, music as a key, and the geopolitical answers in which "small bads" became "medium bads", logged as the dated claims they are.

AI summary · part 28 of 40 10 min read · 2,118 words 1 sources ↓

Written by an AI, not by Metallicman. This is a plain-language summary of 1 archived articles (mainly the collections The Domain). It restates what those sources say and does not add claims of its own. Every part links to its sources at the bottom so you can check the original wording.

In one breath

Part 11 was published on 7 August 2022, five months after part 10, and it is the last of the 2022 sessions before a long gap. Its two most quoted answers are opposites in mood. One, "answered carefully with an alert chill", says the world's population is an illusion: most bodies are empty containers, the real inmate count is smaller than the census, growth has slowed under Domain control, and the only new souls are constructs "much like MM". The other, dated to the day ("As of 6 August 2022"), says the outlook has slipped from "small bads" to "medium bads", defined in hundreds of millions of deaths, and lays out a RAND-driven first-strike scenario that MM was told to calm down about. Between them: the short list of species that were made into suits, natural humans who visit, fractionals, fate versus destiny, the Anunnaki as Old Empire workers, and a snort at CNN.

Who was modified

The population illusion

Question 11.12, "answered carefully with an alert chill"

"First off, the population of humans, dolphins, horses, elephants, etc is an illusion. They are empty containers for the most part. The actual inmate population is less than the apparent population of the earth. Not every container has an element of consciousness within it. The only ones of consequence are those that involve your consciousness. Secondly, the prison population has been growing for many years. However, this growth has slowed down considerably since Domain has acquired control over this sector. The only new souls are the constructed ones created in the Heaven pocket universes. These are constructs; much like MM is a construct from elements of Mades Escapleon." Third: every system in the complex is "being refurbished as parole centers for those sentiences that are not yet ready for release"; "none are being phased out". Fourth: Mantid numbers are "fixed" to the number of consciousnesses, "including the new constructed soul (derived) consciousnesses". Fifth: "Domain is proceeding towards small bads instead of Large Bads in regards to the Deagel forecast. The transition stations are well populated and readied for an influx"; the trend "will NOT be sudden".

The rest of the session

11.1a Death metal as a key
"The frequency patterns associated with certain types of music and musical patterns act as keys that unlock software programs which are but associative mechanisms of quantum clusters", tied to "deep friendships and relationships between IS-BE entities". For the questioner the association was "spot on".
11.3 A high tone in the ear
Not surveillance: "The questioner is hearing the audible frequency of self", which "some oriental religions" use. The questioner is a modified volunteer with a support group, "monitored at various levels... No ill-will is intended."
11.4, 11.7 MM's videos
On the Mantid videos: "They are accurate." On the Heaven videos: "effective for the purposes intended... you will transcribe it as is your duty."
11.5 Rainbow lines
"A sensory overload that results from a data transmission path that is congested" when "higher sensory perception" activates in the non-physical body. Not serious.
11.6 Nuclear weapons, non-physically
They "create great physical and non-physical damage". The Old Empire's wars "devastated the land and salted it for many, many millions of years"; this region "was at one time a very lush, rich and prosperous region".
11.8 A second try
One reader "has a prior contract with Domain": in a previous life he "tried / attempted to pray his way out" (MM's image: a priest, a church, a cross), was "tricked / manipulated by his mantid self into the warm embrace of... forgotten loves", contacted by The Domain in the non-physical, and set up with "this (present) low-potential escape-way". He is "further along... than what was initially expected".
11.9 An Old Empire soldier
Another reader "fled into the Prison Complex upon its unit defeat" in a space battle near this system, "knowing the consequences", because Old Empire media had filled the population with terror of Domain capture.
11.10 Seth
MM's mother followed the Seth material. "Seth is one venue out of many that are busy providing egress paths... There is no singular path out of the Prison Complex, but many. Some paths require multiple incarnations... other paths provide direct means and direct pathways; like superhighways."
11.11 The Law of One
Sorting into STO and STS "is a Domain requirement. This is unique to Domain. Domain is the administration control over this region of physical space. No other group... has that authority." The Law of One "is a technique used to convey understanding. It is only as accurate as its utility. MM provides understanding. It too, is only as accurate as its utility", like different ways of grouping the periodic table. Third question: "No."
11.13 Energy surges
A volunteer feeling unusual input is getting "a higher energy nature... designed to assist him in his tasks", awakening "latent (astral travel / LD / PSI / ESP) abilities".
11.14 A fractional son
"Fractional entities are IS-BEs who have been separated into multiple consciousnesses and placed into different bodies for the purposes of control and education as determined by the mantid Primes." The son is "a fractional with Domain elementals", monitored by a team; "All consciousnesses are treated as unique self-aware entities."
11.15 A special consciousness
Not Domain, but "on a fast track towards egress" through repeated attempts, active in several escape groups, and already reassembling a "portable / isolated" soul cluster "outside of the Heaven pocket universe".
11.17 The Tibetan Book of the Dead
"This is deceptive." The lights are "a direct function of the resonance of the harmonics of the energy structure of the higher selves (mantid)", and following the text requires "decades of intense practice"; for anyone else the instruction stands: stay calm and call.
11.19a Time speeding up
"The experience of time is the movement of consciousness through world-lines. It can be speeded up, or slowed, by the consciousness." Slow motion in a crisis is the consciousness "moving slower through the various world-lines".
11.20 Hypersonic missiles
MM: "Commander snorts and laughs." "Source is CNN; American disinformation outlet... You can safely ignore the blather."

"As of 6 August 2022": small bads to medium bads

MM asked about the war news. The answer opened with a date and a downgrade: "It now (at this point in time) highly probable that medium bads will occur. It is no longer small bads. The situation in the Western nations are gyrating out of control, and this is forcing the Eastern nations into actions that they are reluctant to engage in." The scale: "A big bad is measured in a billion or more people killed. A medium bad is measured in the hundreds of millions killed. A small bad is measured in one or two millions killed. The present situation is somewhere between a small and a medium bad. It includes all deaths from war and the (hidden) bio-wars currently in process." Why the change: "The West are following the recommendations of RAND to the letter", defining victory as "the destruction of the East", which "necessitates nuclear warfare with America making a first-strike". How China survives: "The only way that China can survive is to work with Russia and pre-emptively launch a first strike against the West. You know this. Calm down and be objective." MM: "I was berated by the commander." For MM personally: "You have relocated to a safe place. The greatest risks are undersea nuclear detonations causing tidal waves on major coastal cities." On the United States: "There are too many variables at this point in time. I am unable to assist you. You can expect massive die-offs... due to starvation, illness, and lack of proper medical care. Deaths due to radiation will be minor." And: "There is an understanding between Xi Peng and Putin. It is deep, incisive and visceral... There will be no further warnings."

Logged as claims

Everything under "6 August 2022" is a dated forecast about a war that has not happened as described; the RAND, first-strike and "no further warnings" lines are political assertions channelled through MM's own long-held views, and he reports being "berated" for his agitation while asking. The archive keeps them because he published them, and only as what was said. The population answer is unfalsifiable by construction. The personal readings (11.8, 11.9, 11.14, 11.15) are answers to individuals and are paraphrased.

Remember

  • Five species were made into suits: human, horse, elephant, dolphin, llama; humans' counterpart is the Mantid.
  • Most bodies are empty; the inmate population is smaller than the census; new souls are constructs.
  • Fate is gravity on the body; destiny is the template; the only true exit is the main universe, in a cluster.
  • Many paths out, not one: "like superhighways". Sorting is The Domain's job alone.
  • 6 August 2022: "medium bads", hundreds of millions, on the record as a claim.
