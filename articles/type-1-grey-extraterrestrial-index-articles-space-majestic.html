Type 1 Grey Extraterrestrials

Metallicman 16 July 2021 5 min read · 1,003 words Original ↗

This is a sub-index of my Extraterrestrial Index. Which is, in turn, a sub-index of my MAJestic Index.

Type – 1 Greys

/wp:post-content wp:paragraph

This is the most common extraterrestrial race on the planet. And it was the first species that I was introduced to while I was in MAJestic. Thus the nomenclature of a Type-1. Meaning, the first type that I was exposed to. Makes sense right? Or would you be more comfortable with the MAJestic nomenclature?

/wp:paragraph wp:paragraph

I have things to say about this species, and things that I am limited to discuss. This is my attempt to add some clarity on this issue, in the midst of all the massive disinformation out there off in internet land.

[1] Summary of the Type-1 Grey species

This summary was written based on my MAJestic exposure. The little that I learned through entanglement and experience, was placed here. When I read the "Alien Interview" document, it caused me to revise certain statements. As some were incorrect. Updating of this article is pending.
/wp:paragraph wp:paragraph

And where they are from.  Well, they are not really from any particular planet. Not at all.  Nope they are not from Zeta Reticuli.  But where do they resides? Well, they live on many planets all over the place. and many are from some place nearer…

[2] Let’s chat about brown dwarf solar systems

Up until a few years ago, no one was really sure that brown dwarf stars existed, and to this day there are arguments that say that nothing can live on the planet that orbit them. The arguments are interesting, but they are all false. I hope that this article helps shed some light on the situation.
/wp:paragraph wp:paragraph

Now you might ask, just how near are these stars to the solar system? Well, in galactic terms they are very near. In fact, they might be nearer than your wildest dreams…

[3] Is our solar system a binary system?

There seems to be evidence that it is. And if it is, then the reason that we do not recognize it as such is because our stellar partner must be a very small and cold star; a brown dwarf.
/wp:paragraph wp:paragraph

How long has this species been involved in the earth? Well, for a long, long time. Scouting expeditions to our area happened a long, long time ago, and settlements also followed. Let’s look at some evidence.

[4] The Hammer inside the Rock – The London Hammer.

Here is a very interesting OOPART based upon the metallurgy of the material used. We take the time to break this item down into it's components and come up with an understanding of the tool, it's purpose and it's use.
/wp:paragraph wp:paragraph

And, if they are so prevalent all over the earth, then where is their bases of operation? The sightings are global, and constant and consistent. But Google Earth doesn’t indicate any extraterrestrial bases or housing structures.

/wp:paragraph wp:paragraph

Where are they…

[5a] The Hollow Moon

All evidence points to the moon being hollow. But no one has ever parsed out how it could be hollow. But we do, and the story is an interesting one. We also look at what it must be like to live within this hollow body that we can see up in the sky most every night.
/wp:paragraph wp:paragraph

[5b] Someone Else is on The Moon by George Leonard (Full text in PDF)

And maybe a little bit about how they started interacting with humans.

/wp:paragraph wp:paragraph

The way we can do this is by looking at old Sumerian historical records, and putting pieces together. I am not an expert on ancient languages, but others are. Get ready for a “deep dive”…

[6] Genesis Revisited (full text) by Zecharia Sitchin in free HTML 

The title, Genesis Revisited, is a reference to the Book of Genesis - the first book in the Holy Bible which purports to explain the beginning of life on Earth as we know it. You don't have to be a scholar to understand that the Bible is a fictitious volume of made up, doctored fairy tales to support a corrupt hierarchy.
/wp:paragraph wp:heading
