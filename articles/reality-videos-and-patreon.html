The video series and the Patreon posts, mapped

The deep dive on part 17's "where things are". MM's YouTube "Quantum construction" series as the curriculum he laid out (115 videos in 31 groups, 28 marked as must-watch), the 285 Patreon posts from April 2022 to September 2026 by period and theme, the 2024 affirmation classes, the Commander comm numbering across both platforms, and which written part of these summaries covers each video group.

It restates what those sources say and does not add claims of its own. Every part links to its sources at the bottom so you can check the original wording.

In one breath

Not everything MM made is an article. From 2022 he moved much of his teaching to video: a YouTube channel with a "Quantum construction" series that he asks people to watch in order, and a Patreon where most new material has appeared since. This archive holds the map of both, not the recordings: every video title and link in MM's own order and grouping, and every Patreon post's title, date, type and tags (285 posts, of which 31 are public). This part reads the map. It shows the curriculum MM built (basics, affirmations, the 28 "@" fundamentals on Mantids and death, the Commander's corrections, the DIY portal videos, the Tunnel of Light thread), the rhythm of the Patreon (a launch burst in June 2022, the affirmation "classes" of spring 2024, then updates and Commander comms through 2026), and, for each group, which written part of these summaries covers the same ground, so a reader without the videos still has the content.

Two channels, two audiences

The collection page says how MM wants it used: "I have set up a You-tube channel, and I have posted a ton load of videos on it. Many regarding mantids." The videos are listed "In the order they have been presented. Very important to watch them one after the other. I cover some very advanced subjects and try to make them very simple." And the marker: "Those marked with a '@' are considered to be important and fundamental videos. You start at the top here and watch every video with a '@' in order." The Patreon is the second channel, "and have some very important videos on it." The archive indexes both; it does not contain the member-only bodies.

The curriculum, in MM's order

The YouTube page is a syllabus. It starts with six numbered "Basics" videos, moves to affirmations and world-line travel, then to the block MM flags as "MUST WATCH videos that set up the terms, and conventions used throughout the MM dialog series", then to Mantids, death, the Commander's corrections, and outward from there to the "Alice in Wonderland" material, the DIY builds, and the Tunnel of Light. The ladder below keeps his order and his group names, with the number of videos in each and how many carry the "@".

The Tunnel of Light thread

This is the one place the article side and the video side meet on a single day. On 31 August 2022 MM posted a 290-word article, "Remote Viewing of the Tunnel of Light", pointing to a remote-viewing video by others about the tunnel "that one observes upon death". His own reaction is unusually unsettled: "Very interesting. I am trying to process this video. I really don't know what to think, but I will query the Domain Commander for some answers later on." Daegonmagus, quoted in the article: "This is very, very similar to my experiences. This is THE best video i have watched so far on the traps. This is legit." MM then announces the method that produced the next month of material: "I am generating a series of videos where I chat with the Domain Commander about this remote viewing exercise." The Patreon shows the result: "The Tunnel of Light" and "Remote Viewing the Tunnel of Light (Part 1)" in August, parts 2 to 7 in September 2022, and the YouTube page carries the same nine videos plus DM's two commentaries. The written follow-through is the loosh and farming material of parts 8 and 20.

How to use the map

  1. Read first, watch second. Every video group above has a written part in these summaries; the articles the videos were made from are in the archive in full. Use the group cards to jump.
  2. Follow the "@". If you do watch, take MM's own rule: the 28 marked videos, top to bottom, before anything else.
  3. Treat Patreon titles as a table of contents, not content. The archive has title, date, type, tags and link. What was said in a members-only video is not here and this part does not guess at it.
  4. Date everything. The Patreon runs to September 2026 and includes forecasts and geopolitics. As in parts 24 to 29, a dated claim is a dated claim.
  5. For the method, use part 19. The 2024 classes and the 2026 "How to Build an Affirmation Campaign" post are the same material the articles teach.

What this part cannot do

It cannot tell you what is in a video. The YouTube titles are MM's and their order is his; the Patreon entries are metadata. Where a title matches an article (most of the June 2022 batch, the Basics series, the DIY videos, the affirmation classes), the written part is the place to read the content. Where it does not (the 2025 and 2026 updates, the dated Commander comms after part 15), this archive has only the title and the date.
