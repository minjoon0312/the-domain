Reality, explained

What this material says reality is, in plain words and pictures: 17 short illustrated parts written by an AI from the archived articles, in reading order, each linked to its sources.

17 articles · 24,126 words
These 17 parts were written by an AI (Claude) as plain-language, illustrated summaries of the archived articles. They are not by Metallicman. Each part restates what its sources say, labels who said it, and links to the original articles so every statement can be checked.

Read in order

Start at part 1. Each part explains one piece of the picture in plain words, with drawings, and ends with the sources it was written from. Together they cover everything in this archive.

What reality is, according to this material

The big picture in one page: you are an immortal consciousness inside an engineered body, inside a pocket universe built as a prison, moving through frozen world-lines that you experience as time.

Time, world-lines and the MWI

Time does not flow. Reality is a huge set of frozen moments, and your consciousness steps across them about four times a second. That stepping is what you call time, and a "slide" is a step sideways.

5 min · 17 sources

You: soul, consciousness and body

A soul is a cloud of organised "quanta". A consciousness is the slice of it plugged into a body. The human body here is an engineered "inmate suit" that lets only a fifth to a third of you in, and makes you forget the rest.

8 min · 17 sources

Your life has a template

Before you were born, your life path was mapped out and agreed to. Most people ride that "pre-birth world-line template" from start to finish and call it fate. You can leave it, but the template pulls you back unless you keep steering.

6 min · 16 sources

How thoughts steer: intention and affirmation campaigns

The practical core of the whole blog. Thoughts pull you toward matching world-lines, but in this bubble thought alone is throttled, so you write your goals down and say them out loud, daily, for months. Here is the method, the rules and the traps.

7 min · 28 sources
 6

What happens when you die

You leave the body with all your memories intact. Then a well-rehearsed welcome (loved ones, a light, a tunnel) leads to a memory wipe and another life. The material says there are four doors at that moment, and only one of them is the default.

6 min · 19 sources
 7

Heaven, the Mantids and the lessons

"Heaven" here is not the sky. It is a small pocket universe attached to the prison, one per species, where you rest, get reviewed and get scheduled for the next life. The Mantids run it, and the curriculum, the material says, has gone badly wrong.

6 min · 19 sources

The prison planet and the Old Empire

A fallen galactic power took this region by force, built a bubble around six solar systems, and filled it with beings it wanted to forget. The bubble's rules (amnesia, a fence, throttled thought) are the prison. Here is its history, its builders and its current status.

8 min · 20 sources

The Domain and the Commander

A vast, disciplined organisation from the main universe that says it conquered the Old Empire, lost 3,000 of its own inside the prison, and since September 2021 has been answering questions through an implant in Metallicman's skull. Who they are, how the comm works, what they want, and their rules.

The US program that handled extraterrestrial contact, as told by one of its implanted agents.
