This is a listing of all the Daegonmagus articles. Daegonmagus became a contributor to MM on 29 July 2021.

Biography

Daegon Magus is a writer, musician, music producer, product developer and electronics technician who grew up in the semi rural town of Wooroloo, Western Australia. He was the speculative fiction facilitator of The Katherine Susannah Prichard Writing Centre in his home state in 2014, and was the founder of the Western Australian Chapter of the international occult alchemical society, The Hell Fire Club, in which he still holds a Stewardship role along with his wife Storme Diano.

Daegon has contributed lyrical works to the Hell Fire Club and has had several articles published on the spiritualist site in 5D.com.

In 2012, Daegon was contacted by a group of Ascended Masters and given information about a spiritual amnesia that was affecting mankind, as well as given access to a higher state of consciousness that he was tasked with using by these Masters to help fix the amnesia.

Daegon was told by these Masters themselves that it was because of techniques he had mastered after over a decades worth of experiments in consciousness projection via lucid dreaming and an understanding of the Higher Self he’d picked up through his occult studies that they were able to make contact with him by the tender age of 22.

These Ascended Masters would go on to make contact with Daegon several more times on and off over a 7 year period in which they taught him about how consciousness had been affected by the amnesia and gave him access to memories of his own reincarnation as well as other past lives.

In 2016 Daegon was initiated into a nameless organization that existed solely in the astral planes, who were tasked with monitoring the inter-dimensional traffic coming into and out of Earth; he was told by this organizations leader he had passed his initiation after coming to the true understanding of the alien agenda unfolding on earth, after deep meditation on what had been revealed to him by the Ascended Masters.

In 2020, after being put onto the Alien Interview and noticing some unsettling similarities with its content and his experiences, Daegon, founded the Ordo Occultum Astrum – Order of The Hidden Star – specifically to deal with the information given to him by the Ascended Masters and the leader of the unnamed organisation, as well as to reunite the 20 000 other consciousness that were present at the original meeting with those same Ascended Masters.

The goal of the Order is to break out of the reincarnation cycle using Daegon’s philosophies on the soul and techniques he developed in lucid dreaming.
