Biography

Normal typical high-school kid in the suburbs.

Joined the USCG in the mid 80s and became an electronics tech. Fixing radar and radios. This was the only thing I wanted to do.

The rest happened - it was pure internal drive. Now I know the barriers that were placed to “funnel” me here.

I HAD to first join the reserves and become an aircraft mechanic, which I did while I went to college - again it was a drive get a degree degree in bio and focus genetics.

Then I had to become a physician.

All that I knew was that I needed a medical degree, not a DO or veterinarian. I had to become a psychiatrist, which I did in Florida.

I was then told I had to study Islam and learn Arabic and Farsi, which I did on my own.

After several twists and turns I was told that this was my “retirement” and I could do as I wished, but I was not allowed to maintain a medical license so I became a teacher.

That ended this past spring. Which coincided with MM finding me...
